Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* Mutant clips from Mixamo, on the Mutant rig, each file named for its clip:
  idle (Mutant Breathing Idle), walk (Mutant Walking), run (Mutant Run),
  punch (Mutant Punch), swipe (Mutant Swiping), smash (Mutant Jump Attack),
  roar (Mutant Roaring), hit_reaction (Standing React Large From Front),
  death (Mutant Dying), falling (Falling Idle). The source FBXs stay outside
  the repo. Copy each into one folder under its clip name (idle.fbx, ...),
  beside the rig as Mutant.fbx, and build with Blender 5.2.3:
    blender --background --python tools/mixamo_to_gltf.py -- \
      --input-dir <folder> --library --base Mutant.fbx \
      --in-place smash hit_reaction \
      --trim smash_windup=smash:434 --trim swipe_windup=swipe:900 \
      --trim punch_windup=punch:167 \
      --trim smash_strike=smash:434- --trim swipe_strike=swipe:900- \
      --trim punch_strike=punch:167- \
      --trim jab_quick=punch:167-967 \
      --event punch:hit=267 --event smash:hit=1633 --event swipe:hit=1300 \
      --output content/dev/models/clip_libraries/mixamo_brute/library.gltf
  The export is deterministic: rerun, it reproduces library.gltf and
  library.bin byte for byte (checked 2026-11-15, with and without
  --factory-startup; rechecked 2026-11-16 after adding jab_quick, which
  left every earlier clip's keys and buffer bytes unchanged). Those checks
  used the pre-E21 converter with the half-turn yaw flag it then needed for
  +Z facing. Today's default produces the same +Z facing without the flag,
  so the recipe above should still reproduce the library (expected, not
  re-verified).
* The converter's default output faces +Z, the front AI facing aims, matching
  the bound Mutant model; root motion then runs the way the model faces.
* smash and hit_reaction are pinned in place: the jump attack lands 1.6 m
  forward and the hit reaction staggers 1 m back, and the
  body would snap back when either clip ended.
* smash_windup, swipe_windup and punch_windup are the attacks cut at their
  windup peak. Played without looping they hold that pose, for enemy
  stances. Each cut is measured from the library's own joint positions,
  hips-relative, at 30 fps keys:
  - smash_windup at 434 ms, ending on the key at 433 ms (frame 13): the
    crouch's bottom, where the hips sit lowest (0.535 m, from 0.805 m
    standing) before the leap.
  - swipe_windup at 900 ms (frame 27): the lean back before the lunge.
    The hips and the striking left claw are furthest back at 867-900 ms,
    within 1 cm of each other.
  - punch_windup at 167 ms, ending on the key at 167 ms (frame 5): the
    fist drawn back. The right hand is furthest back at 133-167 ms (within
    2 mm) and slowest at 167 ms (1.2 m/s), where it turns before the swing.
* smash_strike, swipe_strike and punch_strike are the rest of each attack
  from its windup cut, starting on the windup's last pose: a stance's release
  plays one after its windup holds.
* jab_quick is one swing of the punch, 167-967 ms (frames 5-29, 800 ms),
  for a combo that loops it after punch_windup holds. It starts on the
  windup's last pose and ends on the key after the swing where the arm comes
  back nearest that draw-back, measured as the cuts above: the right hand,
  0.31 m from its start position (0.45 m in the hips' own frame), the right
  upper arm 41 degrees and the forearm 42 degrees from their start
  rotations. The swing never returns to the draw-back, so the loop pops by
  that much each pass. Its hit is punch's, at 100 ms.
* Clip events (--event above) are moments measured in source clip time.
  punch hit at 267 ms (frame 8): the right hand's swipe crosses straight in
  front of the hips at its farthest forward point in the clip, near its peak
  speed of 12 m/s. A first measurement took 767 ms, the slower return sweep
  that brings the hand back past the front at a third of that speed; a
  playtest read the hit as late (2026-11-14). smash hit at 1633 ms (frame
  49): the hands, slamming down at 8-12 m/s, pass about 0.6-0.7 m above the
  floor, near a player's camera height; they reach the floor at 1667 ms
  (frame 50) and stop by 1700 ms. A playtest chose the earlier frame so the
  hit meets the camera, not the floor (2026-11-14). swipe hit at 1300 ms
  (the left claw sweeping across, frame 39) is the first frame the claw is
  within about 5% of its full reach.
* Every windup cut falls before its attack's hit, so the windups carry no
  events and each strike carries its attack's hit, shifted by its cut's
  key: smash_strike at 1200 ms, swipe_strike at 400 ms, and punch_strike
  and jab_quick at 100 ms, each to the nearest millisecond.

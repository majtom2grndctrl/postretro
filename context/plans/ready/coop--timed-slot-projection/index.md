# coop--timed-slot-projection

Brief · compact · reads: `context/lib/networking.md` §What gates, and what replicates instead, §Combat authority, §Presentation events vs. replicated state · `context/lib/ui.md` §3 · read at d6f3471

## Problem
Owner playtest under the dev lag proxy after PR #601: a connected client's reload meter fills in steps. Basis: observed defect. Cause: a client's store receives the host's sample about 30 times a second (`SNAPSHOT_TICK_INTERVAL` = 2), and the meter shows each sample raw. It steps on a clean link at any frame rate above 30; loss and jitter only lengthen the holds. The client has no rate to advance the value between samples. When done, a connected client's meters ramp smoothly wherever the host's value ramps, between samples and through lost or reordered snapshots, with no change to the host, single-player or any mod HUD.

## Decisions
- **The projection sets the display target.** For an enumerated slot on a connected client, everything the UI reads from that slot reads its projection: the target a display value retargets to (`ui.md` §3), `visibleWhen` predicates and a Bar's max, and so focus reachability, hit-testing and accessibility state, which read the same values. The rest of §3 holds: a bound tween eases toward the moving target, and an untweened binding shows the projection. The quantity keeps its meaning, so it projects under the existing slot name and no mod HUD changes. The store keeps the host samples, so state crossings and every store reader see them. Reload sounds and the overheat cue read the client's committed weapon projection, which this leaves alone.
- **Scope: the timed slots, each in its timed direction.** Reload progress and cell project only upward, weapon cooldown and heat only downward: the directions the host's fixed tick ramps them (reload timer, regen, cooldown countdown, cooling). A shot moves heat up, cell down and cooldown up, against those directions, so with a cool or regen delay no per-shot step extrapolates at any fire rate. With a zero delay, the SDK default, a shot interval can still net in the timed direction; the error is at most one shot's step. Each projects only to its bound: reload progress 1, cooldown and heat 0, cell the capacity sample naming the same host weapon slot, and with no such sample cell does not project. Cooldown projects although no shipped HUD binds it, so a mod HUD that does gets a smooth meter. Another timed slot found at build is reported in the plan of record, not projected.
- **The rate is derived on the client, from host-stamped samples.** The meter is presentational, so it takes the cheapest shape that looks right, not the authoritative one. Each accepted snapshot whose state batch applies is a sample at its header's server tick: a record samples its value, and no record, or a resend at the held baseline, samples the held value (`ServerStateReplication::produce_for_client` omits only an acked, unchanged slot). A rejected batch, or a record held back to request a refresh, is no sample; state apply reports which, since today a rejected batch and an empty one return the same outcome. Slopes are per server tick and come from the host's values, never local tuning.
- **Extrapolate only on an agreed slope.** The projection advances while the two newest intervals both move in the slot's timed direction and agree on slope within a tolerance that absorbs the timer's whole-millisecond rounding. Otherwise the target is the newest sample, so a reload's exit fade matches the host HUD's. An agreeing sample feeds the projection once the timeline reaches its tick; a sample that breaks agreement, or one at the slot's bound, sets the target on arrival.
- **The projection runs on the samples' own timeline.** It projects to the sample that would be arriving now, not to the host's current value, so the meter stays in step with the raw `reloadActive` flag and the reload sounds. The timeline starts at time sync's first sample, and after a reset it restarts from the current estimate with nothing carried over; until time sync has a sample, the target is the newest raw sample. It never re-anchors on a snapshot's arrival and never rewinds; it slews through time-sync corrections, as interpolation playback does.
- **Bounded, then hold.** A derived slope is an extrapolation, so it follows the bounded-extrapolation-then-hold rule of `done/M15--p2-replication-timesync-lifecycle` and `done/E10--networked-enemy-authority-baseline`, not the uncapped closed forms of E17 movers and `anim_start`, which replicate a schedule. Projection stops once the timeline runs more than a cap past the newest sample, and holds until the next. During a real outage the meter freezes rather than showing heat cooling while the client's own fire goes unheard.
- **Resets follow the store.** History and timeline clear wherever state apply resets (level unload, manifest commit, rejoin) and at demotion. A heat or cell `[slot]` absence clears that slot's target and history, as it clears the store.
- **Display only.** The projection runs every frame, including frames with no snapshot, before the UI snapshot is built. It writes no store slot and no weapon component, and the wire is unchanged.
- **The dev HUD gains a heat bar.** It mirrors the cell bar: shown while `player.weaponResource` is `"heat"`, its max bound to `player.overheatAt`. It is the heat projection's first consumer in content and the manual proof's meter.
- **Docs owed at promotion.** `ui.md` §3: amend the intro sentence and the first bullet (the authoritative slot is the target) and the crossings-versus-display bullet, so UI reads of an enumerated slot on a connected client see its projection. `networking.md` §What gates: the client-derived timed-slot projection. `networking.md` §Combat authority: projected heat and cell are display, with no client heat state, overheat latch or gameplay read. `docs/scripting-reference.md` ships in SDK bundles, so it changes with the build, not at promotion: reload, cooldown, heat and cell meters ramp smoothly on clients with no author change.
- **Non-goals.**
  - The UI clock and frontend tweens: `drafts/ui-tween-clock` owns them. A tween on a projected slot eases on UI time, so the two land in either order.
  - Loss recovery on the reliable channels: `ready/coop--lossy-link-delivery` owns it; this brief smooths what arrives.
  - Client ammo, heat, cell and reload prediction (`networking.md` §Combat authority). The host keeps the reload timer and reserve transfer.

## Acceptance
Pins are in `research.md` §Ordering pins. "The profile" is 45 ms delay, 60 ms jitter and 5% loss, with client frames at 144 Hz, unless a row says otherwise. Times are on the harness's shared virtual clock, and a host event's time is when its tick falls due, not the host frame that runs it (P55). An interval is the span between two consecutive applied samples' ticks, a loss gap included. The arrival allowance is the link's jitter plus one tick. A step for an interval is slope × (1 + the slope tolerance) × (that interval + the arrival allowance). A ramp's span is 1 for reload progress, the overheatAt sample for heat, the capacity sample for cell, and the ramp's first sample for cooldown. The base bound for a frame is slope × (1 + the slope tolerance) × the timeline's top slew rate × frame time, plus 1e-4 of the ramp's span.

### Automated
**Proof setup**
- [ ] Reproduces first. The harness sends at `SNAPSHOT_TICK_INTERVAL`, advances the host at 60 Hz (50 ms per three ticks, never a flat 16 ms) in host frames on their own clock that run one to three ticks each, stamps each header with the host frame's last tick (so stamps run 1 to 4 ticks apart, P58), runs client frames on their own clock, passes snapshots through production's newest-snapshot gate and runs time sync over the conditioned link. Samples come from the host's own timers, never synthetic values. On the build this starts from, a connected client's reload-progress target under the profile changes only on frames that apply a sample, and the test is committed failing.

**Ramps** (each ramp: reload, cooldown countdown, cooling, regen; under the profile, a reseed and folded echoes mid-ramp, stamps 1 to 4 ticks apart, and the timer's 16–17 ms ticks). Each frame falls in exactly one class below, or in a Samples row that names it.
- [ ] Onset: the ramp starts projecting once two full intervals agree (P2, P3, P19). On that frame the target moves only in the timed direction, by at most a step, and never falls back past the raw sample it showed (P52, P53).
- [ ] Between samples: inside an agreeing ramp, short of its bound and the cap, every frame that neither applies nor feeds a sample advances the target in the timed direction by at most the base bound, or holds it while a fed sample sits behind it (P28, P29, P34, P37).
- [ ] On samples: a frame that applies or feeds an agreeing sample, other than the onset frame and the first sample after an outage, changes the target by at most the base bound plus slope tolerance × slope × (that sample's interval + the arrival allowance), and never retreats (P38, P46, P54). A sample at the slot's bound sets the target to it on its frame (P46).
- [ ] Breaking: when a ramp slows or stops without a jump, a lost slope change included, the frame whose sample breaks agreement retargets to it, a retreat of at most a step for the interval ending at that sample (P18, P51). A jump that breaks agreement follows the Jumps rows.
- [ ] The slope is the host samples' slope: with the client's installed reload time and cool rate set to twice the host's, the projected slope stays within the slope tolerance of the host samples' slope (P43).

**Jumps and bounds**
- [ ] Refuse: a jump never extrapolates. On a 60 ms-jitter, lossless link, a shot's heat step, rapid fire at one shot per two ticks, a cooldown reset, a reload wrap, a cool delay ending and a jump in the timed direction each show the host's sample from the frame it applies until two intervals agree again. On each frame before it applies, the target stays within a step of the newest applied sample (P9–P12, P15, P44).
- [ ] Bounds: reload progress never passes 1, and cooldown and heat never pass 0. Cell never passes the capacity sample naming the same host slot; with none, cell does not project, and it projects once that capacity applies (P14, P50). In an overheat lockout, projected heat reaches 0 no earlier than the link's one-way delay less one tick after the host's heat does, and no later than the frame that applies the first sample with the latch down (P13).
- [ ] Refuse: the target falls on a host cancel or slot change, and the reload meter disappears when its active flag falls (P4).
- [ ] In step: on the frame `reloadActive` rises, the target is the Started sample, or the first active sample when the Started snapshot is lost, not a value ahead of it (P1, P56). At the end the target reaches 1 no earlier than the link's one-way delay less one tick after the host completes, and no later than the frame that applies the Completed sample; when the Completed snapshot is lost, no later than one tick of timeline time past the host's completion tick, unless the fall sample applies first (P6). On the flag-fall frame the exit fade captures the progress target that frame shows, the host's fall value when the fall batch carries progress, as the host HUD does (P7, P25).

**Samples**
- [ ] Sample intake, observed at the intake seam: a snapshot with no record for the slot, a held-id resend, a batch of only held-id resends and a snapshot with no state records each yield a sample at the held value at its header tick, including through a cool delay longer than an ack round trip (P22–P24, P39). A left-out delta, a rejected state batch, and a deferred, duplicate or older snapshot yield no sample (P8, P20, P21, P25–P27).
- [ ] Frame cadence: a frame that applies two snapshots takes both as samples, in sequence order (P35). Below 30 Hz client frames, each frame's target is the projection at that frame's timeline time, not the newest raw sample (P36).
- [ ] Outage: with no sample for longer than the cap, the target holds at min(newest + slope × cap, bound) and does not move until the next sample. That sample retargets on its frame: to the projection from it when the gap interval agrees, else to the sample (P33, P49).
- [ ] Before time sync has a sample, the target is the newest raw sample (P30). Its first sample mid-ramp, at join or rejoin, starts the timeline there with nothing carried from before; the target never falls below the raw sample it showed, that frame is exempt from the base bound, and the projection advances within two intervals (P45).
- [ ] Resets: level unload, manifest commit, rejoin and demotion clear history and timeline, and no slope spans them. On a client whose time sync is already running, the timeline restarts from the current estimate and the projection resumes once two intervals agree; samples from a host whose ticks run below the old timeline do too (P31, P32, P48). The first sample naming a new host weapon slot resets that slot's history, a switch away and back included, and a sample still waiting for the timeline is dropped on a slot change or a break of agreement (P16, P17, P57). A heat or cell `[slot]` absence clears the target on its frame, as it clears the store, and the first number after it shows raw (P47).

**Raw readers and isolation**
- [ ] Raw readers stay raw: reload sounds and the overheat cue play on the same frames as with the projection off, a host cancel near full with the projection at 1 included, which presents a cancel and no complete sound (P5). A fire press while the projection shows full and the newest sample is short of it presents as reloading. Cell dry-fire reads the raw charge (P40). The client's cooldown reconcile reads the store's `player.weaponCooldownMs`.
- [ ] State crossings on an enumerated slot fire once per crossing in the applied host samples, never on the projection's advance (P41).
- [ ] UI reads: a binding, a `visibleWhen` predicate and a Bar max on an enumerated slot all read the projection on the same frame. A tween bound to one eases toward the moving projection; with reduce motion on, it shows the projection that frame (P42).
- [ ] Host and single-player are unchanged: each frame, the UI snapshot's value for every enumerated slot equals the store slot. On a connected client, every other slot's UI snapshot value equals its store slot each frame.
- [ ] No client heat or cell state is written: each frame the client's enumerated store slots equal the last applied host sample, and the client weapon components' heat and cell fields are unchanged from install. Grep gate: the projection code writes no store slot and no weapon component.
- [ ] The wire is unchanged: `SNAPSHOT_VERSION`, `WIRE_VERSION`, the application protocol, the state-schema fingerprint and the wire-shape tags keep the values they hold on the build this starts from, and no slot record gains a field.

### Manual
- [ ] Loopback recipe (`networking.md` §Manual loopback recipe) on `coop-presentation-test`, client conditioned at the profile: the reload meter ramps smoothly; with `coop_heat_test`, the heat bar steps per shot while firing, then cools smoothly; with `reference_plasma_bolt` from the default loadout, the cell bar steps per shot and refills smoothly.

## Path
- Seams: `hud.ts` `reloadMeter` and `cellBar`, the model for the heat bar. `PlayerHudStatePublisher::tick_for_role_and_report_sampled_weapon`. `WeaponComponent::reload_feedback_sample`. `ReplicatedWireShape::WieldableSlotNumber`. `ClientStateApply::apply_snapshot_state` and `StateApplyOutcome` in `postretro-netcode`, where intake belongs. The UI snapshot's `slot_values` (`crates/ui/src/output.rs`). `update_client_overlay_anchors`, a per-frame neighbour that already runs before the UI snapshot. Host timers reachable from netcode tests: `advance_authoritative_weapon_resource` (cooling, regen) and `simulate_tick` (reload). `research.md` has the facts behind each.
- Naming: `state_slots/resource_projection.rs` already uses "projection" for something else; pick a distinct module name.
- Sample timeline: the time-sync estimate (`ClockEstimator::estimated_server_tick`) minus half the smoothed RTT, behind a slew and a never-rewind clamp; or each sample's arrival lag smoothed against its recent minimum, as the snapshot-lag law in `networking.md` §Time sync does, provided samples stay within the arrival allowance of it. Report the top slew rate in the plan of record.
- Harness: extend `state_slot_loss_harness_test.rs` to the Proof setup row first, routing snapshots through `ClientReplication` so the newest-snapshot gate applies, with a server-to-client lane for time-sync echoes. The first slice is that harness and the failing reproduction row.
- Rivals considered:
  - A replicated schedule (value plus the time of the next change, as E17 and `anim_start` carry). Exact at a change, where a derived rate shows a sample or two raw, which is invisible on a meter. It would cost a new owner-private slot or a widened correlated sample, a schema fingerprint change, and host work on every timed slot.
  - A single-interval slope behind the same direction gate, bounds and cap. One interval sooner at onset, but a jump in the timed direction or a partial first interval would extrapolate for an interval.
  - Projecting to the host's current value. It leads the reload flag and its sounds by half the RTT, so the meter jumps at a reload start and sits full before vanishing.
  - Advancing at the client's installed tuning rate. It drifts from the host on any tuning difference and breaks `networking.md`'s no-local-fallback rule.
  - A faster owner-private snapshot cadence. It multiplies snapshot bandwidth to every client and still steps at frame rates above the new cadence.
  - Interpolating samples behind the timeline, at any delay. Below interval plus jitter (about 90 ms at the profile) it starves and holds under jitter; at or above it, the meter trails the flag and the sounds and vanishes short of full.

## Open questions
- The hold cap's value. — **delegated**: more than two of the longest stamp gaps plus ordinary link jitter, by at least one tick; report the value and the profile it was checked against in the plan of record.
- The slope-agreement tolerance. — **delegated**: at least the timer's whole-millisecond carry over the shortest interval (1 ms in one tick, about 6.25% at 60 Hz); report it in the plan of record.

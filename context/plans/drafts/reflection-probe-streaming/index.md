# reflection-probe-streaming

Brief · resumable · reads: `context/lib/index.md` §1.1 · `rendering_pipeline.md` §Cluster SH residency, §4 (Lightmap cell-block residency), §7.3a, §7.8, §10 · `boot_sequence.md` §1 (Settling) · `build_pipeline.md` §PRL section IDs · `resource_management.md` §5 · `development_guide.md` §1.4 · `testing_guide.md` §Resource bounds · read at 949a79411 · builds after `reflection-probes` lands · **build when** the next stress map with reflections is authored, or any shipped map drops probes at the cap, whichever comes first

## Problem
Owner-requested capability, anticipated need. A level holds at most one array's portable layer limit of reflection probes, because every probe stays resident from install to unload and the fluid pass indexes both the probe array and the per-probe uniform table by level probe id. The cap is the binding constraint, not the bytes: `stress-env-volumes.map` already drops hundreds of derived probes, and the owner's next stress map reflects. The record can also only grow, by a distance layer, a larger tile or a roughness chain, and today that growth costs VRAM on every laptop for the whole level. When this is done, probes stream from the PRL the way SH clusters and lightmap blocks do: a level may hold any number of probes, a fixed pool holds the ones near and in view, interior maps show no reflection pop because every camera cell's mandatory probes fit the pool, and a reflection that is late fades in instead of popping.

## Decisions
- **Stream, superseding whole-resident** (`resource_management.md` §5.3; `reflection-probes` `research.md` §Budget, which ruled streaming out on bytes alone). The cap, not VRAM, is what binds, and slot-indexed residency is what lifts it (`research.md` §Why stream). The reflection-probes look, capture, light share and author surface are unchanged.
- **Residency unit: one probe's whole record**, every tile section 54 stores for that probe plus its table row. A format that adds layers per probe (a distance tile, say) grows the unit, not the design; slot counts are stated per layer count.
- **Pool: a fixed slot count, one array at the portable layer limit**, first generation the smaller of that and the level's probe count, never grown. Slots are per unit, so a two-layer record halves them. Per-device sizing is a non-goal: the pool registers with the residency budget tiers when `large-map-spatial-residency.md` builds them (`research.md` §Budget).
- **The cap now means per-cell fit.** The compiler guarantees every camera cell's fit set, the probes bound to faces in the cells of its id-51 entries at the section's maximum lead, plus those faces' blend neighbours once `reflection-probe-seams` lands, fits the pool. Where a cell overflows, derived probes drop largest bound surface area kept first, deterministically, with one warning naming the drop count and the worst cell; authored probes past it refuse the build, naming the cell and the slot count. This applies the owner's 2026-09-28 rule (`large-map-spatial-residency.md`): a budget is valid only when every camera cell's mandatory set fits it, and a miss fallback never stands in for residency. A level without a usable id 51 keeps today's level-wide cap and runs all-resident, the lightmap precedent.
- **Cell→probe relation baked in section 54**, a probe belonging to every cell its bound faces' bounds touch. One relation serves the compiler's fit check and the runtime's demand, so they cannot disagree (`index.md` §2, baked over computed).
- **Demand through the shared cell-demand stage**: visible cells, pins and id-51 lead L map to probes through the baked relation, as SH maps cells to clusters; lead within L is mandatory, beyond is optional band and trimmed first. Hysteresis and eviction order follow the SH controller's, and a departed probe evicts after hysteresis even when no other probe wants its slot, so every streamed map, one within the pool included, exercises reads, eviction and fade. Residency is local presentation state: nothing reaches the wire or the digest (`networking.md` §What gates).
- **Slot-indexed GPU state.** The probe array and the share table are indexed by pool slot; each slot's table row (box, capture point, share entries) uploads with its tiles. The vertex stage resolves level probe id to slot and resident flag and passes them flat, the lightmap block-table precedent (`rendering_pipeline.md` §4). Fluid fragment binding counts stay unchanged, and the per-probe map is not bounded by a uniform binding's size.
- **Install ordering.** A fragment never reads a slot holding a different probe than its map entry names: an eviction clears the map entry before the slot is reused, and an install points the entry at the slot only in the submission that uploads its tiles and row, or a later one.
- **Miss policy: no probe term, then fade in.** A face whose probe is not resident renders exactly as a face bound to no probe, and every reader of the probe term (the planned SSR layer included) treats it as unbound. When the probe installs, its reflection weight ramps from 0 to 1 over an engine-owned duration, restarting on every reinstall. Probes resident before reveal draw at full weight on the revealed frame, and all-resident mode never fades. A non-resident blend neighbour folds its weight into the face's own probe; a resident neighbour never draws on a face whose own probe is missing.
- **Reads and drain: a third resource on the shared issuer and drain** (`rendering_pipeline.md` §4: one issuer, one drain-budget owner). Tiles read positionally from the open PRL; in stream mode the CPU keeps the records, bindings and relation, never the tiles. Mandatory before optional, as for SH and lightmaps.
- **Settle.** Reflection probes answer at the settle chokepoint: the presented pose's mandatory probes plus every probe it draws must be resident before reveal; all-resident mode answers not streamed; a timed-out reveal names the unsettled probe count and takes the miss policy (`boot_sequence.md` §1).
- **Modes**, the lightmap precedent: `stream` (default) and `all-resident`, the parity baseline. All-resident on a level with more probes than the pool warns, naming the count, and streams. Frame capture preloads its view's set synchronously, as it does for lightmaps.
- **A section without the cell relation loads all-resident.** Its probe count is within the old level cap by construction, so maps built before keep drawing.
- **Byte meter.** Reflection probes report as their own residency family (pool, table, map, index buffer) in the residency log line, the dev Performance tab and the capture report, closing the capture report's probe-bytes gap (`rendering_pipeline.md` §7.8).
- **Non-goals.** Per-device pool sizing and an Auto tier: `large-map-spatial-residency.md` owns the resource-neutral tier. Pool growth past one array: the per-cell fit makes it unneeded. An SH fill on a miss: it adds the SH read the reflection-probes design rejected, and the fade covers the rare miss. Non-fluid probe readers, prefiltered mips and the distance layer itself: format changes this brief carries, not makes.

## Acceptance
### Automated
Compiler
- [ ] Per-cell fit: a fixture where one camera cell's fit set exceeds the pool drops derived probes smallest bound area first until every cell fits, warning once with the count and the worst cell; ties keep the earlier in fluid entity order, then face order; the result is byte-identical at `--jobs 1` and `--jobs 4`.
- [ ] A level with more probes than the pool, whose every cell's fit set fits, keeps every probe; one cell over the pool with only authored probes in excess refuses the build, naming the cell and the slot count.
- [ ] A level without a usable id 51 applies the level-wide cap exactly as before this brief.
- [ ] The baked relation: a probe whose bound faces touch two cells lists both; a probe's relation changes when a bound face moves across a cell boundary, and the stage's cache key misses.
Format and load
- [ ] Section 54 at the version that adds the relation round-trips it; a relation naming a cell past the cell count, a non-monotone offset table, or a later version fails the load naming the section; an earlier version loads and installs all-resident (S7).
- [ ] In stream mode, decoding the section reads no tile bytes, and no tile bytes stay in CPU memory after install, settle or eviction (allocation or retained-size observation).
Runtime, CPU references pinned to the shader and controller
- [ ] Parity: on a level within the pool, a settled stream-mode frame equals the all-resident frame pixel for pixel at the same pose.
- [ ] A face whose probe is not resident renders exactly as a face bound to no probe.
Pin ids refer to `research.md` §Pin table.
- [ ] Fade: weight 0 on the frame its probe installs in play, monotone to 1 at the engine duration, never above; S3; S4; S6; all-resident mode never fades.
- [ ] Slot reuse (S1): no frame samples B's slot under A's map entry or A's tile under B's; a probe moved to another slot reads the same ratio for the same lights.
- [ ] Two probes installed in one drain both resolve; S2; S5; S8.
- [ ] Demand: visible and lead-within-L probes are mandatory; band probes are trimmed first under pressure and never displace a mandatory one; a probe leaving demand is evicted only after hysteresis, and is evicted then on a level within the pool with free slots; mandatory demand past the pool at runtime refuses the excess as misses and logs once.
- [ ] Shared drain: probe items rank with SH and lightmap items by class; settling uses the settling budget; a mandatory probe read issues before an optional read of any resource.
- [ ] Settle: reveal waits for the presented pose's mandatory and drawn probes; all-resident answers not streamed; a forced timeout reveals with the miss policy and names the unsettled probe count.
- [ ] Level change: no slot, map entry or tile of level A survives into level B; unload frees the pool; S9.
- [ ] Modes: unset resolves to stream; `all-resident` on a level over the pool warns with the count and streams; an unknown value is a load error, as for lightmaps. Frame capture of a streamed level equals its all-resident capture.
- [ ] Byte meter: the reflection-probe family reports pool, table, map and index-buffer bytes, rebuilt on install and unload; the capture report includes them.
- [ ] Budgets: fluid fragment counts unchanged; fluid vertex storage rises by at most one and stays within the limit; the slot table at the pool fits one uniform binding (constant assertion). DX12 FXC compiles the fluid shader (ignored-gated, Windows).

## Path
- **Precedents.** SH: `ShReach` (recompute on camera cell or L change), `ShResidencyController` targets, `pressure.rs` eviction keys. Lightmap: `LightmapStreamingController`, `resolve_lightmap_block` in `lightmap_sample.wgsl`, `preload.rs`. Shared: `CellDemand`, `ReadIssuer`/`ReadRoute` and `LevelReadIssuer::spawn`, `SharedDrain`/`DrainRank`, `StreamResource`, `SettleReport`, `settle_report`, `prepare_drains`. The two-resource shape is hard-coded in the issuer spawn, `SettleReport` and `prepare_drains`; generalize there rather than special-case a third.
- **Today's probe runtime.** `FluidProbeResources::new` uploads the whole array in one call; `pack_probe_table` builds a `UNIFORM`-only table sized by `MAX_REFLECTION_PROBES` (needs `COPY_DST`); `FluidProbeGpu.layer` is already an indirection word the shader reads; the probe id already reaches the fragment as a flat varying, so the vertex stage can resolve it to a slot there. Fluid vertex storage has room; fragment storage has none. `LevelWorld.reflection_probes` keeps tiles today; `ReflectionProbesSection::from_bytes` reads the whole payload, so stream mode needs a records-only decode over the retained file (`PrlContainer::retained_file`, `positional_io.rs`).
- **The cap's users.** `MAX_REFLECTION_PROBES` splits into the pool's slot count (runtime: table size, shader array length literal, layout test) and the compiler's fallback level cap; `place_probes`' cap step becomes the per-cell fit, after every other drop.
- **Shape and rival.** Chosen: fixed slot pool with a compile-time per-cell fit, whose VRAM stays constant whatever the level's probe count or record size. Rival: whole-resident with several tiles packed per array layer and a larger table, which lifts the cap with less machinery but grows VRAM with probe count times record size (`research.md` §Rivals).
- **Grouping is not the lever.** The stress map's fluids each draw their own height, so cross-entity grouping at one height (`reflection-probe-seams`) barely moves its count (`reflection-probe-seams` `research.md` §Grouping evidence); report the manual stress-map figures against whichever grouping is current.
- **Two pipelines.** Task 8b of `reflection-probes` picks the reflection-free variant per draw; a draw whose bound probes are all non-resident may take it too.
- **First slice.** Records-only decode, slot-indexed table and vertex-stage resolve, all-resident at pool size: proves pixel parity before demand or reads exist. Then the third issuer route and settle answer.
- **Large files.** `fluid_surface.rs` and `fluid_surface.wgsl` are near or past 800 lines; split first, behaviour-preserving, own commit, if the slot work extends them.
- **Docs at promotion.** `resource_management.md` §5.1, §5.3; `rendering_pipeline.md` §4 (a third streamed family), §7.8, §10 (fluid bindings); `build_pipeline.md` id 54 and the stage list; `boot_sequence.md` §1 (settle answers); `large-map-spatial-residency.md` (probes as a tier-planned family).

## Wire format
Section id 54, next internal version (`reflection-probe-seams` also bumps it; whichever lands second takes the version after). Earlier versions stay loadable and install all-resident. The new version keeps the earlier header, records, bindings and layer-major fixed-size tiles, so each probe's tiles stay readable by offset arithmetic, and adds after the bindings a cell relation: a per-probe offset table (probe count + 1 `u32`) into a sorted, deduplicated `u32` cell-id list, probes in record order. The header gains the relation's entry count. Records keep their `layer` field as the probe's first tile in the payload; slots are a runtime notion and never reach the file. A relation naming a cell at or past the cell count, or an offset table that is not monotone or does not end at the entry count, fails the load naming the section.

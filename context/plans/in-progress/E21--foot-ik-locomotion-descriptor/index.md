# Foot IK + Locomotion Descriptor

## Goal
Stop feet from sliding and floating. Add a tag-driven foot-IK modifier to the shipped pose-modifier stack, feed it game-logic ground probes, and calibrate playback so stride matches motion. Two independent halves compose:

- **Foot IK** — a two-bone (hip-knee-ankle) analytic solver run once per leg over a model-declared, N-leg-ready leg set, on masked local-TRS joints, consuming per-foot ground probes from `PoseInputs`. Presence of leg tags builds the modifier, exactly as `aimSpine` tags build the aim-pitch bend today; no descriptor gate. A probe miss immediately and statelessly retains the clip pose for that leg. A hit outside leg reach clamps to the reachable annulus instead of becoming a miss.
- **Locomotion calibration** — a per-clip `travelSpeed` auto-derived from root motion, an authored per-state override, and a rate rework so speed-scaled playback is exactly `measured / authored` instead of `speed_xz / move_speed`. A `speedScale` archetype toggle turns rate-scaling off for authored-cadence gaits. Enemies inherit the calibration; the shipped E10 speed-scaled walk becomes the degenerate case.

Depends on the shipped `E21--pose-modifier-stack` (`context/plans/done/E21--pose-modifier-stack/`): the stack, `JointMask`, `PoseModifier`/`PoseModifierStack`, `PoseInputs`, the `poseMask` loader path, the `sample_*_modified` samplers, and the tick→collector→renderer plumbing this plan extends. Re-grounded on that landed source.

## Scope

### In scope
- A foot-IK `PoseModifier::FootIk` variant on the stack's closed enum (`crates/model/src/pose_modifier.rs`), holding an ordered leg set and iterating it. Per leg it indexes the matching `FootProbe` in `PoseInputs`, forward-computes hip/ankle model-space positions by walking the chain's local transforms (no collision, no wgpu), and solves the knee against the two-bone reachable annulus. Far targets clamp to the segment-sum limit; near targets clamp to the segment-difference limit. It writes hip/knee local rotations and orients the foot toward the probed normal within an angular limit. A height-derived plant weight is full at/below contact and fades across a defined blend band; it preserves clip lift, but cannot distinguish a swing from a large terrain drop during stance without gait-phase input. Each leg is independent; a probe miss immediately leaves that leg on its clip pose. The leg set, probe array, and solver loop are sized for **N legs**; this plan authors and tests bipeds.
- Game-logic ground probes: a fixed-tick step (beside `update_pose_inputs` in `crates/postretro/src/sim/mod.rs`) that, for each leg-tagged animated entity, samples the unmodified world pose to find each declared foot's world position, casts a bounded world-down ray with upward penetration allowance and downward planting reach against the collision world, and writes the per-leg model-space results into `PoseInputs.feet`. Unsupported transforms and every failed probe write fresh misses, clearing stale contacts.
- A locomotion descriptor authored per archetype (Rust ↔ TS/Luau): an optional `locomotion` block carrying a `speedScale` boolean (default `true`), and a per-state `travelSpeed` override on the animation-state descriptor.
- `travelSpeed` auto-derivation at load from an unambiguous locomotion-root translation track (ground units per animated second), the descriptor override taking precedence. Entity transforms own displacement: after measuring, the loader removes the selected root track's net XZ drift while preserving vertical motion and intra-cycle residual, so the sampled pose loops in place. A forest with no valid declared root warns and derives `None`.
- Reworking speed-scaled playback so the rate is `measured_ground_speed / effective_travel_speed` (clamped), replacing the current `speed_xz / move_speed` reference. The v1 consumer is the AI brain's alert-mapped locomotion state, for authoritative local/host enemies and presentation-only remote enemies. The E10 walk is the degenerate case when no root motion, no override, and no `speedScale: false` exist.
- Leg-chain and foot-target joint tagging via glTF `extras`, extending the shipped `poseMask` mechanism: side names for bipeds, indexed names for the N-leg path.
- A behavior-preserving split of `crates/entities/src/components/animation.rs` (~1616 lines) before its playback-rate machinery is extended.
- Headless tests for probe determinism, IK planting on slopes, and `travelSpeed`-driven rate.

### Out of scope
- The pose-modifier stack, masks, aim modifiers, and base plumbing — shipped in the prerequisite.
- A per-archetype IK on/off descriptor field. IK is tag-driven; to disable planting, leave the rig untagged. Dynamic suppression for airborne/dead states is not a descriptor concern; a failed probe is the stateless clip fallback, while a reachable probe target outside leg reach clamps in the solver.
- A locomotion sync-mode enum. Speed-scaling and IK planting are orthogonal and separately gated (a boolean and tag presence); a three-value `speedScaled`/`ikPlant`/`both` selector would encode a choice neither surface owns.
- Hand IK onto the weapon, full-body IK beyond legs, cloth/hair, facial animation (Epic 21 non-goals).
- Any change to hit-zone authority — leg hit capsules keep reading the unmodified world pose (`sample_*_world`, untouched by the stack).
- Blend trees, directional locomotion, multi-locomotion-state selection — deferred.
- Measured-speed playback for arbitrary mesh states or non-AI entities. V1 applies it only to the AI alert-mapped locomotion state.
- Co-op avatar / weapon presentation and any wire change — the co-op-avatar plan.
- Authoring and tuning monsters with more than two legs, and front legs doubling as melee attackers. The data path here is N-leg-sized; multi-legged content, per-monster leg tuning, and the leg↔melee bridge are the later `Multi-legged & melee-capable legs` spec.
- `travelSpeed` derivation for non-forward clips. Derivation measures root XZ displacement magnitude; a turn-in-place or strafe clip with near-zero net root displacement carries a descriptor `travelSpeed` override instead.
- Gait-phase input or stance/swing classification. The v1 height-only plant blend cannot distinguish a clip swing from a stance foot above a large terrain drop; improve that in a follow-up before claiming drop-aware planting.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A character whose rig is leg-tagged plants both feet on flat ground across a walk cycle while a clip-lifted foot retains its lift. The plant blend is height-derived: full at/below contact, linearly fading to clip pose across the defined blend band.
- [ ] On a walkable slope, each planted foot uses the slope contact height and model-space normal. The solve rotates only leg joints and never translates the root. Targets outside the two-bone reachable annulus clamp to its near or far boundary; they do not force clip fallback.
- [ ] When a downward probe finds no ground within reach, hits an unwalkable surface, or cannot be sampled under the supported transform envelope, that leg immediately and statelessly uses its clip pose; other legs are unaffected and prior contacts do not persist.
- [ ] IK is driven by tags alone: the same model with its leg tags removed builds no `FootIk` entry and plants no feet (unit test on `build_pose_modifier_stack`); no descriptor field enables or disables planting (review/grep gate — a negative-existence check, not a runnable assertion).
- [ ] A synthetic model tagged with more than two leg chains drives one independent two-bone solve per leg with no code change — the probe array, leg set, and solver loop are not biped-hardcoded.
- [ ] A clip authored with root motion reports an authored travel speed from its selected locomotion-root translation. A valid declared skin root wins; a single undeclared hierarchy root is accepted; an ambiguous forest without a valid declaration derives `None`. Horizontal net root drift is removed after measurement without changing vertical motion or loop continuity. A per-state `travelSpeed` override replaces the measured value.
- [ ] An AI enemy in its alert-mapped locomotion state moving faster than its clip's authored travel speed plays the clip proportionally faster (and slower when slower), locally and remotely. Both consumers apply the same override → derived clip speed → E10 `speed_xz / move_speed` fallback policy, with the rate within clamp bounds.
- [ ] An archetype with `speedScale: false` does not rate-scale — its authored cadence plays unchanged — while the default (absent block, or `true`) rate-scales.
- [ ] The shipped E10 enemy walk still speed-scales identically when its state declares no derived travel speed, no override, and the default `speedScale` — no behavioral regression.
- [ ] Ground probes produce identical results across repeated headless runs of the same tick sequence.
- [ ] After the `animation.rs` split, every pre-existing `postretro-entities` test passes unchanged and no resulting non-test source file exceeds ~800 lines (test modules are exempt — move them with their targets or into sibling `*_tests` files).

### Task 1: Split `animation.rs` into a module directory (behavior-preserving)
`crates/entities/src/components/animation.rs` is ~1616 lines and Task 4 extends its playback-rate machinery; split it first along existing seams, changing no behavior. The `MeshAnimation` struct + `new` stay in `mod.rs`. Move in-file tests alongside their targets. All existing tests pass with no assertion edits.

### Task 2: `travelSpeed` auto-derivation at load
Derive each clip's authored travel speed at model load in `crates/model`. Select the locomotion root from a valid declared skin skeleton root; when none is declared, accept the only hierarchy root. A forest without a valid declaration is ambiguous: warn and disable derivation rather than selecting topo index 0. For each clip, measure that selected joint's horizontal (XZ) displacement from its first to last translation keyframe and divide by clip duration to get ground units per animated second. No selected root/translation track, non-positive duration, or near-zero net XZ displacement yields `None`, so Task 4 uses the E10 fallback instead of dividing by ≈0. After measuring, subtract the selected track's linear net XZ drift from every key. Preserve Y values and residual intra-cycle horizontal motion, while equalizing the first/last XZ samples so the pose loops in place and entity transforms remain the displacement source. Store the derived value on `AnimationClip` beside `name`/`duration`/`joints`; descriptor override and consumption remain Tasks 3 and 4. Add `crates/model` unit tests for measured speed and neutralization, an explicitly selected nonzero root in a forest, and the ambiguous-forest `None` fallback.

### Task 3: Locomotion descriptor (Rust ↔ TS/Luau)
Add the authored locomotion surface to the mesh descriptor. Extend `MeshDescriptor` (`crates/entities/src/data_descriptors/types/entity.rs`) with an optional `locomotion` block carrying a `speedScale` boolean, defaulting to `true` when the field or block is absent (preserving current behavior). Add an optional per-state `travelSpeed` override (finite, > 0; ground units per animated second) to the animation-state descriptor; when present it replaces the Task 2 derived value for that state's clip. Extend the two hand-written front-ends — `mesh_descriptor_from_js`/`raw_animation_state_from_js` (`crates/scripting-core/src/data_descriptors/js/entity.rs`) and their Luau twins — to parse the new fields into `RawAnimationState`/the raw mesh shape, then funnel through the **existing shared validator** `MeshDescriptor::build`, which already enforces both front-ends' rules; add `travelSpeed` positivity/finiteness there so QuickJS and Luau reject the same inputs. `speedScale` is a plain bool. Mirror the new fields into the **generated** SDK typedef twins `sdk/types/postretro.d.ts` / `.d.luau` and the reference behaviors under `sdk/behaviors/reference/` — the twins regenerate from the hand-written Rust typedef registry `crates/postretro/src/scripting/primitives/mod.rs` (extend the `MeshDescriptor` and `AnimationStateDescriptor` registrations, beside the existing `crossfadeMs?`) via `gen-script-types`, guarded by the `committed_sdk_types_match_current_registry` drift test; extend the registry and regenerate, never hand-edit the twins. ### Task 4: Playback-rate rework to `measured / travelSpeed`
Rework speed-scaled playback (extends `animation/playback.rs` from Task 1) to compute the rate as `measured_ground_speed / effective_travel_speed`, clamped by `RATE_MIN`/`RATE_MAX` and gated by `RATE_CHANGE_EPSILON`. Resolve the denominator in one shared policy: active-state `travelSpeed` override first, selected clip's derived speed second, then the E10 `speed_xz / move_speed` fallback when neither exists. V1 consumes this only for the AI brain's alert-mapped locomotion state: local/host AI uses measured steering velocity; remote presentation uses displayed interpolation velocity. Both honor `speedScale` and the same shared resolution policy without wire fields. `speedScale: false` skips rate-scaling entirely (rate stays 1.0). Strengthen the E10 regression test by loading and identifying the shipped `Walking_A` clip; prove its derived value is `None` separately from store or clip lookup failure.

### Task 5: Foot-IK two-bone solver modifier (N-leg data path)
Add a `PoseModifier::FootIk` variant to the closed enum in `crates/model/src/pose_modifier.rs` (a direct add — the enum is deliberately concrete). It holds an ordered leg set (a `LegChain { chain_mask: JointMask, foot_joint }` per leg, not a fixed pair) and iterates it, indexing the matching `FootProbe` in `PoseInputs` by leg index — a biped runs two solves, a hexapod six, from one loop. Its apply arm joins `apply_pose_modifier_stack`'s match beside the aim/split arms; that arm receives the full per-joint local buffer, so a leg's ancestors compose to model space. Per leg (hip → knee → ankle/foot), forward-compute current hip and ankle model-space positions. The target keeps clip XZ and takes the probe's `contact_height`; the analytic solve clamps hip-to-target reach to the two-bone annulus: segment difference + epsilon through segment sum − epsilon. Thus both too-close and too-far targets solve at the nearest reachable boundary; only degenerate chains retain clip rotations. Orient the foot toward the probed model-space normal within its angular limit. A probe miss is an immediate, stateless per-leg clip fallback. For hits, blend solved rotations by a height-only plant weight: `1.0` at/below contact, linearly to `0.0` over the 0.15 model-unit blend band above contact. This preserves clip swing lift, but a stance foot above a terrain drop follows the same fade and cannot plant down once beyond the band; gait-phase input is a follow-up, not implemented here. Reads only `PoseInputs` and the local buffer — no collision, no wgpu. Add `crates/model` unit tests: flat-ground plant, sloped-ground plant with normal alignment, near/far reach-annulus clamps, miss fallback, the height-blend envelope, and a synthetic model with more than two leg chains producing one independent solve per leg.

### Task 6: Game-logic ground probes feeding `PoseInputs`
The foot-probe types — `FootProbe`, `PoseInputs.feet`/`foot_count`, `MAX_FEET` — land in Task 9 (foundation, Phase 1); this task adds the game-side step that fills them. Probe index `i` aligns with leg-set index `i`; `FootProbe` carries model-space contact height, ground normal, and a hit flag.

Add a fixed-tick probe step in `crates/postretro/src/sim/mod.rs`, ordered **after** `update_pose_inputs`. It reaches per-model skeleton, clips, and the Task 7 leg set through the retained game-side CPU model store the hit-zone system already populates and `simulate_tick` already receives (`HitZoneStore`, keyed by model handle, holding `Arc<Skeleton>` + `Arc<Vec<AnimationClip>>`; it already runs `sample_*_world` for zone bounds); Task 7 installs the leg set into that store in the same load sweep that moves skeleton and clips in. For each leg-tagged animated entity, resolve current sample parameters render-free and sample the animated foot from the **unmodified** model-space pose. Transform that point model→world. Cast from 0.15 model units above the foot and cover that allowance plus 0.5 model units downward, scaled at cast time by entity Y scale. A walkable hit yields model-space contact height and normal; ground beyond the downward reach is a miss. V1 supports upright entities with normalized identity/up-only rotation, positive invertible uniform or non-uniform scale, and a finite transform inverse. Unsupported transforms are misses. Convert contact points with the world→model inverse. Convert normals with the transpose of the model→world linear map, then normalize; never use the inverse as a normal direction under non-uniform scale.

Set `foot_count` to the entity's leg-set length and write `feet[0..foot_count]` (leg index aligned with the leg set) — a read-modify-write that leaves the aim fields `update_pose_inputs` set intact; conversely, `update_pose_inputs` must carry the existing `feet`/`foot_count` forward rather than reconstructing a fresh `PoseInputs` literal that would clobber them. Start every probe pass from a default foot array and set `foot_count` to zero when the model, animation, sample, or transform is unavailable. Therefore a skipped/failed probe clears stale contacts rather than preserving them. The write stays in the tick because the render collector borrows the registry immutably; this world-pose-only sample is distinct from the renderer's modified palette sample. Add a determinism-harness test (`sim/determinism_tests.rs`) asserting repeatable probe outputs and stale-probe clearing.

### Task 7: Leg-chain and foot-target tagging at load (side names + N-leg)

Before that set reaches `LoadedModel` or `FootIk`, validate every retained leg as exactly one connected, non-branching hip→knee→ankle chain whose terminal ankle is that leg's sole `foot*` target; disconnected, branched, short/long, duplicate-target, or non-terminal declarations warn and drop. In `build_pose_modifier_stack`, push one `FootIk` entry carrying the whole validated leg set whenever the set is non-empty — mirroring how a non-empty `aimSpine` chain yields an `AimPitchBend` entry. Unknown/malformed tags warn, never fail. A biped tags `legL`/`legR` + `footL`/`footR`; nothing in the loader, solver, or probe path assumes exactly two. Add `crates/model` tests for the valid set, missing halves, overflow, and malformed topology/foot-target cases, plus the tag-presence gate (AC 4).

## Boundary inventory
The locomotion descriptor crosses Rust ↔ TS/Luau (no wire or FGD surface — mesh presets are authored, not map KVPs). The leg/foot tags cross the glTF-authoring boundary only. Casing: camelCase in TS/Luau and the decoded JSON/table, snake_case in Rust, matching the shipped `crossfadeMs`/`crossfade_ms` and `moveSpeed`/`move_speed` conventions.

| Name | Rust | Authored key (TS / Luau) | Values |
|---|---|---|---|
| locomotion block | `MeshDescriptor.locomotion: Option<LocomotionDescriptor>` | `locomotion?` | object below |
| speed-scale toggle | `LocomotionDescriptor.speed_scale: bool` | `speedScale?` | boolean, default `true` |
| per-state travel-speed override | `AnimationState.travel_speed: Option<f32>` | `travelSpeed?` (on an animation state) | finite, > 0; ground units / animated second |
| leg chain (glTF `extras`) | leg chain mask via `read_pose_masks` | node `extras` `poseMask: "legL"`/`"legR"`, or `"leg0"`, `"leg1"`, … | topo joint set, one per leg |
| foot target (glTF `extras`) | leg foot joint | node `extras` `poseMask: "footL"`/`"footR"`, or `"foot0"`, `"foot1"`, … | single joint, one per leg |

Leg/foot masks extend the shipped `poseMask` `extras` mechanism; they are glTF-authoring only. Side names cover bipeds (L=0, R=1 in the ordered leg set); indexed names are the general N-leg form and align each leg with its `PoseInputs.feet` slot.

# glossy-world-reflections

Brief · resumable · reads: `context/lib/rendering_pipeline.md` §4, §6, §7.3, §7.3a (Reflection, Per-probe sums, Screen-space layer, Reflection quality tiers), §10, §13 · `context/lib/resource_management.md` §4.7, §5 · `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Custom FGD, §Compiler pipeline step 14, §PRL section IDs · `context/lib/testing_guide.md` §Resource bounds · read at a1d14351 · builds after `reflection-probe-fluid-pass-performance` lands on main

## Problem
Owner-requested capability, for a genre-staple look: polished floors and walls that reflect their room, as in Control, where a monument stands free on a gleaming floor. Today only up-facing fluid faces bind a reflection probe: placement, the binding format and the shader all assume water. The first consumer is wet marble and bronze accents in the owner's game, but the feature serves any glossy static surface, not marble alone. When done, every static world face whose material paints sharpness above a floor reflects its room from compile-time probes. Each texel's reflection is blurred by the exponent that shapes its highlight, never sharp enough to read as a mirror, and coloured as its highlight is. A glossy floor also reflects what stands on it, the monument, characters and doors, as water does. It follows the author's reflection style and the player's reflection quality. Water looks as it does today.

## Decisions
- **§13 lifted for opaque world faces (owner).** Image-based specular on static world faces, blurred by the per-texel Blinn-Phong exponent. Still no energy-conserving BRDF and no roughness or metallic input (`rendering_pipeline.md` §13, `index.md` §2 Physical bake, stylized runtime). Amends at promotion: §13's "outside fluid surfaces" exception and its "no input suppresses diffuse" clause, `specular-texel-sharpness`'s tint Decision ("never suppresses diffuse"; here tint fades static diffuse through W, Composite), and `resource_management.md` §3's "Environment-mapped reflections" row. `index.md` §2's "Physical bake, stylized runtime" is reconciled at promotion to name this lift, the image-based half of PBR specular, for world faces only (owner, 2026-11-21).
- **Faces in scope (owner).** Floors and walls; any planar static world face orientation the generalized rules support.
- **Floors add a screen-space layer; other world faces are probe-only (owner, 2026-11-22).** Up-facing glossy world faces, by water's up-facing test, take water's screen-space layer over the probe term at the tiers that run it, with its march and IP rules (§7.3a Screen-space layer). This is the second screen-space use `index.md` §2 Pre-RTX asks owner sign-off for; it is given for up-facing faces only. Walls, ceilings and slopes stay probe-only. The planar layer stays water's.
  - It shows movers and characters (owner): the overlay records after the skinned-mesh pass and before the Ghost Pass. A mover face coplanar with a glossy world face takes no reflection term.
  - Blurred like the probe term (owner): the screen-space colour blurs to the texel's lobe (Blur), so its target and march can carry less detail than water's. Its per-tier values are engine-owned and set at the plan stop from 5300M cost.
- **One planar-reflector path; water moves onto it (owner).** Water's look is pinned by parity, not re-tuned.
- **Generalized probes (owner).** The capture point lifts along the face normal. Up-facing probes, water's and glossy floors', keep capture exclusion, since the screen-space layer draws what it drops (`resource_management.md` §5.2); the rule's water inputs become the probe's bound reflector faces. A probe binding walls, ceilings or slopes captures every static brush: they have no screen-space layer.
  - One probe per coplanar glossy face group, not room-centre probes: those leave capture-shadow holes behind pillars, which the IP rules forbid filling. Probe boundaries go behind occluders, as an authoring rule (roadmap, Fluid Rendering, owner 2026-11-22). A freestanding object on a floor is excluded and reflects through the screen-space layer; one the rule keeps leaves its capture shadow withheld.
  - `reflection_volume` may claim glossy world faces as well as water. A box whose faces face several ways makes one probe per facing, sharing the box, each with its own capture point and hemisphere (owner).
  - Placement and capture stay compile-time (`resource_management.md` §5, IP fences below).
- **Opt-in by sharpness (owner).** A material reflects only if its `_p` sharpness has texels above an engine-owned sharpness floor. This is decided per material at compile time and weighted per texel at runtime: the reflection weight fades in from 0 at the floor to full over a band of exponent above it, so a sharpness gradient never shows a seam at the floor, even at grazing (owner). Prefix-only materials (no `_p`) never reflect. PBR-converted materials (owner uses Material Maker) all carry `_p`; the floor keeps rough ones free. This departs from gi-rtx-lite graft 5's explicit material flag (`context/research/gi-rtx-lite/index.md`): opt-in is implicit in `_p`, so sharpening a material also spends probe budget.
- **Blur (owner).** Compile-time prefiltered colour mips, selected per texel by a lobe matched to the highlight's exponent, so a texel's reflection blurs as wide as its highlight. No glossy world face is a mirror (owner, 2026-11-22): the selection never reads sharper than an engine-owned minimum-blur mip, on walls and floors alike, so reflections keep the pixel world's soft, low-detail read. Water keeps mip 0.
- **Strength and colour (owner).** Strength is Schlick Fresnel on water's reflectance scale (0.02 × `surfaceReflectivity`, `scripting.md` §2, §7.3a): a fixed head-on base of 0.04 for untinted texels, raised toward 1 by the texel's tint, so a metal approaches a full tinted reflection. `_s` keeps shaping highlights only, so Material Maker's maps, which carry no specular-strength map, fill every input. A texel's reflection, both layers, is coloured as its highlight is, under the tint rule `specular-texel-sharpness` owns. `resource_management.md` §5's "a later glass or polished-stone reader takes its base reflectance from its material" is amended at promotion: the base is engine-owned until a `materials.toml` override exists.
- **Overlay pass, not forward integration (owner).** After the opaque passes, an overlay pass redraws bound glossy faces onto exactly the pixels forward shaded whose depth is still the face's. Forward has no free binding (§10), and this leaves the always-run forward shader's cost and registers untouched (§7.3 Surface Depth cost note).
- **Composite: static-diffuse mix (owner, 2026-11-21).** A glossy texel shows forward + W·(R − D_static), with W the reflection weight (Fresnel × sharpness fade × capture-shadow weight), R the reflection coloured as its highlight is, and D_static forward's baked diffuse after its entity-shadow subtraction, so a character's shadow fades with W (owner). The reflection replaces only the light it re-shows: dynamic light, highlights and emissive never dim, and where W is 0 forward's result stands. On a floor with a screen-space layer, the reflected colour and W take water's layered form (§7.3a Weight and composite) with Fresnel × sharpness fade as F; at c = 0 that is the line above. Water keeps its own composite.
  - SDF-shadowed lights are soft-deprecated (owner): D_static leaves their diffuse out, so their share of R counts twice on glossy faces, an accepted limit.
- **Carved materials follow the player's Surface Depth setting (owner).** With Surface Depth on, the overlay reruns the march, so a carved texel's top and sides each reflect as their highlights shade. With it off, the overlay reflects about the flat plane and the normal map and pays no march. Players who chose carving pay for carved reflections; nobody else does. Cost is measured at the plan stop.
- **Live lighting reads the per-probe sums (owner, 2026-11-22).** Glossy world faces are static geometry, so they read `reflection-probe-fluid-pass-performance`'s sums; no fragment evaluates a stored light. The sums dispatch once per frame with any probe-term draw, fluid or overlay, ahead of the first, and their storage moves from the fluid surfaces to the probe records' owner (§7.3a Dispatch, Storage).
- **IP (owner, 2026-11-22).** The owner accepts the engineering reading of Tencent WO2026113651A1 claim 1 in `plans/done/reflection-probes/ip-check.md` §1, as for the sums brief: glossy faces sample their material per fragment as water does and add no claim-1 element, so counsel does not gate the build. The claims are re-read when the US and EP national-phase entries publish. Binding fences:
  - Placement and capture at compile time; the camera never places or moves a probe.
  - Tiles never rewritten or re-shaded at runtime; live lighting stays a post-sample ratio.
  - Capture shadow withheld, never filled (SH, lightmap, screen space or another probe).
  - Hard per-face binding by position; no lookup-direction value picks the probe.
  - No precomputed intersection table; no history.
  - Only probe-bound faces of geometry with fixed position and orientation read the sums.
  - The probe term and the screen-space term stay separate quantities. The screen-space coefficient never gains the capture-shadow weight s, and its march and blur never read probe colour or distance tiles (WO claim 15; `plans/done/reflection-probe-fluid-pass-performance/ip-check.md` §Shape B).
- **Probe cap stays at 256 (owner).** Raising it is a separate brief beside the roadmap's `reflection-probe-streaming`, sized from measured glossy content. Probes still gain a memory-ledger row, so their cost shows against the 2 GB floor (`index.md` §1.1). Past the cap, probes that bind only world faces drop first, authored or derived, smallest bound area first; water keeps every probe it has today (owner, 2026-11-21). The build warning names each dropped probe's faces instead of only counting them.
- **Governed by existing switches (owner).** `render.reflections.style`: pixel uses the texel-centre reconstruction from `specular-texel-sharpness`; smooth evaluates per fragment. `reflection_quality`: off draws nothing; low draws the probe term on world faces; tiers that run water's screen-space layer add the floor layer. No new author or player surface.
- **Non-goals.**
  - A metallic input: the tint rule covers metals (Strength and colour).
  - Glossy mover faces: the mover pass has its own layout and a documented register cliff (§7.3), and a probe bound to a moving surface needs owner IP review before it reads the sums.
  - Skinned meshes: they never bind the world material bundle.
  - Probe blending: retired (roadmap, Fluid Rendering, owner 2026-11-22). Boundaries go behind occluders; near geometry goes to capture exclusion plus the floor layer.
  - Mover-aware captures: draft `mover-aware-reflection-captures.md` owns them.
  - A PBR importer in `postretro-tool`: `specular-texel-sharpness` holds that non-goal and documents the conversion instead.
  - Fluid surfaces in reflections: water stays absent from every capture, as today, so a glossy wall shows the pool's bed, not its surface. The floor layer runs before the fluid pass and shows no water either.
  - Reflections of reflections, and highlights in reflections: captures shade every face with its diffuse and emissive only, so a glossy face appears in other probes' captures without its reflection or its highlights.
  - Runtime content off floors: captures hold glow at reference, and movers, characters, projectiles and dynamic lights are absent from probes; animated lights and area glass follow only through the share ratio (owner, 2026-11-21). The floor layer shows what is on screen at the tiers that run it. `drafts/dynamic-reflection-layer/` owns walls, off-screen content and Low.
  - A per-material reflectivity override or opt-out in `materials.toml` (owner, deferred). Strength already uses water's reflectance meaning, so a later override is purely additive.
  - The roughness→`_p` conversion formula: the sharpness brief owns its doc row.

## Acceptance
### Automated
**Water parity**
- [ ] On every water map in `content/dev`, for every probe that binds no world face, placement output is byte-equal to the build before, matching each probe by the faces it binds: boxes, capture points and bindings up to probe renumbering. So are mip-0 colour tiles, distance tiles and exclusion classes. Shares match on the +Y, ±X and ±Z lobes, with −Y zero.
- [ ] Water renders within 1/255 of the build before at the reflection-probes parity views, every tier and both styles. Water samples colour mip 0 only.
**Opt-in**
- [ ] A material without `_p`, or with `_p` but no texel above the floor, gets no probe and no binding. No overlay draw reaches its faces, and its render is byte-identical to the build before under both styles.
- [ ] On a bound face, a texel at or below the floor draws no reflection term, a texel past the band draws the full term, and a texel inside the band draws a fraction rising with its exponent. At grazing, adjacent texels across the floor contour differ by no more than adjacent texels inside the band.
**Placement and capture**
- [ ] Derived probes on a floor, a wall, a down-facing ceiling and a 45° slope each sit on their face's front side, lifted along its normal. Coplanar glossy faces of one group share a probe; faces on different planes, or on one plane facing opposite ways, never share a derived probe.
- [ ] A `reflection_volume` claims glossy world faces inside it. A box over neither water nor glossy faces drops with a warning.
- [ ] In one authored box binding a glossy floor and a glossy wall, the floor reflects the wall and the wall reflects the floor; in one binding a pool and a glossy wall, the pool reflects the wall, and the wall reflects the room with the pool's bed where the water lies, as captures hold no fluid surface today (pin P3).
- [ ] An authored box over glossy floors on two heights and two glossy walls facing different ways makes three probes, each with the authored box as its box: one binding both floors, one per wall.
- [ ] An authored box binding faces on several planes, including a pool above a lower glossy floor, keeps each probe's capture point on the front side of every face that probe binds, or a face it cannot serve binds nothing and the warning names it (pin P4).
- [ ] An unobstructed glossy floor and wall reflect over their whole area: no fragment lies in capture shadow cast by the reflector's own surface.
- [ ] A glossy inlay set flush in a floor whose material does not reflect reflects over its whole area, and on a glossy floor crossed by a coplanar matte strip, glossy fragments beyond the strip from the capture point lie outside capture shadow: no face in the reflector's plane casts capture shadow on a bound face (pin P2).
- [ ] Behind a pillar on a glossy wall's plane, and behind a brush a glossy floor's probe keeps (set `always`), as seen from the capture point, fragments draw no probe term. A source gate shows no fill from SH, lightmap, screen space or another probe, as the water gate does.
- [ ] A probe binding up-facing faces alone, water, a glossy floor or both, excludes a brush small relative to its bound area by water's rule, and keeps one with a downward face over a bound face; a probe binding walls, ceilings or slopes captures that brush. `always` and `never` apply to both.
- [ ] A freestanding monument on a glossy floor, each of its brushes under the exclusion threshold: no floor fragment loses its probe term to it, so the region behind it, seen from the capture point, reflects the room. At the tiers that run the floor layer the monument reflects; at Low it is absent from the reflection (pin P16).
- [ ] Lobe weights for any unit direction sum to 1. For y ≥ 0 they equal today's +Y, ±X and ±Z weights exactly, with −Y zero.
- [ ] A glossy down-facing ceiling over a floor `light_area` panel takes that light's share on the −Y lobe: pulsing the light moves the reflected panel and its lit floor together.
**Area lights**
- [ ] A glossy floor under a static `light_area` skylight reflects its glass at the glow, blurred by each texel's exponent, and draws no highlight from that light.
- [ ] A script turning that light off scales its reflected glass and the reflected room by the same live-lighting ratio on the same frame, at every tier; in a fixture lit by that light alone, both go dark, and turning it on restores both.
- [ ] A `light_area` glass face whose material paints sharpness above the floor is a reflector: its glow survives the composite unchanged at W near 1.
**Shading**
- [ ] The exponent→mip function is one pinned function shared by the compiler prefilter and the shader, with a GPU-free reference. Blur grows monotonically as the exponent falls; it never selects sharper than the minimum-blur mip, at the exponent cap included, and never past the header's last colour mip.
- [ ] A one-texel bright source, prefiltered, spreads at each colour mip to the reflected-direction half-width of the exponent the function maps to that mip, within one texel of that mip.
- [ ] At every colour mip, each tile border copies its wrapped interior.
- [ ] A direction reads, at every colour mip down to 4², the interior texel the compiler wrote for it there, under both mips of a selection between two (pin P6).
- [ ] A texel's reflection colour follows its highlight's tint, in both layers: with `_t` by the tint rule, without `_t` by raw albedo.
- [ ] A glossy texel's output equals forward + W·(R − D_static) within one half-float step per channel, and on a floor with screen-space confidence c, the layered form within the same step. Where W is 0 (capture shadow with c = 0, the Off tier, an unbound face) the output is byte-identical to forward's. With R forced equal to forward's own static diffuse, read back from the forward pass on the same frame, every glossy pixel at c = 0 is within one half-float step of forward's, and a deliberately perturbed D_static term fails it. A tint-1 texel under a static point light that owns its face keeps its highlight at W near 1.
- [ ] The overlay's D_static matches forward's static diffuse within one half-float step on fixtures that each isolate one term: baked lightmap direct under a normal map, an animated baked light mid-pulse, a lightmap block miss, SH indirect under Surface Depth AO on a carved texel, and the ambient floor. Under an SDF light it leaves that light's diffuse out, and the measured double count is reported.
- [ ] A character's promoted-light shadow on a glossy floor fades with static diffuse by (1 − W): D_static carries forward's union subtraction at the same promotion weight, and no output channel is below 0 at head-on or grazing, untinted or tint-1, over a dark reflected ceiling (pin P13).
- [ ] On every frame of an animated baked light's pulse, a promoted light's crossfade, an SH cluster becoming resident and a lighting-mask toggle, the overlay's D_static matches forward's static diffuse of that same frame; any mask with a bit of the all-terms set off, including on its toggle frame, makes the overlay draw no term, as for water (pin P14).
- [ ] The difference a dynamic point light and a muzzle flash make to a glossy floor, light on minus light off, is the same with and without the overlay within one half-float step, at head-on, at grazing and on a tint-1 metal, with the floor layer off. So is the difference a scripted `emissive_volume` face and an animated `light_area` face make mid-pulse. A source gate refuses the dynamic light list, the emissive volume table and the SDF selection and visibility target in the overlay's layout; the owner stream, specular lights, influence and shadow-pool bindings it holds are read only by the union subtraction (pin P13).
- [ ] A glossy floor, wall and down-facing ceiling with a stored light forced off its reference match the GPU-free live-lighting reference per lookup direction within 1/255, read through the sums.
- [ ] Head-on, an untinted texel reflects at 0.04 and a tint-1 texel at 1; a texel of a material without `_t` (A = 255) reflects at 0.04, coloured by raw albedo. At grazing all approach 1. The probe share scales by the capture-shadow weight. Changing `_s` changes the highlight and leaves the reflection byte-identical.
- [ ] Under pixel style, fragments inside one magnified texel get identical reflection colour, mip and weight, and adjacent texels differ. Under smooth style they vary within the texel. The first frame after a committed style switch, either way, follows the new style (pin P9).
**Floor screen-space layer**
- [ ] A one-pixel bright hit in the floor layer spreads at a texel to a width that grows as its exponent falls, matching its probe term's blur within the tolerance the plan pins; a texel at the exponent cap and one at the minimum-blur exponent read the same blur.
- [ ] At the tiers that run the floor layer, a character and a door standing on a glossy floor reflect in it on the frame they move; at Low they do not. A ghost, fluid surface, billboard or fog never appears in it.

## Path
- **Compiler seams.** `placement.rs` `up_facing_faces` becomes the reflector-face census: id-52 up-facing faces plus glossy id-17 faces. Glossiness comes from the specular slot's B channel (`bake_specular_slot`; 0 means no `_p`), which `ReflectionProbeInputs` can already reach (`research.md` §Stage order).
  - Generalize `plane_heights` / `plane_of` to planes (normal and offset), and `derive_probes`' grouping with them.
  - Generalize `CAPTURE_LIFT_M` along the normal, `fit_rays` to the face's hemisphere, and `PointClassifier::settle` to in-plane axes.
  - Box fit and live-lighting shares work over the face's hemisphere: make `share.rs`'s `direction.y < 0` test the face's hemisphere.
  - Exclusion: `classify` reads W from `PlacedProbe::bound_area` and the fixed `WATER_REFLECTOR_NORMAL`; the underside rule's `bound_water` reads id 52. Feed both from the probe's bound faces, and skip classification for probes that are not up-facing. The thin floor couples to `SSR_COARSEST_STRIDE_DIVISOR`: a floor-layer stride coarser than water's moves it for floor probes.
  - Bound reflector faces must be transparent to their own probe's primary rays, as water is absent from `SceneTracer` (`research.md` §The reflector is in its own capture).
- **Runtime seams.**
  - Per-vertex world probe index: a forward-free side stream like the specular owner stream (§6), built like `fill_probe_indices`.
  - Overlay draws: an `IndirectDrawPlan` over glossy bucket ranges, with depth `Equal` and forward's `@invariant` clip-position computation, so every overlay fragment lands on a pixel forward shaded. Forward writes no depth, has no `discard` and no MSAA, and the HDR target (`Rgba16Float`) blends. Recording after the mover pass needs a guard for coplanar mover pixels, such as a stencil bit the mover pass writes.
  - Composite blend: additive (source × 1 + destination × 1) with a signed source into the `Rgba16Float` target, so forward is untouched. D_static copies forward's inline static diffuse (`forward.wgsl` `fs_main`): lightmap direct and its vertex inputs, the animated weight maps, SH indirect with Surface Depth occlusion on carved texels, the ambient floor, and the shadowmask union subtraction at the promotion weight, which binds the shadowmask and the promotion inputs. It leaves out the SDF diffuse loop. The per-pixel SH read is the Mac cost that pushed fluid lighting per vertex; measure it in the first slice. The union subtraction reads the specular owner stream, the specular light list, light influence and the shadow pools; the overlay needs no dynamic light list, emissive volume inputs or `area-lights` glow.
  - Reuse `fluid_reflection_ray.wgsl`'s search, fade and composite, with `fluid_reflection_above_horizon` generalized to the face plane.
  - Colour arrays need `mip_level_count` past 1. BC6H stops the chain at 4².
- **Live lighting.** The blend is `fluid_reflection_ratio_from_sums` in `fluid_reflection.wgsl`; the light helpers are in `fluid_reflection_lights.wgsl`. `FluidProbeResources`, owned by `InstalledFluidSurfaces`, holds the sums, and `record_fluid_surface_passes` dispatches them inside the fluid pass's draw gate on `FluidSurfacePass::has_probe_term_draws`. Both move: ownership with the probe records, dispatch ahead of the overlay. `FLUID_PROBE_SUMS_BYTES` derives from `REFLECTION_PROBE_LOBE_COUNT`; the WGSL arrays and unrolled lobe loops hard-code 5. ## Wire format
ReflectionProbes (id 54), version 3. It mirrors version 2's layout (`level-format` `reflection_probes.rs` doc comment); a version-2 section is refused. Little-endian, unsigned counts in the header as today.
- **Lobes.** Lobe count 6, order +Y, +X, −X, +Z, −Z, −Y. −Y is appended, so water's five lobes keep their indices.
- **Colour mips.** The header gains a u32 colour mip count (≥ 1). Each probe's colour tile stores mips 0..count in sequence, each with a 1-texel border copied from its wrapped interior. The distance tile stays one level.
- **Bindings.** Two lists, each ascending strictly, with a u32 count each in the header:
  - fluid: `u32 volume, u32 face, u32 probe` (id-52 numbering), as today;
  - world: `u32 face, u32 probe` (id-17 numbering).
  - An empty list is count 0. A face binds at most once.
- **Probe placement.** The record's `layer` field keeps version 2's meaning: one tile per array layer (owner).

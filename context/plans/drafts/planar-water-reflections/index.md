# planar-water-reflections

## Problem

Requested capability, from the owner after the planar water spike. Today large water reflects through a compile-time probe plus SSR, and SSR loses anything that leaves the main screen, so nearby walls, posts, movers and characters vanish from a lake as the camera turns. The spike showed a mirrored second view over one water plane fixes that at full reach, and that drawing it is cheap: at one lake pose on the GTX 1660, Windows release live, its reflection GPU work is 0.21 ms against SSR very_high's 1.75. Its extra cost was the mirror's walk over the old step-capped portal walk; the mirror now rides the landed bounded-region walk (`research.md` §Post-walk refresh). When this is done, a player at `reflection_quality` very_high or ultra sees the largest visible water plane reflect the room, its movers, characters, sprites and particles from a mirrored view, whether or not they are on screen, in the author's chosen reflection style. Every other reflecting water surface, puddles included, keeps SSR plus probe. Tiers high and below look and cost exactly as today.

## Decisions

- **One mirroring system per surface.** The selected plane reflects planar plus probe; every other reflecting face reflects SSR plus probe, and the SSR pass skips the selected plane's draws. The §7.3a composite `F·[c·X + (1−c)·s·R]` holds per surface, with X planar on the selected plane and SSR elsewhere.
- **Planar at very_high and ultra only.** It shows what very_high's SSR shows within the mirror's content, plus off-screen content, so a lower tier never reaches further (`player_options.md` §4). Planar at high waits on new measurements.
- **Layer chosen per draw; fluid shaders unedited.** Selected-plane draws bind a group-5 scene-inputs group carrying the planar target, other draws the SSR group, and each layer is read at its own scale. `fluid_surface.wgsl`, `fluid_reflection.wgsl`, the CPU reference in `render-cpu/src/fluid_surface/` and their text pins stay unedited.
- **The planar layer follows `reflections.style`.** Style is the author's look (`scripting.md`), so a tier never changes it. In pixel style the planar pass tilts each water cell's ray exactly as SSR and the probe term do, then samples the mirror where that ray lands; tilt moves where it samples, never c. Smooth style tilts nothing.
- **Mirror visibility rides the bounded-region walk.** The mirror walks from its seed cells through the walk's seeded entry (`rendering_pipeline.md` §2) under the shared step cap, which is a backstop, not a path. Reach is full by default; D stays an engine mechanism, off by default.
- **The mirror walk is cropped to the selected plane.** The mirror's walk and cull cover only the plane's projected bounds, padded by the largest deviation pixel-style tilt gives the reflected ray. The reflection shows only through the plane, so the crop drops only cells no mirror sample reaches, and it cuts portal tests, mirror draws and demand.
- **Mirror demand follows the path rules.** On a completed mirror walk with the main view on a walk path, mirror cells join mesh, mover and particle collection, SH sample regions, lightmap demand and animated-lightmap compose; they never request probes (`rendering_pipeline.md` §4). On any fallback they add no new reads: a tripped mirror walk draws the camera's visible cells that fall in the mirror frustum, all of which the main view already draws. During Settling, mirror cells join the settle set on any path, as the main view's drawn cells do (`boot_sequence.md` §1); the no-new-reads rule applies from reveal.
- **Mirror-only cells count against the bake-gap gate, on water fixtures.** The gate's own fixtures hold no water. A water fixture that fails it stops the build for an owner decision. Levers, in order: D, then a mirror cell table loaded only at planar tiers, which is a new PRL section with its own brief. The id-51 set is never widened for mirror cells (`rendering_pipeline.md` §2, §4): its mandatory entries load at every tier.
- **Mirror-only admissions yield to the main view.** Lights and mesh instances only the mirror reaches rank after every main-view admission, ghosts and shadow-only casters included, and a slot held from an earlier frame gives them no claim against a main-view admission. A full pool drops mirror-only ones first. A mirror-only light's shadow shows only inside the reflection, never on the world outside it.
- **Presets are engine constants.** Both tiers start at half scale, D and R off, and the owner retunes them late in the build from a motion read. No preset becomes a player setting or map key; the spike's env seeds, dev-panel sliders and capture controls stay dev-tools or capture only.
- **Selection stays automatic, behind an engine-owned area floor and the camera's reach.** Selection scores projected solid angle with 1.25× hysteresis, so without a floor a puddle near the eye can win. Planes below the floor never compete, and neither does a plane with no seed cell in the camera's reach that frame, so on a walk path a lake behind a wall never holds selection over a visible pool. On a main-view fallback the camera's reach is its drawn set, as the spike does, so planar survives a transient fallback and a level with no portals. No map key or mapper override (`rendering_pipeline.md` §7.3a).
- **No plane, no planar.** Eye below the plane or within `FLUID_FOG_BAND`, no plane above the floor, or a tier below very_high: that water renders SSR plus probe at the tier. Planar keeps the reflecting gate's probe requirement; the probe fills pixels the mirror misses.
- **Mirror view content.** Static world always, plus movers, skinned meshes, billboards and particles. Ghosts, SSR, bloom and the viewmodel stay out (`rendering_pipeline.md` §7.3a): the viewmodel is a main-view overlay, SSR and bloom are post-passes over the main view, and ghosts are the lib's standing exclusion. Water stays out too, as in the spike: drawing it would bind a probe in a planar pass, which the IP rules forbid.
- **IP rules.** The spike contract's rows carry forward: static world always drawn and any planar/probe split by distance only (NetEase CN117496039A); single-threaded recording on the frame encoder (Tencent CN116778049B); no Fresnel or view-direction cut, routing by plane identity never per pixel (Microsoft US10586375B2); no s, probe colour, distance tile or probe binding (Tencent WO2026113651A1 cl. 15). The pixel tilt follows `ip-check.md` D1–D9, path P2.
- **Counsel does not gate the build.** The owner has reviewed the cited patents and how to avoid them, so the build proceeds without `ip-check.md` §For counsel. Every builder follows the IP notes closely: each worker brief carries the full text of `ip-check.md` §Design rules D1–D9 and §Recommended path (P2), the IP-rules Decision, `rendering_pipeline.md` §7.3a Planar layer IP rules and the SSR IP rules, and `research.md` §IP map, and review checks the planar shader text and its routing against them.
- **Planar target lifecycle mirrors SSR's.** Allocated at scene extent times preset scale while a level with a candidate plane is installed at a planar tier, and held across frames with no selected plane; rebuilt on resize; released at lower tiers and unload. Tier changes compile nothing. This holds the target where §7.3a's text allocates it only with a selected plane, since per-selection allocation churns at the fog band; promotion updates §7.3a.
- **Spike records land with the change.** The spike's contract and findings move to `plans/done/planar-water-spike/`, and source comments citing the spike contract repoint to `context/lib/`.
- **Sequence.** After `portal-walk-bounded-regions` (done); before reflection-probe fluid-pass performance, which carries per-draw routing into its own draw shape.
- **Non-goals.** Fog and smoke raymarching in the mirror, so reflections over a fogged room show unfogged: own roadmap item. Multiple planes: one plane per frame is the cost cap. Non-horizontal mirrors: selection, the clip plane and the crop assume a horizontal plane. Planar on non-fluid surfaces: routing lives on fluid draws. Non-fluid puddles and wet floors: `ssr-decals`. Water with no probe or a non-resident one: probe streaming owns the miss policy.

**One frame, two layers**
- [ ] At very_high and at ultra, one GPU frame holding a large plane and a second reflecting plane at another height: the large plane reads the planar layer (an asymmetric object outside the main view lands on its mirrored pixel) and the second plane reads SSR (an on-screen mover lands on its mirrored pixel there, which the probe alone cannot show).
- [ ] The SSR pass records for the second plane and spends nothing on the selected plane's draws in that frame. With the selected plane the only reflecting water on screen, no SSR pass records.
- [ ] Pixel style: each layer is read at its own target scale when planar and SSR scales differ.
- [ ] Pixel style: for the same cell, clock and texels-per-pixel, the planar pass's tilted ray equals the SSR pass's ray (CPU reference). On one GPU frame pair at the same pose and inputs, an asymmetric reflected edge on the selected plane shifts in the same direction and by the same texel count with planar on as with planar forced off, away from depth edges. Smooth style: the planar layer applies no tilt.
- [ ] Tilt never changes c: at a fixed pose with D and R off, c over the selected plane matches the same frame with the offset forced to zero, except where the tilted lookup lands on an empty mirror texel.
- [ ] Same-frame mirror (O6): an object moved between two consecutive GPU frames lands on its new mirrored pixel in the second frame.
- [ ] A billboard and a particle above the selected plane land on their mirrored pixels, facing the mirror camera, including one outside the main view's frustum and visible cells.
- [ ] A mover and a skinned mesh in mirror-only cells, off the main screen, land on their mirrored pixels on the selected plane.
- [ ] A ghost above the selected plane and the viewmodel add no pixel to the planar layer, while a real mesh at the ghost's pose lands on its mirrored pixel in the same setup.

**Mirror visibility and demand**
- [ ] At a fixed pose, the visible and fog sets the main view's draw cull and fog pass receive match with planar on and off (O1).
- [ ] The synthetic seeded-walk headroom test keeps its uncropped mirror mode and adds a cropped one: the step cap stays at least twice the worst portal-test count in both, every cropped reach is a subset of the same pose's uncropped reach, and some pose's is strictly smaller.
- [ ] Crop pad (CPU): over sampled eyes and plane extents, every point on the selected plane, displaced along any ray within the largest pixel-style tilt of its reflected ray at any depth, projects through the mirror cull view-projection inside the padded crop, including planes clipped by the near plane.
- [ ] A pose whose main view is on a fallback path: the mirror adds no lightmap or SH reads; already-resident mirror blocks still draw; misses are counted.
- [ ] A mirror step-cap trip, forced through a test-only cap on the mirror's seeded walk, draws only cells the camera's visible set holds within the cropped mirror frustum (O5), adds no new reads, and logs no SolidCellFallback.
- [ ] A pose with mirror-only cells on a walk path: those cells reach lightmap demand, SH sample regions and compose, and request no probe; once demand at the held pose has drained, the pose draws zero visible lightmap misses.
- [ ] With the shadow pool and the mesh-instance cap full of main-view admissions, adding mirror-only lights and instances evicts no main-view admission. With room, mirror-only lights take shadow slots and mirror-only instances draw. A mirror-only light holding a slot yields it the frame a main-view light needs it, even when the challenger scores under the eviction margin, for dynamic, promoted-static (inside its sticky window) and animated-baked lights, and when mirror-only baked lights hold the promoted cap (O7, O15). The full pool includes ghost draws and shadow-only casters.
- [ ] At a pose where a mirror-only light holds a shadow slot, every pixel off the selected plane matches the same frame with mirror lighting excluded, and that light shows in the reflection.
- [ ] Settling with the main view on a fallback and the mirror walk completed (O3): the held frame's lightmap settle set holds the mirror cells' blocks; on the first world frame on the same fallback, the mirror requests no block it did not already hold.

**Selection**
- [ ] A plane below the area floor is never selected, even when nearer and larger in projected solid angle than the lake. A plane just above the floor is selectable.
- [ ] A plane in the frustum with no seed cell in the camera's reach is not selected; a lower-scoring plane with a seed is (O12). On a main-view fallback, a plane with a seed in the drawn set stays selectable (O14). A reflecting water plane with no bound probe is never selected.
- [ ] Eye below a plane, and eye inside its fog band: that plane is not selected and its water reads SSR at the tier; with no other plane qualifying, no plane is selected.
- [ ] Hysteresis: a challenger at or below 1.25× the held score does not switch the plane; one above it does. A held plane that leaves the frustum yields that frame (O13).
- [ ] Plane switch (O4, O5): on the frame selection moves from one plane to another, the new plane reads this frame's mirror and the old plane reads SSR in that same frame, including when that frame's mirror walk trips.
- [ ] After a level change, the first frame's selection ignores the previous level's held plane (O11).

**Tiers and lifetime**
- [ ] At high and below, no plane is selected, no mirror walk runs, the mirror adds no lightmap, SH or light-ranking demand, no planar pass records, and no planar target is allocated (O8).
- [ ] At a pose with a selected plane, switching high → very_high → high creates no pipeline, allocates the planar target, then releases it; on the frame the tier drops, no mirror walk runs and the mirror adds no demand (O8). At very_high, frames with no selected plane keep the target (O10). Resize rebuilds the target and the group carrying it at the new extent and scale, including when the resize lands on the allocating frame (O9). Unload releases it.
- [ ] Zero selected planes at very_high: the planar pass records nothing and SSR covers all water. A level with no candidate plane allocates no planar target at very_high; a level with one holds the target from install, before any plane is selected; a fluid refresh that removes the last candidate releases it.

## Path

- **Start from the spike.** Keep `MirrorCamera`, `select_plane`, `determine_mirror_visible_cells`, the mirror cull and mask, and the residency unions. Its visibility side is ported, not merged: the landed walk split `portal_vis.rs` into `region_walk/`, so port `mirror_visibility.rs` and its `portal_traverse_seeded` call onto `portal_walk_seeded`. That entry returns a completed reach or a trip with no fallback set, so the spike's `determine_with_step_limit` fallback (every non-solid cell in the mirror frustum, marked drawn) is replaced per Decisions. Cap `MAX_PORTAL_WALK_STEPS` (5,000), shared with the spike's `DEFAULT_MIRROR_WALK_STEPS`, and hardcoded in the seeded entry, so the forced-trip row needs a test-only cap seam; `PORTAL_WALK_EPOCH` 3. Recheck the renderer side against the new base. Drop or gate the scaffolding: `POSTRETRO_PLANAR_*` seeds, `launch_settings.rs`, the dev-panel header, and the SSR early return on `planar_on`. Evidence stays on the spike branch; images and large per-run files live only in the Windows spike worktree (`NOT-IN-GIT.md`).
- **Routing is half built.** The spike's `split_planar_ranges` splits draws per part, but binds every non-selected part to a placeholder zero group (`planar_override.rs`), so other planes get probe only, and it releases the SSR target with planar on (`reflection_pass.rs`). SSR on other planes, with both targets alive at very_high and ultra, is new work. Each group carries its own scale uniform.
- **Riskiest assumptions first.** A second group-5 bind group with its own uniform must pass the source-text pins on `record_draws`, `rebind_reflection_target`, bind-group creation counts and the "exactly two `scene_inputs.rebuild(` callers" pin. Build routing and the two-layer GPU test before anything else. Alongside it, run the seeded cap-headroom sweep: the cap was set from camera walks only, and the seeded worst case so far is fixtures only.
- **Scratch.** A completed walk's reach borrows its `PortalWalkScratch` until that scratch's next walk. Give the mirror its own scratch, one per view like the per-view cull buffers, rather than copying the camera's result out first.
- **Crop.** The spike computes no screen bounds: `select_plane` scores in world space. The plane is fixed under reflection, and the mirror target is screen-aligned once its X flip is undone, so project the plane's bounds through the mirror cull view-projection, clip at the near plane, pad, and compose the crop into the view-projection the walk and cull take; the seeded entry's full-screen start then covers only the crop. The pad is an angle: twice the largest normal tilt (`research.md` §Post-walk refresh).
- **Selection and reach.** The spike's `select_plane` filters faces by the main frustum only, and hysteresis holds a plane while it scores above zero. Restrict candidates to planes with a seed cell in this frame's camera reach before scoring.
- **Admission rank.** The spike feeds mirror reach into light reachability through `include_mirror_lighting`, untagged, and shadow slots carry incumbents across frames under `EVICTION_MARGIN`. The yield needs a main-view or mirror-only tag on each light and instance before shadow ranking and the instance planner, ranked after ghosts there.
- **Collection.** The spike collects particles against the main view's visible cells and frustum; mirror particles need the mirror's cells and frustum.
- **Settling.** The held frame walks only the camera today, and the spike passes no mirror cells there; Settling needs the mirror walk on its held pose (O2).
- **Pixel tilt.** `ip-check.md` §The tilt as built today maps SSR's helpers; path P2 there is the chosen one. Its rival P1, a DuDv-style screen offset, is low risk too but only approximates SSR's tilt. The spike's full-screen resolve has no UV, texture size or derivatives, so the planar resolve becomes a raster pass over the selected plane's fluid draws.
- **Instruments.** Cap headroom: `region_walk/real_map_sweep.rs` drives only the camera entry, and the visibility crate has no mirror camera, so a mirror mode builds the reflected eye and the plane near plane in the test. Bake gap: `walk_measurement` completed-reads mode needs a mirror pose and a second walk per frame. Settle: the `[Settling] entry` line; mirror cells reach the lightmap settle set because it holds every block the pose draws (`boot_sequence.md` §1).
- **Rivals.** Planar blended over a full SSR layer on the selected plane: two mirroring systems on one surface; rejected by the owner. A step-budgeted walk whose frustum fallback is the mirror's normal path: matched the unlimited image at one pose, but makes a fallback ordinary and widens never-refused demand; rejected. Full-screen seeds, as the spike starts them: walk and draw cells no mirror sample reaches; rejected for the crop. Refusable mirror demand (a band or optional tier under the pool cap): removes the bake-gap risk and the lever ladder, but pops reflections in at grazing views, where they are strongest; rejected under the never-refused visible-demand rule (`plans/done/spatial-residency--lightmap-cell-blocks/`).
- **Billboards and particles** are camera-facing. Check where their facing basis enters the draw; the mirror pass needs it from the mirror camera.
- **Area floor.** Measure on the plane's total up-facing area, not per face. Puddle-scale and lake-scale fixtures bracket the value.
- **Split first.** `frame_loop/mod.rs` and `debug_ui/mod.rs` are past 800 lines; extend through new sibling files as the spike did.
- **Ghost entities** has landed (`rendering_pipeline.md` §7.9). Keep its pass out of the mirror and record the ghost pass's order relative to the mirror and fluid passes in the plan.
- `research.md` holds the measurements, commitment map, IP map and ordering pins behind these decisions.

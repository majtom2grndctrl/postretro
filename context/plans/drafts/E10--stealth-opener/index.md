# E10--stealth-opener

Brief · draft, not yet validated · Epic 10 · reads: `context/lib/entity_model.md` §7c, §9 · `context/lib/scripting.md` §1, §11 · `drafts/E10--ai-tick-scheduling/` (its notice meter) · read at 909a0bdbc

**Sequence:** after `E10--ai-tick-scheduling`, whose per-candidate, per-player notice meter for NPCs without a target this extends. Design it with, or after, a general hit-time damage modifier shared with special damage types and critical hits.

## Problem
Owner-requested capability for an anticipated need, split out of `E10--ai-tick-scheduling` by a direction review (2026-11-18): a player's opening shot on an NPC not yet aware of them can deal more, and a player sneaking past an NPC fighting someone else can catch it. Four detail-review rounds placed these rules in the scheduling brief, where each round found new Blockers where the meter's target changes meet the damage paths. The rulings below were made there and are kept as this brief's starting decisions; its direction is not yet validated.

## Decisions (owner rulings carried from E10, not yet validated as a set)
- **A player's opening shot on an unaware NPC scales by their stealth multiplier.** `components.stealth.attackMultiplier` is an optional field on player entity types (those with `movement`), a number or fluent value (default 1), so player level or a power-up can drive it; any other entity type that sets it is refused at load, as is a literal outside 0–64 (owner).
  - *Who qualifies.* A shot is a stealth shot when its first hit on an NPC lands while that NPC's candidacy admits the pawn, it is not targeting the pawn, and its meter for the pawn is below 1, so a player can catch an NPC fighting someone else; allies and neutrals never qualify. Candidacy here is the NPC's own record: each meter entry keeps the verdict from the NPC's latest check, a dead candidate's verdict stands until a revive or respawn, and only a firer with no entry is judged live at the hit. So a rocket in flight keeps its opener when its firer dies (owner).
  - *Which hits.* Every hit of a stealth shot on that NPC, each pellet and a projectile's splash, on any tick until the shot can land no more, is multiplied, stacking with hit-zone multipliers; the first hit fills the meter, so the player's next shot is normal. Plan the architecture around a future barrel or grenade whose explosion carries the shot that set it off (owner).
  - *Value.* The value evaluated at the start of the landing tick, clamped to 0–64; a non-finite result, a missing store read included, counts as 1, warned once per player until it is finite again; a firer who has left takes 1. At 0 the hit only alerts: it fills the meter but records no damage and wakes no tier (owner).
- **Meters on NPCs that already have a target.** An NPC fighting one player keeps filling its other candidates' meters over `awareness.engagedFillMs` (default `fillMs`), so it slowly notices a second player in view; these meters gate only stealth shots and `brain.awareness`, and target switching keeps today's ranking, a separate aggro rule (owner). Costs the early stop `perf-ai-tick-cost` added for NPCs with a target and amends `entity_model.md` §7c's candidacy order; state both as deliberate trades.
- **A resting NPC's held selection.** An NPC's target is its last check's selection, whether or not its activity engages, and survives one check that fails to see it, lapsing on the second in a row (owner).
- **Acquiring by any path sets the meter to 1**, an instant re-acquire and a ranking switch included, so a former target is not a stealth target until it has broken sight long enough to drain (owner).
- **Non-goals:** stealth-hit feedback (hit marker, sound, VFX): clients see only the larger damage; special damage types and critical hits are a later brief (owner).

## Open questions
- **blocks build:** a general hit-time damage modifier shared with critical hits and special damage types — design it first, or with this brief, so the multiplier is one consumer rather than a stealth-only path in three damage sites (direction review).
- **blocks build:** candidacy gating damage: authors wrote candidacy for whom to engage; this brief makes it decide damage. Confirm, or choose a separate stealth-eligibility predicate.
- Light, stance, movement and distance modifiers, investigate states and client HUD replication of the meter: in scope here or a further brief.

## Acceptance (carried from E10; revalidate)
- [ ] An NPC fighting player A with player B in view and line of sight fills B's meter over `engagedFillMs`, and with it at 1500 notices B no earlier than 1.5 s after the first check that saw them; B's shots are stealth shots only until then. B more than 1 m nearer than A is switched to at once by today's ranking, and that switch sets B's meter to 1.
- [ ] On the decide whose check raises a player's meter from 0.95 to 1, that player's projectile landing that tick deals multiplied damage and their host hitscan landing that tick deals normal damage; a remote client's shot fired before the check and authorized after it deals normal damage (P42).
- [ ] A player with `stealth.attackMultiplier` 2 whose meter on an NPC reads 0.9 deals double with every hit of one shot: each of an eight-pellet blast's pellets, a hitscan with the hit zone's multiplier applied too (melee weapons included), and a rocket's splash; their next shot deals normal damage. The same holds against an NPC fighting another player; a shot on an NPC targeting this player, or whose meter for them is 1 while another player's meter on it reads 0.2, and a shot on an allied or neutral NPC, deal normal damage (P40).
- [ ] A multiplier evaluating to 100 deals 64× and a literal 100 is refused at load; a non-finite result deals normal damage and warns once until it is finite again; a prop or NPC archetype that sets `stealth.attackMultiplier` is refused at load by name (P40).
- [ ] A resting NPC whose activity does not engage, hit between checks by the player its last check selected, takes normal damage; it keeps that selection through one check that fails to see the player, and loses it on the second in a row (P48).
- [ ] A resting NPC whose selection lapses on a check with a teammate in view acquires the teammate on that check; with none in view, the selected player reappearing on the following check is acquired on the first check at least `fillMs` after it, and on no earlier one (P49).
- [ ] A player an NPC acquired at once after its last target fell, or by a ranking switch, and who then loses the NPC's target to a nearer player while still in view, deals normal damage to it; after breaking sight long enough for the meter to drain below 1, their next shot is a stealth shot (P52).
- [ ] A player whose rocket is in flight when they die, against an NPC whose candidacy rejects dead pawns and whose last check admitted them, deals stealth damage; after an assist revive the NPC's next check judges them live again (P40).
- [ ] A multiplier driven by a store value changed this tick applies from the next tick; a projectile fired with the multiplier at 3 that lands after it falls to 1 deals normal damage; trap and contextless script damage, and a projectile whose firer has left the game, take no multiplier (P40).
- [ ] A player with `stealth.attackMultiplier` 2 and a meter of 0.9 on an NPC deals double with both hits of one `ShotId` landing on consecutive ticks, the NPC's check acquiring them between; a three-round burst deals double with its first round only; one shotgun blast deals double on an unaware NPC and normal damage on a second NPC targeting that player; two players whose shots share a `ShotId` value are separate shots; a hit whose shot carries no `ShotId` deals double and the next normal; a shot whose first hit is normal stays normal on a later tick after the NPC moves its target off the firer (P45).
- [ ] After a hitscan stealth shot's tick and after a rocket's splash tick, the NPC holds no record of that shot (counted under test); four players firing stealth shots at one NPC for 60 s leave no records behind once their projectiles are gone, and a firer who leaves leaves none (P51).
- [ ] Against an archetype whose candidacy rejects pawns beyond 40 m, a player with `stealth.attackMultiplier` 2 who reached a dormant NPC since its last check deals double from 20 m and fills the meter, and deals normal damage from 60 m and leaves the meter unchanged (P40).
- [ ] A non-finite or negative `engagedFillMs` rejects at load and 0 loads; with it omitted and `fillMs` 300, an NPC fighting one player ends a second player's stealth window on the first check at least 0.3 s after the first check that saw them (P50).
- [ ] An NPC fighting player A beyond its tactical range, with `engagedFillMs` 1500, ends a second player B's stealth window on the first check at least 1.5 s after the first check that saw B, checks falling every third decide; with `fillMs` 300, B's credit for the interval ending at the check that acquires A is at 300 (P50).
- [ ] A rocket striking an unaware NPC with `stealth.attackMultiplier` 2 deals double splash, its zero-damage contact landing first; a multiplier of 0 leaves a perceived NPC's health and `brain.timeSinceDamageMs` unchanged and its `brain.awareness` at 1 on its next decide, and leaves a dormant NPC dormant until its next dormant check, where it acquires the firer if they pass candidacy and line of sight (P46).

## Path
- Stealth multiplier: the `ShotId` already rides projectiles (`projectile_stage.rs` `source_shot`) and weapon commands; `ShotId` is unique per firer, so key each NPC's per-shot decision records, normal or stealth, by attacker and `ShotId` and keep them until the shot can land no more hits; `emit_splash_damage` and the impact function take the `ShotId` as a new parameter. Keep each meter entry's candidacy verdict beside its value; the damage path reads it, and needs the candidate filter (`pub(crate)` in `brain_programs.rs`) only for a firer with no entry. Fill the meter before `HealthComponent::apply_damage_with_context`'s zero-damage gate, so a ×0 hit still alerts. Store the last check's selection in a new brain field: `acquired_target` is cleared for non-engaging paths (`compute.rs`). Apply it beside the hit-zone multiplier in `weapon_stage::impact::apply_weapon_impact_damage_with_source`, and on the splash and projectile paths (`splash.rs`, `projectile_stage.rs`); evaluate it at tick start with `defense_parameters::DefenseParameters` as the precedent, reading the pawn's own state, global store values and its seat's per-player values.
- Rival not yet weighed: a general per-hit damage modifier expression (on the attacker, reading target facts such as awareness) instead of a stealth-specific field and rules.

# E10--ai-tick-scheduling

Brief · resumable · Epic 10 · reads: `context/lib/entity_model.md` §3, §5, §6, §7c · `context/lib/scripting.md` §11 · `context/lib/movement.md` §6 · `context/lib/rendering_pipeline.md` §Cluster SH residency, §12 · `context/lib/networking.md` · `context/lib/testing_guide.md` §Resource bounds · `context/research/cell-visibility-substrate.md` · `drafts/perf-ai-tick-cost/` · `spike-visible-rates.md` · read at efdab38d6

**Sequence:** after `perf-ai-tick-cost` (exact cost cuts, same target on the same tick). Merges the roadmap's *Decision cadence* and *Arena relevance* items; *Parallel replans* follows.

## Problem
Owner-requested capability for an anticipated need (roadmap Epic 10, AI at scale): content today does not hit the limit. Every NPC decides every tick, and every NPC runs its whole tick at 60 Hz whether or not any player can perceive it. Most NPC timing counts ticks rather than time, so no part of an NPC can run slower without changing behavior. A spike on a four-arena map (`spike-visible-rates.md`), measured before `perf-ai-tick-cost`, put deciding at about 96% of an engaged NPC's AI tick and NPCs no player can perceive at the larger share of the tick on maps of 500 to 1,000 placed. When done, NPC timing runs on time, NPCs decide only as often as players can notice, NPCs nobody perceives cost little, and a designer places Quake/Doom-class encounters at minimum, Serious Sam class as the attempted target, with no engine tuning. The notice meter that replaces instant acquisition also gives authors a stealth opening: a player's first hit on an NPC not yet aware of them can deal more (owner).

## Decisions
- **NPC timing runs on time, not tick counts.** Every tick-counted NPC window becomes a duration (inventory in `research.md`). An NPC keeps its attack turns, and any queued grant, across ticks it does not run. Behavior at 60 Hz is unchanged except where a decision here changes it.
- **Each part of an NPC runs at its tier's rate.** One per-NPC choice of tier, made on the host's fixed tick before the AI stage; it never reads the renderer, the host camera or frame timing.

  | Tier | Who | Target acquisition | Decide: guards, move and action selectors, combat-slot choice | Acting: steering along the chosen route or slot, separation, route following, clip time and moments, attacks, facing and aim, hit reactions |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Engaged | has a target, perceived or not | 15 Hz | 15 Hz | every 60 Hz tick |
  | Idle | perceived or `alwaysActive`, no target | 15 Hz, through the notice meter | 15 Hz | every 60 Hz tick |
  | Dormant | unperceived, no target, not `alwaysActive` | every 2 s, through the notice meter | every 2 s | whole tick at 2 Hz |

  - Every rate is a constant expected to be retuned on better test maps. Decide rates set reaction feel as well as cost, so a retune for cost is also a feel change. Per-NPC decide rates are not authored: one fast archetype in a horde would break the staggered pools; how fast an NPC notices is authored through the notice meter instead.
  - **Tactical range.** An engaged NPC whose target's XZ distance is beyond its `tacticalRange` acquires on every third decide, and under every engaged motion verb (chase, strafe, reposition) routes toward its target instead of choosing a combat slot. It chooses a slot once its target is 10 m inside the range, so the line does not flap. A slot around a target that far away is stale before the NPC arrives, and choosing one costs a long route search per candidate slot. `tacticalRange` is an optional behavior-root field in metres, default 100, authored per archetype (owner); the engine keeps the behavior beyond it, the author moves the line. A value below the archetype's largest attack standoff plus 10 m is raised to that and warned at load and on every hot reload that commits it, so a reload never fails over it (owner).
  - Decides and dormant steps are staggered from each NPC's identity (map placement, or spawner and spawn count), never spawn order or frame split, so assignment replays identically in co-op and replays. Spawns with no map identity (synthetic placements, spawners without an ordinal) keep the registry index, replay-stable only under a fixed spawn order (owner). Balance across phases is statistical, not exact.
  - Amends `entity_model.md` §7c: guards, move and action selectors and combat-slot choice evaluate on decide ticks, engine-gated rows (a hold's release, a release's exit, `animationDone`) included, so those land up to one decide interval later than at 60 Hz (owner: about 67 ms at 15 Hz, imperceptible). A strafer re-aims each decide. A queued attack grant opens its row at the NPC's first decide after the grant. A dormant NPC's 2 Hz step resolves each moment inside it once.
- **An NPC without a target notices through a meter.** Each NPC keeps an awareness value from 0 to 1 per candidate it could acquire, for the nearest few (at most 8, as the attacker ledger). On each acquisition check, a candidate that passes candidacy and line of sight on this check and the previous one gains the time between them over `notice.fillMs`; a first sighting gains nothing, so a reveal, a teleport into view or a waking arena still gives the slow first notice (owner). One that fails drains by the time since the previous check over `notice.drainMs`. The NPC acquires the first candidate to reach 1, by today's ranking among those that do. In co-op, each player has their own meter, so a hidden player is not spotted because a teammate was. A hit from a candidate sets that candidate's meter to 1, so the NPC acquires them once they pass candidacy and line of sight; damage with no attacker changes no meter (owner). An NPC with a target ranks and switches targets as today, without meters. One that had a target on its previous check acquires any candidate passing candidacy and line of sight at once; with none, its meters start from zero, so a player out of sight when the fight ends is noticed only through a new fill (owner). `notice` is an optional behavior-root block; `fillMs` defaults to 1000 (the slow first notice the owner wanted) and `drainMs` to 2000; at 0, a meter fills on the second sighted check or empties on the first failed one. Guards read the leading value as `brain.awareness`.
- **`alwaysActive` keeps an NPC out of the dormant tier.** An optional behavior-root boolean, also settable on a placed NPC with the bare map key `always_active` (0 or 1, anything else refused by name at load), set-piece state read at spawn like `enabled_on_spawn`; the placement wins, and an archetype reload moves only NPCs placed without the key. NPCs a spawner releases take the archetype's value. Such an NPC runs as idle or engaged whether or not it is perceived, so an unseen escort, race or scripted walker reaches triggers on time. It costs full rate for those NPCs only.
- **A player's hit on an unaware NPC scales by their stealth multiplier.** `stealth.attackMultiplier` is an optional entity-type field, a number or fluent value (default 1), so player level or a power-up can drive it. When a hit from a pawn lands on an NPC that has no target and whose meter for that pawn is below 1, its damage is multiplied by the pawn's value, stacking with hit-zone multipliers, before the hit fills the meter, so only the opening hit gains it. It covers all of the pawn's damage, hitscan, melee, projectiles and splash, with awareness and the multiplier both read when the hit lands: the multiplier as evaluated at the start of the landing tick, as defense-layer values are. Damage with no live pawn behind it takes no multiplier. Later stealth work adds player-side light, stance and noise to the same `stealth` block (owner).
- **Visible NPCs keep acting at 60 Hz.** A lower acting rate measured too small a saving for its cost (`spike-visible-rates.md`). Presentation, hit tests, snapshots and the client interpolation delay are unchanged.
- **Mover carry and pushes reach a dormant NPC every tick.** Carry and displacement run each tick for a dormant NPC, so it stays planted on a moving lift or platform. Amends `movement.md` §6's single call order and its source-text gate for dormant NPCs.
- **Perceivable means inside some player's id-51 set.** The engine locates the cell of every player pawn in play or down, host and clients alike, and unions the `CellResidencySet` (id 51) cells reachable from those cells within an AI lead. The lead has an engine default the executor picks and reports; a map overrides it with the worldspawn key `ai_wake_lead` in metres, carried to runtime in a level-file field as `initialGravity` is (no underscore: underscore keys are compiler-only); 0 means the players' cells and their visible sets alone, and a negative or non-finite value fails the build by name. An NPC is perceivable when its cell is in that union. The union is AI policy. The step under it, player pawn → cell → id-51 set, is engine-owned, keyed per client, and recorded as the intended input for network relevance and combat VFX culling. A gap that shows is fixed by refining id 51, never by a PVS bake (owner).
  - Owner choice over a new sightline bake. id 51 is sampled, so a cell it misses can still be drawn; an NPC there that notices a player through its meter moves up then. One that cannot acquire the player (another faction, aggro gate closed, candidacy rejects) may step at 2 Hz in view; accepted as rare.
  - id 51 is streaming-owned; the AI lead is its own constant, never streaming's runtime L, so streaming retuning never moves AI detail. A lead, default or authored, above id 51's baked maximum is capped to it, with a warning for an authored one. Doors are not applied: closed doors only widen the set, the safe direction.
  - Armed ghost links couple their cells, per `networking.md` "Ghosts constrain future relevance": a perceived cell's linked cells join the union.
  - A level with no id 51, or a tick with no pawn in play or down, runs every NPC perceivable.
  - Monster closets get no special case. A closet NPC stays dormant until it enters the play field; waking at the reveal or after a teleport reads as fair disorientation (owner).
- **Tier changes.** An NPC moves up on the tick it becomes perceivable or gains a target. Damage and an opened aggro gate move it up on the tick they land when that is before the AI stage, otherwise on the next tick, and reach its brain at its next run. Moving up credits the time it has not run to its timers; it moves one ordinary step, so it never jumps. Moving down waits a short hold so an NPC at a set's edge does not flap. A dead NPC leaves the tiers; a revived one rejoins on its revive tick by the usual rules, with no credit for time dead.
- **The dormant tier never skips simulation.** Its whole tick runs with its step scaled. Diverges from `cell-visibility-substrate.md` "must NOT cull authoritative simulation" in letter, not intent: amended to permit a coarser authoritative tick and keep forbidding a skipped one.
- **The replan budget scales with demand.** Each tick's budget is `max(4, ⌈owed / fair wait⌉)`, owed counting NPCs in any replan tier, fair wait about 100 ms. Route-query cost per tick grows with the crowd.
- **Replan tiers outrank detail level.** The shared per-tick replan budget keeps its tier order (blocked route, then drift, then staleness) and its blocked-route wait bound for every NPC; within a replan tier, a paced NPC (a leap in flight) goes first, as today, then higher NPC tiers.
- **Perception decides the tier, never targets.** Keeps `perf-ai-tick-cost`'s rejection of a visibility broad phase inside target selection.
- **NPC is the umbrella term** for brain-bearing actors that are not player-controlled, enemies and allies alike (owner). This brief's rules apply to every NPC; the term lands in `entity_model.md`.
- **Docs amended at promotion:** `scripting.md` §11, `entity_model.md` §3, §5, §6 (an NPC's cell is re-located on every move, wherever it is cached), §7c (guards, selectors, slot choice and newly entered activities on decide ticks; strafe re-aiming each decide; the think stride becoming acquisition and decide pools plus `tacticalRange`; dormant steps for Pace, activity speed, release exit and "Clip time is game time"), `movement.md` §6, the substrate doc's no-cull rule and a note that the per-player id-51 step exists for its consumers, the roadmap's E15 Phase 4 and combat VFX culling entries pointing at that step without settling their miss tolerance; the no-PVS ground rule lands in `context/lib/`. `build_pipeline.md`'s worldspawn row and load-time declarations for `ai_wake_lead`. Human-facing: `tacticalRange`, `notice`, `alwaysActive`, `stealth.attackMultiplier` and `brain.awareness` in the SDK types, `docs/scripting-reference.md` and the guard doc comments in `sdk/lib/brain.ts` and `brain.luau`; `always_active` and `ai_wake_lead` in `docs/level_design.md` and `sdk/TrenchBroom/postretro.fgd`.
- **Non-goals:**
  - Lower acting rates for perceived or engaged NPCs, and the host interpolation, drawn-pose hits and per-step snapshot wire they need: measured not worth it (`spike-visible-rates.md`); the design is kept in `research.md`.
  - Network relevance and combat VFX culling: later consumers of the per-player step, each deciding in its own brief whether id 51's sampling misses are tolerable for it (a miss here costs an NPC one decide; a missed entity in network relevance is never sent).
  - Projectiles: a projectile ticks at 60 Hz whoever fired it; tiers never step or steer projectiles.
  - A sightline PVS bake, a tighter id 46, or door-aware relevance: the owner's no-PVS ground rule under Perceivable; runtime value comes from metadata the bake already produces, even after its source such as the BSP is dropped.
  - Stealth beyond the meter and the multiplier: light, stance, movement and distance modifiers, investigate states and client HUD replication of the meter belong to a later stealth brief that extends this shape (owner).
  - A hearing model or alert propagation between NPCs: NPCs wake as they do today, through damage, targets and the aggro gate.
  - Route-query cost inside the tactical range: `perf-ai-tick-cost` (exact cuts). Long-range routing over a coarser graph for very large maps: a later roadmap item.

### Scripting surface
```ts
behavior: {
  initial: "idle",
  moveSpeed: 3,
  engagementRadius: 4,
  tacticalRange: 60,      // optional, metres, default 100: beyond it the NPC heads for its target
  notice: { fillMs: 300, drainMs: 4000 },  // optional: a sharp-eyed sentry that stays suspicious
  alwaysActive: false,    // optional: true keeps it simulated at full rate when unseen
  activities: { /* ... */ },
  transitions: { /* ... */ },
},
```
```ts
// a guard reading the meter: turn to look while half aware, before the NPC acquires
{ to: "lookAround", when: brain.awareness.ge(0.5) }

// on the player's entity type: opening hits on unaware NPCs grow with the player's level (a per-player store)
stealth: { attackMultiplier: read(progress.level).times(0.5).plus(1.5) },
```
Luau mirrors each block field for field (`tacticalRange = 60`, `notice = { fillMs = 300, drainMs = 4000 }`, `brain.awareness:ge(0.5)`).
Map keys: `always_active` (0/1) on an NPC placement; `ai_wake_lead` (metres) on `worldspawn`.

Type docs: `notice`: "Optional. How fast an NPC with no target notices each candidate: awareness fills over `fillMs` (default 1000) while it sees them and they pass its candidacy, and drains over `drainMs` (default 2000) otherwise; full awareness targets them. Each ≥ 0." `brain.awareness`: "Highest awareness of any candidate, 0 to 1; 1 once it has a target." `alwaysActive`: "Optional. True keeps this NPC at full rate while no player perceives it. Default false." `stealth.attackMultiplier`: "Optional damage multiplier for this entity's hits on an NPC not yet aware of it. Default 1." `tacticalRange`: "Optional metres to the target inside which an engaged NPC fights tactically; beyond it the NPC heads for its target. Finite, > 0, default 100; raised with a warning to the largest attack standoff plus 10 m."

## Acceptance

### Automated
Timing on time:
- [ ] Each converted window expires on the first step at or past its duration at 60 and 2 Hz, and never on an earlier step (P18).
- [ ] While an NPC holding a turn skips its AI, its turn still counts against its pool on every skipped tick: no other NPC is granted that capacity and no refresh starts until the holder releases it; a turn that ends while it is not running is released on its next run, and a queued grant made while it is not running opens on its next run (P1).
- [ ] A queued attack granted to an engaged NPC opens its row at that NPC's next decide, for every decide phase; no turn stays held with its row closed, and other NPCs waiting on that pool are granted no later than one decide interval after their 60 Hz tick, and none starves (P27).
- [ ] A perceived idle NPC on a patrol route, or returning to its anchor, leaves each reached point on the tick it arrives, as at 60 Hz: it never stands at a point waiting for its next decide.
- [ ] An idle NPC with `fillMs` 1000 that sees a player continuously acquires them on the first check at least 1 s after the first check that saw them, and on no earlier one; one that loses sight 0.6 s after that check and regains it 0.5 s later, with `drainMs` 2000, acquires on the first check at least 0.65 s after the first check that sees them again (0.6 filled, 0.25 drained), and on no earlier one; a player teleporting into view of an idle or dormant NPC is noticed no sooner than `fillMs` after the first check that sees them; a non-finite or negative `fillMs` or `drainMs` rejects at load (P31).
- [ ] With two players, an NPC that sees only one fills only that player's meter; the other player, stepping into view later, starts from zero (P31).
- [ ] An idle NPC hit by a player in view acquires them on its next decide, whatever its meter; hit from out of sight, it acquires them on the first decide they pass candidacy and line of sight, unless the meter has drained first; trap and contextless script damage changes no meter. An NPC with a target switches targets by today's ranking without a meter.
- [ ] A player hit with `stealth.attackMultiplier` 2 on an NPC with no target and that player's meter at 0.9 deals double, the hit zone's multiplier applied too, for hitscan, melee, a projectile and splash; the next hit deals normal damage; a hit on an NPC with a target, or whose meter for that player is 1, deals normal damage, even while another player's meter is low (P40).
- [ ] A multiplier driven by a store value changed this tick applies from the next tick; a projectile fired with the multiplier at 3 that lands after it falls to 1 deals normal damage; trap and contextless script damage, and a projectile whose firer has left, take no multiplier (P40).
- [ ] An NPC whose target dies acquires another player in view on its next decide; with none in view, a player stepping into view later is noticed only after a full `fillMs`.
- [ ] `brain.awareness` reads the highest meter, 0 to 1, and 1 while the NPC has a target; a guard on `brain.awareness.ge(0.5)` fires before acquisition when sight lasts past half of `fillMs`.
- [ ] An NPC whose eight meters belong to hostile NPCs that then die and despawn still notices a player who steps into view, in the same time as with empty meters (P32).
- [ ] An NPC with nine nearer hostile NPCs out of sight behind a wall notices a player in view at the same pace as with none; with nine passing candidates, the farthest is the one left unmetered (P33).
- [ ] A candidate evicted from an NPC's meters with awareness 0.8, re-admitted on a later check, starts from zero; a hot reload that changes `fillMs` from 1000 to 500 when a meter reads 0.4 keeps 0.4 and then rises at the new rate from the next check (P31).
- [ ] A guard on `brain.awareness.ge(0.5)` fires on the same decide whose check first raises the meter to 0.5 or above, not the decide after (P35).
- [ ] Two players whose meters reach 1 on one check: the NPC acquires the nearer, and the farther one's meter stays at 1 (P36).
- [ ] `fillMs` 0 loads, and an NPC with it notices on the same check as one with `fillMs` 30; `drainMs` 0 empties a meter on the first check that fails (P37).
- [ ] An `alwaysActive` NPC, set on the archetype or by `always_active 1` on its placement, never enters the dormant tier; `always_active 0` on a placement overrides an archetype's true; `always_active 2` is refused by name at load; a spawner's NPCs take the archetype's value.
- [ ] A hot reload that sets `alwaysActive` true moves that archetype's dormant NPCs up on the next tick, timers credited once; one that sets it false moves unseen ones down only after the hold; NPCs placed with `always_active 0` stay dormant (P38).
- [ ] An unseen `alwaysActive` NPC already on a patrol route crosses a trigger volume on the same tick as when perceived; one whose walk starts on a guard crosses within one decide interval of its 60 Hz tick (P39).
- [ ] A map with `ai_wake_lead` uses it as the AI lead, and a map without it the engine default; neither ever exceeds the loaded level's id-51 maximum, and an authored value above it is capped with a warning; 0 runs with the players' cells and visible sets alone; a negative or non-finite value fails the build by name.
- [ ] An NPC that leaves the AI set (held, quiescent or despawning) still returns holding nothing, requesting nothing, with no wait clock; only a scheduler skip keeps its turns (P2).

Tiers and rates:
- [ ] Each tier runs each part of an NPC at exactly its table rate: a perceived idle NPC checks acquisition and runs its guards, including a patrol pause, a clip's `animationDone` exit and a release exit, at 15 Hz; a dormant NPC checks acquisition and decides on every fourth dormant run and on no other tick (P4).
- [ ] An allied NPC runs the same tiers and rates as an enemy.
- [ ] With many NPCs, every staggered acquisition, decide and dormant step is identical across frame splits, reversed spawn order (for NPCs with a map identity), and host versus replay.
- [ ] A reposition entered beyond its tactical range flanks within its arc once it chooses slots (P30).
- [ ] On the stress fixture, including NPCs from two spawners counting from zero, placements whose ordinals step by 4, and interleaved spawns, the busiest decide or dormant phase carries at most the per-phase mean plus 2√mean + 1, and no phase is empty at 64 NPCs or more (P15).
- [ ] Target acquisition, counted at the candidate scan, runs only on decide ticks, and `@brain.acquisitionDue` is true on each of them; an engaged NPC beyond its tactical range acquires on every third, for every spawn tick relative to its phase (P5).
- [ ] An engaged NPC beyond its tactical range claims no combat slot and routes toward its target under chase, strafe and reposition alike; none stands still for want of a slot. Its target coming 10 m inside the range, it chooses a slot at its next decide; a target stepping just past the range and back does not drop a held slot (P28). With `tacticalRange` omitted the line is at 100 m; authored at 60, at 60 m.
- [ ] `tacticalRange` rejects at load a non-finite or non-positive value, as other behavior fields do. A value below the largest attack standoff plus 10 m (30 m with a 45 m attack) runs at 55 m and warns, at load and on each hot reload that commits it; a reload that changes the value moves the line for live NPCs from their next decide, without dropping a held slot inside both lines. The Scripting surface example lowers identically in TypeScript and Luau. An NPC with a 5 m and a 120 m attack and no `tacticalRange` runs its line at 130 m, with the warning, whichever attack is selected.
- [ ] Measured from its entry tick, the clip-timed jab, the brute's combo (three jabs 320 ms apart after a 2,000 ms hold) and a leap land no earlier than their 60 Hz ticks and at most one decide interval later, for every decide phase and frame split; clip moments themselves still resolve on the tick they fall.
- [ ] A clip moment inside a dormant step resolves once, on that step, with its gates evaluated then; none is skipped or fired twice, except a repeating release whose passes inside one step whiff as they do inside one tick today.
- [ ] A dormant NPC riding a lift or pushed by a door moves with it every tick, carried exactly once on a tick it also steps: carried, pushed or blocking, never embedded or dropped through (P29).
- [ ] Tier assignment is identical on a headless host and on a listen host whose camera looks elsewhere.
- [ ] A dormant NPC wandering, patrolling or falling never passes through a wall, floor or ledge it would stop at under 60 Hz.
- [ ] A knocked-back dormant NPC travels the same distance at 2 Hz as at 60 Hz, within one step's travel, for every authored drag.
- [ ] Hit tests, host presentation, snapshots and the client interpolation delay for perceived NPCs are unchanged: the existing networking, hit-zone and walk-rate loopback suites pass unchanged.

Perception and tier changes:
- [ ] An NPC in a cell inside any player's id-51 set at the AI lead is perceived; one outside every player's set and with no target is dormant. Two players in different arenas each keep their own arena's NPCs perceived; a third, empty arena stays dormant.
- [ ] An NPC moves up on the same tick it becomes perceivable or gains a target, including mid step; an aggro gate opened by a trigger-bound command moves it up that tick, and one opened by a named reaction moves it up for the next tick's AI stage.
- [ ] Damage that lands before the AI stage (projectile impacts, remote hits ready at the input boundary) moves an NPC up that tick; damage during or after it (another NPC's attack, host hitscan, trap shots, remote hits awaiting that tick's fire authorization) moves it up for the next tick's AI stage (P6).
- [ ] An NPC that moves up part way through a dormant step moves exactly one ordinary step, and one second later its weapon resources, cooldowns, activity time, damage recency and attacker ledger read the same as an NPC that ran at 60 Hz throughout: no quantity is credited twice (P3, P11).
- [ ] A dormant NPC that gains a target on its own dormant run moves exactly one ordinary step in that tick's steering, and from the next tick runs engaged (P21).
- [ ] A perceived idle NPC whose aggro gate opens acts on it at its next decide and runs engaged from then on.
- [ ] An NPC moves down only after the hold, and one oscillating across a set edge does not flap; an unperceived NPC with a retained target stays engaged and goes dormant only after losing its target and the hold.
- [ ] A dead NPC leaves the tiers; a revived one rejoins on its revive tick, and its timers are not credited for time dead.
- [ ] A player who teleports or respawns into a dormant arena on a tick has that arena's NPCs perceived on the same tick's AI stage (P7).
- [ ] A dormant NPC at the fastest shipped `moveSpeed` walking toward a player is perceived while still in the AI lead; it is never first seen already inside a cell visible from a player's cell (P8).
- [ ] An NPC spawned, re-using a freed slot, or moved by a script, mover or knockback is classified from its new cell on the next tick, and inherits no cell, hold or phase from a slot's previous occupant (P9).
- [ ] An unperceived hostile NPC whose candidacy admits a player, with line of sight to that player from outside the player's set, notices the player through its meter on its dormant checks (with default `fillMs`, on the second that sees them), then moves up (P20).
- [ ] Cells linked by an armed ghost link and teleporter arrival cells count as perceived; a disarmed ghost link does not couple.
- [ ] Pawns of seats in play or down count as perceivers, whatever their health or life state; loading and reconnecting seats have no pawn and do not; a tick with no counting pawn runs every NPC perceived (P17); a level loaded without id 51 runs every NPC perceived on every tick.
- [ ] Changing streaming's runtime lead does not change which NPCs are dormant.
- [ ] A dormant NPC whose route a closed door blocks is served within the blocked-route wait bound even while higher-tier NPCs fill the budget with drift and staleness replans; within one replan tier, every paced request and then every higher-tier request is served first (P13).
- [ ] Replan budget, both sides: with 24 or fewer owed NPCs it is exactly 4; with 128 owed chasers it admits ⌈128 / 6⌉ = 22 per tick, the drift tier's fair wait is at most 6 ticks, and the gate tier's ⌈N / budget⌉ bound holds while gates flip.

Scale (reported per tier on `perf-ai-tick-cost`'s multi-arena row and the packed stress row, `sim_ai` and `sim_steer_agents` with its sub-stages; measured, not gated; every row reports per-tier cost per NPC as a fraction of today's 60 Hz NPC, and the worst tick as well as the mean):
- [ ] Quake/Doom class (about 64 engaged in view, about 500 placed across arenas) and Serious Sam class (about 128 engaged, about 1,000 placed): the fixed step never spirals. AI and steering are reported against `perf-ai-tick-cost`'s goals and the spike's estimates; a miss is planned as an addendum, not a failed landing.
- [ ] Idle-tier AI cost and line-of-sight tests per acquisition check, reported with 1 and with 8 hostile candidates in reach, against the same NPC with a target, on the multi-arena row.
- [ ] A player teleporting into the most populated dormant arena moves every NPC there up on one tick without spiralling the fixed step; that tick's cost is reported (P14).
- [ ] The perception union costs no per-tick allocation, and its cost per tick is reported against player and NPC counts.
- [ ] Route queries per tick and their cost under the scaled replan budget, reported at 64 and 128 chasers on both rows; on the multi-arena row with far-band NPCs at 500 and 1,000 placed and moving targets, their route queries per tick and the replan wait of in-arena engaged NPCs are reported. NPCs beyond their tactical range report AI cost per NPC against an engaged NPC's inside it.

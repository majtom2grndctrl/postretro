# occluder-first-partition

Brief · resumable · reads: `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Compiler pipeline, `context/lib/rendering_pipeline.md` §2, `context/lib/resource_management.md` §5.2 Exclusion · read at 0380ba710

## Problem

Raised by the owner from an observed defect on `occlusion-test.map`, with the stair platforms of `stress-warren-hallway-inspection` (which prompted `portal-walk-bounded-regions`) as a second case and an industry account as support: IW-era COD hand-placed its portals because too many runtime portals slowed it down. Measured offline and live (`research.md` §Measurements): the red pillars beyond the retaining wall cut the hall into floor-to-ceiling column cells that are portal-visible over the wall, so their floors and pillar tops draw while hidden, and the walk tests several times the portals the wall alone needs. The runtime walks portals every frame, so portal count is per-frame cost. Cause: every static brush feeds BSP splitting, and plane selection scores only spanning and balance, so small brushes' planes win high in the tree and cut open space into slivers. When this is done, prl-build sorts brushes by occlusion value with no author input. From behind a wall, what its top hides beyond it no longer draws, and small brushes (pillars, lamp posts, shelves, curbs) no longer cut a room into portal-visible columns. Maps compile to fewer portals and a cheaper walk, with no significant increase in geometry, effects, textures or probes processed per frame, and outdoor city scenes keep big buildings, decks and walls as occluders whether or not they seal.

## Decisions

- **Two tiers in one BSP.** Occluder brushes split first, so a large occluder's planes extend across open space; every other brush still splits, only beneath an occluder-tier leaf, so solidity, hidden-face culling, buried lights, collision, baked light and the BVH keep their inputs (`research.md` §Shape). A runtime cell is an occluder-tier leaf: convex, with portals only between cells, while its solid and exterior sub-leaves stay their own solid and exterior cells (`research.md` §Exterior sub-leaves). The change lives in the compiler's partition and portal stages: the walk's algorithm is unchanged, but its inputs, and so every visible set, change.
  - This diverges from `portal-walk-bounded-regions`' non-goal "Compiler detail brushes or cell merging": the bounded walk still pays for every sliver cell and its portals, so this brief pays the re-keying of cell-grouped bakes once in the compiler and adds no authoring surface (`research.md` §Divergence).
- **Occluder rule, split by axis.** A brush occludes when, judged from either of its broad faces (the face spanned by its two largest axes), each broad axis passes its test, and never when a broad axis is under 1.2 m. A face counts only when the open space in front of it is at least that 1.2 m floor deep. The values are named starting constants, tuned by measurement and reported with every measurement (`research.md` §Occluder rule).
  - A horizontal broad axis passes at 20 m, or at half the open horizontal span in front of the face, bounded by solid or by exterior void. A slab, such as a deck or platform, has two horizontal broad axes and is judged on this test alone.
  - A vertical broad axis passes when its top stands at least the see-over height above the floor in front of the face, starting at 2.5 m: eye height 1.5 m plus jump reach.
- **Clusters.** Touching brushes that are each exempt alone form a cluster, a brush that occludes alone never joins one, and a cluster is judged per near-planar section of its solid. A section occludes only when its bounding broad face passes the occluder rule and its solid covers most of that face, at a named, tunable coverage constant (`research.md` §Clusters).
- **No author key.** No `_occluder` key until measured misclassifications on real maps survive tuning, and never a per-map classifier constant (`research.md` §Classification precedents). Classification reads only canonical brush geometry, so it runs in the shared compiler stage, never the Quake adapter (`build_pipeline.md` §Source-format neutrality).
- **Occlusion value is the test, not sealing.** An exempt brush between two rooms may only over-include visibility. Automatic classification never makes the walk under-include or a sealed map leak, except through a crack near the portal-area cull, whose portals and leak status can change either way under the unchanged cull (pin R14).
- **Doors keep sealing.** Every doorway a closed door seals today stays sealed, and a door in a wall of exempt brushes seals its opening. Movers that seal no doorway (lift cars, platforms) add no cell boundary.
- **Stepped solids escalate through their hulls.** If the arena sweep shows riser or tread planes slicing the room, stacked and stepped solids occlude through their outer hull planes instead, added alongside clusters. It is built only then (`research.md` §Stacked and stepped solids).
- **Cells are size-capped.** Owner ruling after the Task 9 sweep: non-solid cap splitters, like q3map's blocksize, divide any occluder-tier leaf larger than a named cap, ranked after occluder and door planes. Without a cap, one interior cell holds everything exempt brushes used to cut apart, so it draws whole and its residency cannot divide (`research.md` §Cell size; `plan.md` §Block).
- **Streaming hints never change visibility.** Hints stay outside cell construction and never change cells, portals or visible sets, per `build_pipeline.md` §Entity resolution. A seam that matches no runtime portal fails the build by name (`research.md` §Streaming hints).
- **The baker is the fix.** No runtime occluder culling and no full PVS bake.
- **No wire-format change.** Every PRL section keeps its layout and version constant; the runtime already reads cell ids everywhere, and the locator's leaf children name cells many to one (`research.md` §Wire format).
- **Measure, don't gate.** Frame-cost, visibility-count and per-map geometry deltas are measured and reported, and the owner judges the Problem's per-frame guardrail on the Reported rows; correctness rows pass or fail.
- **The bake gap is the one gate.** The change is held to the bake-gap gate (`rendering_pipeline.md` §2) as a correctness row. Its unit is deferred lightmap texel area per frame, the deferred blocks' area, not block count (`research.md` §Bake gap and id-51 sampling).
- **A generated street map ships with this work** as the classifier's proving ground and first consumer: an elevated street over a promenade, a further deck above for opposite-direction traffic, both snaking around corners, with barriers that keep players off a full grid. Building facades are narrow strips around windows, to test clusters; the street holds both a continuous row of abutting buildings and a free-standing building, so the sweep shows how the horizontal tests treat each outdoors; lamp posts are TrenchBroom linked groups holding a light.
- **Non-goals:**
  - Fog raymarch and skinned-viewmodel GPU cost: an owner-carved follow-up (`research.md` §Related follow-ups).
  - Hint brushes and hand-placed portals: no visibility hint brush exists today (the FGD's hint brushes are SH streaming hints), and they come in only if automatic classification fails on measured maps.
  - Re-merging coplanar face fragments: the follow-up `face-fragment-remerge`, opened if the owner judges the per-map rise in face and lightmap-chart counts significant. Occluder planes crossing open space split the faces they pass, and any door or hull splitters add more, so both counts may rise.
  - The roadmap's author-defined Sector Graph. The runtime cell, portal and locator contracts stay, so it is not foreclosed.
  - A per-project setting for the classifier constants: a later option if a game's movement outreaches the see-over height.
  - Changing `MIN_PORTAL_AREA_M2`: the cull stays as it is on every portal set, so its effect on portals stays separable from the partition's in the measurements. Retiring it is the follow-up `portal-area-cut-retirement`; the lake-slot hidden sightline stays until then (`research.md` §`MIN_PORTAL_AREA_M2`).

## Path

- **Shape.** Seams: `select_splitter` and `build_recursive` in `partition/brush_bsp.rs`; `brush_contains_region` keeps deciding solidity; `partition::partition` takes bare `BrushVolume`s, all a geometric classifier needs. Classify, then at each node choose among occluder planes only, scored over occluder candidates with an area term; the node where none qualifies is an occluder-tier leaf, and splitting continues on the rest beneath it. Generate the runtime portal set at the occluder tier only, since the loader's `validate_portal_adjacency` rejects a portal with one cell on both sides. Rivals and the Quake 3 precedent: `research.md` §Rivals.
- **One leaf → cell chokepoint.** Every consumer in `research.md` §Leaf-id consumers reads through it, including the AlphaLights cell in `pack.rs`, `affinity_grid::reachable_leaves` and the `chunk_light_list_bake` flood, which seed a portal flood from a point-located leaf. Readers that only test solidity keep the full tree. The id-51 key hashes leaf hulls when teleporters exist; it hashes cell hulls after. `PORTAL_WALK_EPOCH` stays; id 51 re-samples, because the cells and portals it encodes change.
- **Doors and stepped solids.** Feed the closed-hull planes of each mover whose closed pose seals a doorway opening (`MapData::kinematic_movers`) into the occluder tier as non-solid splitters, so `kinematic_geometry::sealed_portal_ids_for_mover` finds portals its hull covers. Rival: promote the brushes framing such a mover into the occluder tier. Recognising such a mover before cells exist is an open question. If the stepped-solid escalation fires, the solid's outer hull planes take the same non-solid splitter path while its brushes stay below the tier; the arena map is `stress-warren-showcase.map` (`PRESETS["warren"]`), whose `emit_arena` builds its staircase with `emit_walk_stairs` from full-height touching step blocks.
- **Split first.** `pipeline.rs` is past ~800 lines: call-site edits only. `partition/brush_bsp.rs` and `portals.rs` are past it, mostly tests: move their test modules to sibling files in the first commit, and put the classifier in its own partition module. `partition/face_extract.rs`, `geometry.rs` and `cell_visibility_bake.rs` are past it too; keep their edits to call sites.
- **First slice.** The classifier and two-tier BSP alone, in a compiler test on the occlusion-test brush set: count occluder-tier leaves and portals, and locate points below and above the wall top beyond the wall. It falsifies the riskiest assumption, that occluder-first selection carries the wall-top plane across the hall before anything else cuts it, before any consumer remap.
- **Street map.** Generated like the stress maps by `tools/gen_stress_map.py` (`PRESETS` in `main`, `write_map`, `write_generated_data_script` for the `.generated.ts` sidecar worldspawn `data_script` names). `stress_zones.py` and `stress_environment.py` are the precedent for a preset in its own module; tests live in `tools/tests/test_gen_stress_map.py`; `stress-env-volumes.README.md` is the README precedent. Linked groups follow `campaign-test.map`: `func_group` with `_tb_type _tb_group`, `_tb_id` and `_tb_linked_group_id`, which parse keeps as `MapAssembly`.
- **At landing.** `build_pipeline.md` §Compiler pipeline steps 2 and 5 and `rendering_pipeline.md` §2's opening paragraph describe every brush plane splitting cells; restate them for the two tiers. Restate `rendering_pipeline.md` §2's bake-gap gate in the bake-gap Decision's unit. Restate "cell id = BSP leaf id" as many leaves to one cell in `build_pipeline.md` §PRL section IDs (Cells), `section_registry.rs`, level-format `cells.rs`, `geometry.rs` and `portals.rs` (whose portal endpoint fields keep leaf-era names, `front_leaf`/`back_leaf`), compiler `pack/spatial.rs`, and loader `prl.rs`.

## Open questions

- Span sampling, blocking set and broad axes — **delegated**: where across the face the span is taken, which brushes count as blocking, the two broad axes when extents tie, as on a cube, and which test a sloped broad axis takes. The classification and order-independence rows pin the outcome for all but sloped axes, which no row pins (`research.md` §Available space; pins R18, R28, R30, R31).
- Cluster coverage, touching, membership and sections — **delegated**. Recommendation: coverage starts at 50%, between the colonnade and the windowed facade; touching is the 1 mm CSG contact epsilon brush-side projection uses (`resource_management.md` §5.2 Contact). Still open: which member planes reach the occluder tier, so that a fixture touching a cluster adds no portal of its own while the facade still occludes, and how a cluster finds its near-planar sections (`research.md` §Clusters).
- How a mover is recognised as sealing a doorway before cells exist — **delegated**. `kinematic_mover` makes no door/lift distinction, and the only "does it seal" test runs after the BSP against portals the classification now shapes. Options: a pre-BSP test that the closed hull, with the static brushes it touches, closes an opening; or a two-pass build (`research.md` §Doors).
- Whether the id-51 eye-point lattice scales with cell size — **delegated**, decided by the measured id-51 misses on coarse cells (`research.md` §Bake gap and id-51 sampling).

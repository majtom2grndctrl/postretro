Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* Mutant clips from Mixamo, on the Mutant rig, each file named for its clip:
  idle (Mutant Breathing Idle), walk, run, punch, swipe, smash (Mutant Jump
  Attack), roar, hit_reaction (Standing React Large From Front), death
  (Mutant Dying), falling (Falling Idle). Built with:
    mixamo_to_gltf.py --library --base Mutant.fbx --yaw 180 --in-place smash hit_reaction \
      --trim smash_windup=smash:400 --trim swipe_windup=swipe:900 \
      --trim punch_windup=punch:467 \
      --trim smash_strike=smash:400- --trim swipe_strike=swipe:900- \
      --trim punch_strike=punch:467-
* --yaw 180 turns the rig to face +Z, the front AI facing aims, matching
  the bound Mutant model; root motion then runs the way the model faces.
* smash and hit_reaction are pinned in place: the jump attack lands 1.6 m
  forward and the hit reaction staggers 1 m back, and the
  body would snap back when either clip ended.
* smash_windup, swipe_windup and punch_windup are the attacks cut at their
  windup peak (the crouch before the leap; the lean back before the lunge;
  the fist drawn back high, 467 ms, frame 14, where it stops moving). Played
  without looping they hold that pose, for enemy stances.
* smash_strike, swipe_strike and punch_strike are the rest of each attack
  from its windup cut, starting on the windup's last pose: a stance's release
  plays one after its windup holds.

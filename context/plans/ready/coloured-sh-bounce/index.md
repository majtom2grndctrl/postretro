# coloured-sh-bounce

## Problem
Owner-raised, the second step of the owner's GI order: one-sample bounce (landed), then colour, then `surface-radiance-cache` (its `research.md` §Direction review). Basis: an observed look defect. Indirect light never takes surface colour, so a red wall spills no red. Cause: the SH bounce for ids 34 and 27 multiplies every hit by one grey albedo, 0.45, a value no measurement chose (`research.md` §Estimator today). TextureMips has since put each world texture's diffuse within the compiler's reach. A justified rider: entity shaders read SH indirect unscaled, an older defect the colour A/B depends on fixing. Done means probe bounce takes each hit surface's own diffuse colour, so the indirect light that world, entities, fog, fluids and reflection captures read carries it, and entities read it at the world's indirect scale. The owner's A/B approves the look and the overall brightness.

## Decisions
- **One mean per texture.** A hit's albedo is the linear-light mean of every full-resolution texel of its texture's diffuse, looked up by the hit face's texture index, and the same at any worker count. No UVs and no per-texel sampling: a probe's footprint spans many texels, and a texel lookup would add noise at probe scale. No downsampled level takes part (`research.md` §Diffuse chain).
- **Derive, don't store.** The shared material table derives each texture's stats every build from its sidecar's full-size level, rebaked or reused, and nothing beyond the sidecar persists. That level is the decoded source, so the mean is exact and bounce reads the texture the game ships. A derived value persists only once a measurement shows its cost (`research.md` §Derive, don't store).
- **Lifetime bound** (`development_guide.md` §1.4). Per-texture stats stay resident for the build, one fixed-size record per texture. Texel data streams one texture at a time while stats are computed. Lazily loaded footprint data keeps its existing lifetime.
- **Glow texels excluded.** A texel is left out of the mean only if it renders as glow: its `_e` RGB is non-zero and its material's emissive strength is above 0. A texture with no `_e`, with zero strength, or with every texel glowing takes the full average. Glow is emission, not reflectance (`resource_management.md` §4.5; `research.md` §Glow exclusion).
- **Grey fallback.** A face with no texture, a name with no source, or a texture with no diffuse slot bounces exactly today's 0.45 grey, unsnapped and unsaturated; never the reflection capture's magenta placeholder. A source that fails to read or decode fails the build, so a broken asset never bakes as grey.
- **Means snap to 8-bit sRGB.** Each mean encodes per channel to 8-bit sRGB, the precision authors paint in, before saturation. The bake's albedo is a pure function of the snapped values, so bytes always agree with the key (`research.md` §Snapping).
- **One bake-wide saturation constant.** A code constant in [0, 1], default 1, pulls every snapped mean toward its own linear luminance before it reaches SH; the A/B tunes it and the SH keys fold it. Bake-wide, so a texture bounces the same in every map (`research.md` §Saturation).
- **Scope: ids 34 and 27, one shared estimator.** Colour is the same per-hit multiply in both paths, so it never loosens the parity between an animated light's bounce and the static bounce beside it.
- **A material stage resolves textures before the lighting bakes.** A new stage ahead of ShBake bakes every texture static-world faces use and builds the shared material table. Fluid-only names keep their late append and bake after ShBake, so `build_pipeline.md` §Fluid surfaces holds, world indices do not shift, world bundles still charge first, and the byte report stays one report. Texture mips bake after every lighting stage today; SH bounce now reads texture content, so materials move ahead.
- **One shared material module.** `reflection_probes/materials.rs` `SurfaceMaterials` moves into a shared compiler module that holds the per-texture stats and footprint sampling. The consumer picks the fallback: lighting gets grey, reflections keep magenta. It moves now because `surface-radiance-cache` patch albedo and surface lights' emissive colour come next and need materials at the same point (`research.md` §Shared material stage).
- **Keys fold means, not sidecar keys.** The SH group and delta-entry keys fold the snapped means of the textures static-world faces use and the saturation constant, so an edit that moves no 8-bit mean still hits: a normal or specular slot alone, or an `_e` intensity change. An albedo edit re-keys every group and entry, as a geometry edit already does; accepted, and no divergence from `plans/done/incremental-bake-per-element/`, which localizes light edits only.
- **Physical albedo (owner).** Maps with dark textures get dimmer indirect light. If the A/B wants brightness back, the renderer's `DEFAULT_INDIRECT_SCALE` is retuned and its reflection-capture mirror moves with it. Rejected: normalizing each map to a 0.45 mean, because one texture would then bounce differently on different maps.
- **One indirect scale for every SH consumer but fog (owner), as the first commit.** World, fluids, movers, skinned meshes and billboards multiply SH indirect by the renderer's one indirect scale, so entities match their environment; fog takes no scale. It lands before any colour, so the colour A/B compares against grey bounce at the unified scale and entity dimming stays out of the colour judgment.
- **No author-facing knob (owner).** No per-map bounce scale, per-texture albedo override or saturation control: no map key, FGD field or flag. Authors steer bounce through the textures they paint, and `docs/level_design.md` says so: indirect light takes each texture's average colour, leaving out texels that glow.
- **Layer placement.** A compile-time estimator change, plus a runtime consistency fix so entities match their environment. No PRL or `.prm` format change; the bytes of ids 34 and 27 change.
- **Non-goals:**
  - Surface emission, emissive surfaces lighting others: the roadmap's surface-lights brief, after this one, owns it.
  - Multi-bounce: `surface-radiance-cache` owns it.
  - Movers and fluids as bounce surfaces. SH bakes against static-world faces only: baked bounce cannot follow a moving surface, and a fluid's visible light, such as lava's glow, is emission, which surface lights own.
  - Fog retuning. Fog samples SH with no indirect scale, so it dims with the darker bounce (`research.md` §Expected energy); its per-volume `min_brightness` and `ambient_scatter` are the controls authors adjust.

## Acceptance
### Automated
Byte-identical rows compare against the pre-change bake: digests checked in from the indirect-scale commit (`research.md` §Byte oracle).

**Colour and fallback**
- [ ] A fixture room with a red wall and a white floor, lit by a white light and baked through the material stage from fixture PNGs, not a hand-built means table: a probe beside the red wall reads R above G and above B in its indirect term. The same bake with an empty material table reads neutral.
- [ ] Regression guard: that fixture with every face unresolved, and a map whose textures are all unresolved, bake ids 34 and 27 byte-identical to it, and the map's reflection-probe section too: reflections keep their placeholder. Unresolved covers a face with no texture, a name with no source, and a texture with only a normal or specular slot.
- [ ] Regression guard: on the red-wall fixture, ids 35, 41, 45, 47 and 48 are byte-identical to the pre-change bake.
- [ ] An animated hard light and a static hard light of the same colour at the same spot, composed through the animated light's scale at its peak, give the same bounce at the probe beside the red wall: the delta composed with its scale and the static base agree per channel within 0.02 absolute, the existing direct delta-versus-base tolerance, on uncompressed, pre-encoding values, with the bounce's largest channel at least 0.2 so the tolerance cannot pass on a near-zero fixture, both with colour and with every face on the grey fallback.
- [ ] Regression guard: in a map where some faces resolve and others do not, an unresolved face's hit multiplies by exactly 0.45, today's expression as the reference multiply, at saturations 0, 0.5 and 1, and a resolved face takes its own mean (research P-GREY-MIXED).
- [ ] Regression guard: a map with no fluid-only names, one with no textures, one whose static faces use none of its textures, one with no static lights, and one with a degenerate SH grid each bake without error, with the outcomes their pins name (research P-ZERO-TAIL, P-ZERO-TEX, P-ZERO-FACES, P-ZERO-SH-WORK).

**Saturation** (red-wall fixture, white light, probe values before encoding; tests pass saturation as an argument and never edit the constant)
- [ ] At saturation 0 every SH basis coefficient of the probe is neutral, R, G and B agreeing within 0.1% of the band's largest coefficient magnitude, and the Rec. 709 luminance of every basis coefficient matches its value at saturation 1 within 0.1% of that magnitude.
- [ ] Each SH coefficient at saturation 0.5 equals the mean of its values at saturations 0 and 1 within 0.1% of the band's largest coefficient magnitude, and the red-to-blue spread at 0.5 lies strictly between 0 and its spread at 1.
- [ ] The shipped saturation constant lies in [0, 1], and the table build rejects a value outside that range.
- [ ] At saturations 0, 0.5 and 1, no channel of any texture's bounce albedo exceeds that texture's largest snapped-mean channel by more than one f32 ulp, and a neutral texture's albedo equals its mean within one f32 ulp at every saturation.

**Mean**
- [ ] Before snapping, a known two-colour texture's mean equals the mean of its two colours after sRGB-to-linear decode, not the decode of their sRGB mean; on a non-power-of-two fixture it equals the independently computed linear average of every source texel within f32 rounding, and snaps to the same 8-bit value. Each snapped mean is an 8-bit sRGB value per channel, and at saturation 1 the bake's albedo is exactly its sRGB decode.
- [ ] One texture gets the same mean in two maps whose other textures differ.

**Glow exclusion**
- [ ] A `neon_` fixture texture with a bright painted strip, and an `_e` covering exactly that strip and opaque black elsewhere: the mean equals the average of the non-strip texels.
- [ ] The same texture without `_e`, and an all-emissive `neon_` texture, each take the full average.
- [ ] The same texture and `_e` under a zero-strength prefix take the full average, also when a `neon_` name shares its sidecar, in either order, cold or warm (research P-SHARED-SIDECAR).

**Cache keys**
- [ ] A diffuse edit that moves an 8-bit mean re-keys every SH group and delta entry: a regional repaint on a fixture texture. One that leaves every snapped mean unchanged hits them all, with ids 34 and 27 byte-identical: a one-texel edit on a fixture texture whose 8-bit mean stays put.
- [ ] On a `neon_` texture, an `_e` edit that changes which texels are non-zero re-keys every SH group and delta entry when it moves an 8-bit mean, and hits them all, with ids 34 and 27 byte-identical, when it leaves every snapped mean unchanged. An `_e` edit that only changes the intensity of already non-zero texels hits them all.
- [ ] Editing only that texture's normal or specular slot hits every SH group and delta entry. So does editing a fluid-only or mover-only texture.
- [ ] Changing the saturation constant re-keys every SH group and delta entry; restoring it, within the cache budget, hits them all. Changing it and a texture's snapped mean in one build re-keys them all, and reverting only one of the two still misses (research P-COMBINED).
- [ ] Every texture index any BVH primitive's material bucket can return at a hit is in the set the SH group and delta keys fold; editing the mean of each such texture re-keys.
- [ ] A cache written before this change misses for both stages.

**Order and determinism**
- [ ] World texture indices and their texture cache keys equal the pre-change build's; fluid-only names still land after every world name. Regression guards: the texture byte report lists the same bundles under the same names as before the split, on warm and cold builds; ids 34 and 27 are byte-identical at one worker and at many. The byte report, name-to-key map and sidecar bytes from the material stage plus the fluid tail equal those from one call over the same full name list, on a fixture with a world name, a mover-only name, a world-and-fluid name and a fluid-only name, on sidecars absent and present.
- [ ] With sidecars absent, each distinct world diffuse source, and its `_e`, decodes once, and a world or mover source that fails to decode, or whose `_e` dimensions differ, fails the build before ShBake starts; a fluid-only texture with the same fault fails it in the fluid tail, after ShBake (research P-FAIL-ORDER).
- [ ] A build whose delta working-set gate refuses, and a build whose light-animation validation fails, each fail without reading the texture root or writing a sidecar; a build with a faulty world texture and a refusing gate reports the gate's error (research P-GATE-ORDER).
- [ ] A texture shared by a world face and a fluid face, and one used only by a mover, are each baked once, charged once in the byte report under the world or mover name, and the fluid tail holds fluid-only names only. Two interned names that normalize to the same source get equal means, each at its own texture index; the SH keys fold both indices, and the byte report charges the bundle once, under the first name (research P-NAME-SPLIT, P-ALIAS-NAMES).
- [ ] A build that reuses every sidecar decodes no diffuse or `_e` PNG and gets means equal to a rebake's, on a fixture whose texels cover every byte value per channel; a cold SH bake given either produces byte-identical ids 34 and 27. On the red-wall fixture, a warm SH bake with every group and entry present reproduces id 27 byte for byte and keeps id 34 per channel dimmer-or-equal to the cold bake.
- [ ] A sidecar that is missing, truncated, carries an invalid declared slot or a stale bundle hash, or was evicted between two builds is rebaked in the material stage, and the mean it yields equals the mean from the intact sidecar; the build never takes a mean from a sidecar it rejected (research P-STALE-SIDECAR).

**Derived stats**
- [ ] The build writes no file or cache entry for means: no level-compiler code writes stats to disk (grep gate, `research.md` §Grep gates), and after a cold and a warm build the build cache lists entries only for the stages that wrote them before this change.
- [ ] While stats are computed, texel data held for them never spans more than one texture per worker: its sidecar bytes and parsed slots on a reuse, or its source PNG bytes and linear buffers on a rebake. Nothing accumulates across textures, and the table holds one fixed-size record per texture.
- [ ] One material module, outside the reflection stage, holds the stats table and footprint sampling and serves both the SH bake and the reflection stage; no other level-compiler module computes a texture mean (grep gate, `research.md` §Grep gates).

**Indirect scale and surface**
- [ ] World, fluid, mover, skinned-mesh and billboard shading each multiply SH indirect by the renderer's one indirect scale: a guard fails when any of them skips it or reads another value, and when fog takes it. Mover and skinned-mesh light params, read back from their serialized uploads, hold the renderer's scale at a non-default value and on the frame after a runtime change, so an upload fixed at 1.0 fails (research P-SCALE-RUNTIME).
- [ ] The reflection capture's indirect weight equals the renderer's default indirect scale, retuned or not.
- [ ] No worldspawn key, FGD field or `prl-build` flag exists for bounce strength, albedo or saturation (grep gate, `research.md` §Grep gates). Every `.prm` sidecar is byte-identical to the pre-change bake, with its format and version unchanged. The diff changes no PRL section layout.

### Manual
- [ ] Owner A/B at `campaign-test` stations against a build of the indirect-scale commit alone, grey bounce at the unified scale: hue spill reads right, and overall energy is checked; this decides any retune of the default indirect scale and the saturation constant.
- [ ] Entities beside the world read indirect consistent with it; fluids and water reflections take the tint with it.
- [ ] The A/B checks fog separately: it dims with the darker bounce, the expected result, and raising a volume's `min_brightness` or `ambient_scatter` restores its look.
- [ ] Cold bake time before and after on `campaign-test` and `stress-warren-mini`: SH Bake, the material stage, TextureMips and total, one bake at a time, plus warm material-stage time (baseline: `research.md` §Bake-time baseline).
- [ ] `docs/level_design.md` tells authors that indirect light takes each texture's average colour, leaving out texels that glow, so repainting a texture changes its bounce.
- [ ] Code review: the diff adds no runtime code beyond applying and retuning the indirect scale.

## Path
- **Read seam.** `sh_bake.rs` `sample_radiance_rgb` holds the one production read of `BOUNCE_ALBEDO`. `Hit` comes from `closest_hit_in_leaf`, which sees `BvhPrimitive.material_bucket_id`; `bvh_build::collect_primitives` sets it to `face.texture_index`, and its doc calls that equality current, so pin it or carry the index explicitly. `RaytracingCtx` reaches both `bake_probe` and `bake_probe_indirect_rgb`, so a table there gives base and delta one estimator. Keep the multiply order at the hit so a grey entry reproduces today's floats exactly.
- **Keys.** Fold the table into `sh_group::group_cache_key` and `delta_sh_cache::delta_sh_entry_cache_key` themselves, not into `geometry_content_hash`: billboard scatter, direct SH and ChunkLightList hash that too and read no albedo. Fold the 8-bit values, and bump both stage versions. The folded set mirrors `reflection_probes/stage_key::cache_key`'s material set, the indices some static-world face uses; that stage keys on the SH bytes, so it re-keys by itself.
- **Estimator.** `bake_world_texture_mips` hands mip 0 of slots 0 and 3 to the shared table: on a rebake the `decode_png_rgba` output, on a hit the payloads `PrmFile::from_bytes_partial` already parsed (`cache_entry_has_valid_declared_slots` gates every declared slot). The table accumulates in f64 in a fixed texel order, decode through `build_srgb_to_linear_lut`, snap with `linear_to_srgb_u8`, and decode the snap through the same LUT. Write the blend as `s·c + (1 − s)·L` so saturation 1 returns the snapped mean bit-exactly (`research.md` §Saturation, §Snapping, §Glow exclusion).
- **Glow strength.** It is a pure function of the name: `postretro_render_data::material::derive_material(name, …).emissive_strength()`, which `SurfaceMaterials::load` and `stage_key::cache_key` already call; the texture-bake loop holds each name. Stats are per name, not per sidecar: the sidecar key is content-only, so two names can share a sidecar and differ in strength.
- **Test seams.** `decode_png_rgba` has no counter. Add a `#[cfg(test)]` decode count and a high-water mark of texel bytes the stats path holds. Precedent: `lightmap_layer/chart_cull.rs` `ExactSkips`, whose verify mode and the `lightmap_layer/skip_stats.rs` `SkipStats` tallies exist only under `#[cfg(test)]`.
- **Parity tolerance.** No grey-bounce animated/static parity test exists. The 0.02 is `DELTA_SUBTRACTION_EPSILON` in `direct_sh_bake.rs` `direct_sh_delta_subtraction_matches_bake_excluding_selected_light`, on decoded atlas values. The base seeds soft visibility by global light index and the delta by the light's own axis, so a hard fixture light keeps sampling noise out of the comparison.
- **Split.** The material stage bakes every name in the table at ShBake: world and mover names, both interned at Geometry. TextureMips keeps the fluid tail, a second call of the same bake on the appended fluid-only names, then models and sprites, all into the material stage's `StageTextureBytes`, logged once; `name_to_key` merges both (`research.md` §Stage order).
- **Indirect scale.** `forward.wgsl` and `fluid_surface.wgsl` apply it; `billboard.wgsl` declares it in the shared `Uniforms` and never multiplies; `KinematicLightParams` and `MeshLightParams` lack it but carry spare pad words. Extend `render/tests/shader_tests.rs`'s `* uniforms.indirect_scale;` guard per consumer, and tie the mover and mesh uploads to the renderer's value (`research.md` §Indirect-scale consumers).
- **Shape and rival.** Shape: one shared material stage ahead of the lighting bakes. Rival: a means-only pass ahead of ShBake with TextureMips left in place; it loses because it adds a second decode path and `surface-radiance-cache` redoes it for footprint albedo.
- **Author docs.** The bounce-colour note sits in `docs/level_design.md` §Lights, beside the static-versus-dynamic paragraph.
- **First slice.** The indirect-scale commit, then the `SurfaceMaterials` move in its own commit, checked against the reflection-probe digest. Then the means table and the red-wall fixture, then `campaign-test`'s means against grey, to price the retune before the A/B (`research.md` §Expected energy). Before extending `sh_bake.rs`, `texture_mips.rs` or `pipeline.rs`, each past ~800 lines, split out the part this extends in its own commit.

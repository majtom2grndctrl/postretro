Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* A derivative of ../mutant (same Mixamo terms: may ship in a game built from
  this repo, never in an SDK bundle offered to other creators). Built
  2026-11-23 by a one-off script that edits mutant's glTF directly; no
  Blender round trip, so joint order, rest pose and extras match mutant.
* Right hand removed: triangles with a vertex weighted over 50% to RightHand
  or its finger joints are dropped (the finger joints stay in the skin).
* Arm cannon: a second primitive (material arm_cannon_M,
  ArmCannon_diffuse.png, 2x2 flat colour blocks), an 8-sided flat-shaded
  barrel rigidly skinned to RightForeArm, from near the elbow to past the
  old wrist.
* Built-in weapon socket: a skin joint `cannon_tip`, child of RightForeArm
  at the bore mouth (local +Y = barrel direction), tagged
  extras.socket = "cannon_tip". It has no clip tracks, so it rides the
  forearm. First consumer: E10--npc-weapon-mounts'
  `{ item, muzzleSocket: "cannon_tip" }` fixture.
* Clips: binds clip_libraries/mixamo_brute, as mutant does.

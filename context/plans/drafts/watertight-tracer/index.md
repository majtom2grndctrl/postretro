# watertight-tracer

Brief · compact · reads: `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Ray traversal, §Build Cache · `context/lib/development_guide.md` §3.6 · `context/lib/testing_guide.md` §Slow / cold-bake suites · read at 343ff98b6

## Problem

Raised by the owner from two findings. The `lightmap-reach-cull` review found that the remembered occluder (`last_occluder`, `clip_occluder`) is tested whether or not the traversal would reach it, so a shadow answer changes with cache contents and walk order. That blocks exact culls such as `lightmap-reach-cull` and adds noise to per-state shadow comparisons. The leak diagnosis found rays within 1 µm of a shared edge missing both triangles, and rays slipping through crease cracks where two brushes' edges miss each other by 1–250 µm (`drafts/lightmap-reach-cull/leak-diagnosis-findings.md` §Verdict). Every leaking texel it found is buried; no visible leak from the tracer has been seen (§Visible impact). Two more holes have no measured occurrence on a map: Möller–Trumbore's scale-dependent `|det| < 1e-8` rejection drops real crossings on small triangles at grazing angles, and the f32 box test can reject a box whose triangle the ray hits at an axis-aligned edge or vertex.

The diagnosis judged shared-edge watertightness "none proportionate" and left crease cracks to snapping. The owner's bar is no visible crack or leak, and once the cache can't change answers, exact culls rely on the hit test's error bound. When this is done, every bake's shadow and closest-hit answer is a function of the segment and the geometry alone. A ray through a shared edge or vertex hits one of its triangles, a ray through a crack or T-junction narrower than a small tolerance τ hits too, no box holding a hit is rejected, and the cache only reorders work.

## Decisions

- **Watertight hit test in every bake tracer, in f64.** Woop/Benthin/Wald 2013 replaces every Möller–Trumbore copy (research §Tracer inventory). It is two-sided and rejects only at `det == 0`. Its inputs are f32, and f64 keeps its rounding near 2⁻⁵³ of ‖v − o‖ (research §Precision and pad).
- **In-plane tolerance τ.** A ray that crosses a triangle's plane within τ of one of its edges or vertices hits it. τ closes crease cracks and T-junctions narrower than it, the oversize-face cut's included, for at most τ of extra shadow at silhouette edges, which is invisible. The leak diagnosis (§Recommendation 5) kept the old test because both culls' proofs rest on it; this brief replaces that test and re-derives those proofs anyway, and welding alone can't close a crease (research §Tolerance).
- **τ follows the census.** It starts at 0.5 mm and covers the widest crease crack the shared-edge census finds, in the crack-width metric of research §Tolerance. If that needs τ above face extraction's 1 mm split tolerance (`REGION_CLASSIFICATION_EPSILON`), the build stops for the owner.
- **f64 ships; the f32 fallback is the owner's call.** Peer bakers trace in f32 and none is watertight (research §Precedent). The design is a hybrid already: f32 conservative box tests, an f64 hit test. Under the fallback the test runs in f32 with a pad sized to the extent, and every tracer segment starts inside the padded extent, so the chunk light list's sun segments start at the sample, not 10 km out.
- **One cost gate.** If the tracer change costs more than about 10% of hallway Lightmap Bake, the build stops for the owner before building any fallback. The gate is read at the first slice and again on the landing build; a precision change reruns the margin derivation and searches (research C8). No fallback to a test that isn't watertight.
- **Conservative box test, pbrt-v4 style.** `tFar *= 1 + 2γ₃`, and a NaN slab constrains nothing. The query is built from the hit test's own origin and direction, not from `bvh::Ray`, which re-normalizes. The accept set becomes a superset of the stock test's, replacing `build_pipeline.md` §Ray traversal's "accepts exactly the stock boxes".
- **Padded boxes, id 19 untouched.** Build the tree on exact boxes and write the shipped BVH section (id 19) from it. Then grow every box of the bake's tree, node child and primitive alike, outward by the pad, τ plus one f32 ulp of the bake's extent (its largest coordinate magnitude), and step each grown coordinate one f32 further out (`next_down`, `next_up`), since a round-to-nearest add can land half a step short; a hit the test accepts at τ, with its own rounding, lies inside its box (research §Precision and pad). The pad is uniform and the rounding monotone, so parents still contain their children, and topology, id 19 and closest-hit tie order stay put (`done/bake-ray-speed-contract.md`); a debug assertion checks containment (`development_guide.md` §3.6).
- **The cache can't change the answer.** The remembered occluder is trusted only when its leaf box passes the same `BoundedRay` predicate the traversal applies, so the cache only reorders the traversal's own candidates, on the slab path and the distance-prune path (research §Cache independence). The shaft cull's `last_blocker` stays ungated: it re-tests its triangle against the tile's own query bound, and a clear tile still needs every candidate separated, so it changes only how soon a blocked tile is found.
- **Keep the distance prune.** The padded box holds every point the hit test accepts, so a pruned box holds no hit before the segment end, and the cache's box check refuses a cached "hit" there (research §Distance prune).
- **`TRACER_EPOCH`,** folded into the key of every cached stage whose computation reaches the tracer, as `PORTAL_WALK_EPOCH` and `SOFT_VISIBILITY_EPOCH` are (research §Stages that reach the tracer). Any later change to tracer answers bumps it. Uncached tracing stages need no fold; texel steps' stored report rides the lightmap-section and weight-map keys.
- **Shaft-cull margins re-derived for the new test and τ.** `margin.rs` sizes each triangle's reach to Möller–Trumbore's `e1`, `e2`, `s` and the shape term ρ (research §Margins). A margin too small breaks bytes, not light: the cull would clear a tile the tracer blocks.
- **Bytes move once.** This lands on `main` after `face-extract-split-epsilon` (#636) and before `lightmap-reach-cull`, which names it as a prerequisite. The whole move is this brief's, and the owner reviews it per section before landing.
- **Named gaps, counted, not closed here:** rays lying in a face's plane (`det == 0`), which enter solid only through a non-coplanar face the test sees; cracks wider than τ, which are holes. `lightmap-reach-cull`'s coverage proof reads this brief's τ constant, matches every face edge within τ by one running the other way, and switches its cull off on any span it cannot match.
- **Lighting states.** `ready/mover-light-flow--foundation/` covers doors, the sliding wall and the rising room, and sizes their masks only; destruction comes from the roadmap. Their per-state comparisons get a cache-independent answer.
- **Layer: compiler only.** All code stays in `postretro-level-compiler`. No runtime crate, shader or shipped section layout changes. Runtime ray tests in `sim` read collision, not the bake BVH, and stay as they are.

## Acceptance

### Automated

Tracer answers:
- [ ] A brute-force reference that tests every triangle with the shipped hit test, with no BVH and no box test, replaces every stock-ray reference scan. Every bake tracer's occlusion answer equals it on the existing parity fixtures and the rounding-stress fixture, whose rays stay inside the prune slack; closest hits match its distance, and a tie may name either triangle.
- [ ] Shared edges, proptest at map coordinates 0 and ±10–500 m, ray origins from beside the edge out to 10 km, at τ = 0 and at the shipped τ: two triangles in different faces share an axis-aligned edge with bit-identical vertices, and a ray crosses exactly on it, or on a shared vertex, at any angle. Every tracer entry point reports a hit on every case, BVH included: lightmap, SH, chunk light list and scatter occlusion, SH closest hit, and the front-face-filtered lead-in and reflection-probe rays when both triangles face the ray. At τ = 0 it fails with Möller–Trumbore restored.
- [ ] Cracks: a crease crack whose edges miss by less than τ, and a T-junction the oversize-face cut leaves, each block a ray crossing the gap; a crack wide enough that a ray through its middle passes more than the pad from both triangles clears that ray (research C11). The narrow cases fail at τ = 0.
- [ ] Silhouette over-shadowing is bounded by τ: a ray crossing a triangle's plane just under τ from its nearest edge or vertex blocks, and one just over τ clears, beyond a sliver's acute vertex included.
- [ ] A triangle with 1 mm edges, crossed 0.5° from its plane, where Möller–Trumbore's |det| falls below 1e-8, blocks. The test fails with the old det threshold restored.
- [ ] Both windings block a crossing segment. A hit just short of the segment end blocks and one just past it clears, for either sign of det.
- [ ] Front-face callers, reflection-probe primary rays and the lead-in test: for rays whose dominant axis is each of ±x, ±y and ±z, a front-facing triangle is hit and the same triangle with reversed winding is skipped, and det's sign agrees with the geometric normal's on every case.

Cache independence:
- [ ] For each segment, the shadow answer with an empty cache equals the answer with each primitive cached in turn. Segments include hits exactly at an axis-aligned edge or vertex, where the answer also equals the brute-force reference, and run on a one-primitive tree too, whose leaf box is the primitive's own. They also include a grazing segment near a sliver's end whose computed `t` falls inside the segment while its box starts past the prune limit, found by search or planted through a test seam on the hit test's `t`. The sliver case fails with the leaf-box check removed (research C1, C2, C4, C6).
- [ ] The flat-box rounding witness, a steep ray through a flat triangle's axis-aligned edge, reads blocked with an empty cache. It reads clear with both the conservative factor and the pad removed, and the PR records which of the two alone still blocks it.

Box test and tree:
- [ ] The box test accepts every box the stock test accepts, on the existing lattice proptest. It accepts a ray lying in a box's face plane for both signs of zero in that direction component and for the min and the max face, on a flat box and on one with extent on that axis, and the bounded traversal then reaches the triangle there.
- [ ] On an exact, unpadded box, the box test accepts the flat-box witness's leaf box, which the stock test rejects by rounding, and rejects it with the conservative factor removed.
- [ ] A shadow segment that ends well short of a box's entry tests none of that box's triangles, with an empty cache and with one of them cached.
- [ ] Every bake-tree box, node child and primitive alike, contains its exact box grown by τ plus one ulp of the extent on every face, compared exactly, including faces at coordinate 0 and a coordinate whose f32 sum with the pad rounds inward (research C13). It holds every point the hit test accepts on its triangles, tolerance hits at τ included. The shipped tree keeps the exact boxes.
- [ ] Every tree a bake traces is padded: at the pre-cut build, at the post-cut rebuild, and on a map with no cut face; so is the tree a test gets from the BVH constructor. The containment assertion runs on each (research C5).
- [ ] A planted tree whose parent box omits a sliver of one child trips the containment assertion, in a test compiled only with debug assertions; every fixture's BVH passes it.
- [ ] On the shared-crease fixture, the padded tree yields leaves in the exact tree's order for every ray that accepts the same leaves on both, so closest-hit ties keep their winner.

Stages and margins:
- [ ] Changing the tracer epoch, passed to each key builder as an input, changes the key of every stage in research §Stages that reach the tracer, re-read from `main` at landing with any tracing stage landed since (research C12). The negative half is a grep gate: the epoch appears at no other stage's key site.
- [ ] Both shaft-cull margin searches, against the watertight test at the precision and τ that ship, find no accepted hit beyond the re-derived margins. Their draws are rescaled to the new margins, and each search's reach guards (phantom hits, certified count, worst ratio) still hold. The query-growth test passes against the new margins, and `done/lightmap-exact-skips/` A11 and A12 pass at 0.95× and 1.05× of them (research C8, C10).
- [ ] Existing parity holds: the layered lightmap equals the monolithic oracle at each floor setting, warm equals cold, and output is identical across worker counts.

## Path

- **One shared hit test.** Put the watertight test, its per-ray precompute (axis permutation, shear) and `TRACER_EPOCH` in `ray_traversal.rs` beside `BoundedRay`, and have every tracer call it. Accept `t` by comparing it with `t_max·|det|`, det's sign folded in. Closest-hit callers need `t` and a normal (SH) or barycentrics (reflection probes); lower bounds (`> 0`, `> RAY_EPSILON`, `> min_distance`) need a scaled compare or a divide after acceptance. The rival, patching each copy in place, invites drift.
- **The tolerance.** Measure τ in the triangle's plane, as distance to the triangle, so corners are rounded. Woop's edge functions live in the plane across the ray, where a fixed tolerance grows as 1/sin θ in the triangle's plane; offsetting each edge by τ pushes a sliver's acute vertex far past τ (research §Tolerance). A tolerance hit's barycentrics fall up to τ outside the triangle; reflection-probe UVs clamp them. The rival, welding crease vertices, needs geometry-wide snapping.
- **Front-face filters.** Reflection probes and `front_faces_clear` filter by their own f32 winding test before the hit test. Take front from det's sign instead, so filter and test agree on grazing rays. det's sign follows the winding only when kx and ky swap for a negative dominant direction component.
- **Rival to the gated cache: drop it.** Answer-neutral, but the remembered occluder answers 65.8% of hallway-mini's shadow rays, mostly on dark texels (`drafts/lightmap-stage-sizing/findings.md` §1); the gated cache keeps that speed with the same answer.
- **The leaf box.** The cache stores a primitive index and tests the box the iterator tests: the parent's stored child box, or a root leaf's own primitive box (`bvh` 0.12 `bvh/iter.rs`; research C6). Cache users: `lightmap_layer::chart_walk`, `shadowmask_bake::estimate`, `lightmap_bake::reference`, `texel_footprint::test_fixtures`.
- **Box query.** `BoundedRay::new` takes the hit test's origin and direction and derives the inverse itself. pbrt-v4's `Bounds3::IntersectP` is the precedent, but it skips NaN value by value through comparison order, so a direct port rejects a face-plane ray for one sign of zero. Drop a NaN slab whole.
- **Pins that invert.** `sh_bake/trace_tests.rs`: the flat-box witness, `bounded_traversal_matches_stock_rejection_of_undefined_slab`, and the rounding-stress envelope comment. `ray_traversal.rs`: the face-plane rejection test and the exact two-pass proptest. Stock-ray references: `segment_clear_full_scan` in the lightmap, SH, chunk light list and scatter tracers, and SH `closest_hit_full_scan`.
- **First slice.** Counters first, byte-neutral (research C9). Then the shared hit test with τ, the shared-edge proptest, the crack and small-triangle rows, and the census, which sets τ; then the f64 and plain-f32 hallway timings against the gate. Then the box test and padding with the flat-box witness, the cache gate, and the margins. The epoch's landing value comes last (research C7).
- **File size.** `lightmap_bake.rs` is past 1,500 lines and `chunk_light_list_bake.rs` past 3,500. Move the lightmap tracer functions into `ray_traversal` first, behavior-preserving, in their own commit; the other copies are deleted.
- **Docs at landing.** `build_pipeline.md` §Ray traversal: the watertight test, τ and its sizing, its precision, the superset box test, padded boxes, the cache-independence contract and the named gaps. §Build Cache: `TRACER_EPOCH` and its bump rule.

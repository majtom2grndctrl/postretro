Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* Character downloaded from Adobe Mixamo as FBX (with skin, T-pose) and bound
  to clip_libraries/mixamo_brute with mixamo_to_gltf.py --yaw 180
  --clip-library, so it faces +Z as AI facing expects. It ships mesh and
  skeleton only.
* Base color only: the normal map and the KHR_materials_specular factor were
  dropped (the engine reads the diffuse slot alone), and the diffuse map was
  downsized from 2048 to 1024 px.

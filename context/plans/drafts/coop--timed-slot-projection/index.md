# coop--timed-slot-projection

Brief · compact · reads: `context/lib/networking.md` §What gates, and what replicates instead, §Combat authority, §Presentation events vs. replicated state · `context/lib/ui.md` §3 · read at d6f3471

## Problem
Owner playtest under the dev lag proxy after PR #601: a connected client's reload meter fills in steps. Basis: observed defect. Cause: a client's store receives the host's sample about 30 times a second (`SNAPSHOT_TICK_INTERVAL` = 2), and the meter shows each sample raw. It steps on a clean link at any frame rate above 30; loss and jitter only lengthen the holds. The client has no rate to advance the value between samples. When done, a connected client's meters ramp smoothly wherever the host's value ramps, between samples and through lost or reordered snapshots, with no change to the host, single-player or any mod HUD.

## Decisions
- **The projection sets the display target.** For an enumerated slot on a connected client, everything the UI reads from that slot reads its projection: the target a display value retargets to (`ui.md` §3), `visibleWhen` predicates and a Bar's max. The rest of §3 holds: a bound tween eases toward the moving target, and an untweened binding shows the projection. The quantity keeps its meaning, so it projects under the existing slot name and no mod HUD changes. The store keeps the host samples, so state crossings and every store reader see them. Reload sounds and the overheat cue read the client's committed weapon projection, which this leaves alone.
- **Scope: slots with a timed stretch, each in its timed direction.** Reload progress and cell project only upward, cooldown and heat only downward: the directions the host's fixed tick ramps them (reload timer, regen, cooldown countdown, cooling). A shot moves heat up, cell down and cooldown up, against those directions, so no per-shot step extrapolates at any fire rate. Each projects only to its bound: reload progress 1, cooldown and heat 0, cell the capacity sample naming the same host weapon slot, and with no such sample cell does not project. The executor enumerates further slots from the state-slot table and reports the list in the plan of record.
- **The rate is derived on the client, from host-stamped samples.** The meter is presentational, so it takes the cheapest shape that looks right, not the authoritative one. Each accepted snapshot whose state batch applies is a sample at its header's server tick: a record samples its value, and no record, or a resend at the held baseline, samples the held value (`ServerStateReplication::produce_for_client` omits only an acked, unchanged slot). A rejected batch, or a record held back to request a refresh, is no sample. Slopes are per server tick and come from the host's values, never local tuning.
- **Extrapolate only on an agreed slope.** The projection advances while the two newest intervals both move in the slot's timed direction and agree on slope within a tolerance that absorbs the timer's whole-millisecond rounding. Otherwise the target is the newest sample. A ramp therefore smooths once two full intervals agree, while a reload wrap, a cool delay ending mid-interval, or a jump in the timed direction (a descriptor reload, a new weapon in the same host slot) shows raw. A host cancel, a slot change and the active flag falling all break agreement, so the target is the host's sample and a reload's exit fade matches the host HUD's. A sample feeds the projection once the timeline reaches its tick; one at the slot's bound applies on arrival.
- **The projection runs on the samples' own timeline.** It projects to the sample that would be arriving now, not to the host's current value, so the meter stays in step with the raw `reloadActive` flag and the reload sounds. The timeline starts at time sync's first sample and carries nothing from before it; until then the target is the newest raw sample. It never re-anchors on a snapshot's arrival and never rewinds; it slews through time-sync corrections, as interpolation playback does.
- **Bounded, then hold.** A derived slope is an extrapolation, so it follows the bounded-extrapolation-then-hold rule of `done/M15--p2-replication-timesync-lifecycle` and `done/E10--networked-enemy-authority-baseline`, not the uncapped closed forms of E17 movers and `anim_start`, which replicate a schedule. Projection stops once the timeline runs more than a cap past the newest sample, and holds until the next. During a real outage the meter freezes rather than showing heat cooling while the client's own fire goes unheard.
- **Resets follow the store.** Sample intake reads the state-apply outcome. History and timeline clear wherever that apply resets (level unload, manifest commit, rejoin) and at demotion. A heat or cell `[slot]` absence clears that slot's target and history, as it clears the store. Projection runs every frame, including frames with no snapshot, before the UI snapshot is built; the UI writes no store slot, and the wire is unchanged.
- **The dev HUD gains a heat bar.** It mirrors the cell bar: shown while `player.weaponResource` is `"heat"`, its max bound to `player.overheatAt`. It is the heat projection's first consumer in content and the manual proof's meter.
- **Docs owed at promotion.** `ui.md` §3: UI reads of an enumerated slot on a connected client see its projection. `networking.md` §What gates: the client-derived timed-slot projection. `networking.md` §Combat authority: projected heat and cell are display, with no client heat state, overheat latch or gameplay read. `docs/scripting-reference.md`: reload, cooldown, heat and cell meters ramp smoothly on clients with no author change.
- **Non-goals.**
  - The UI clock and frontend tweens: `drafts/ui-tween-clock` owns them. A tween on a projected slot eases on UI time, so the two land in either order.
  - Loss recovery on the reliable channels: `ready/coop--lossy-link-delivery` owns it; this brief smooths what arrives.
  - Client ammo, heat, cell and reload prediction (`networking.md` §Combat authority). The host keeps the reload timer and reserve transfer.

## Acceptance
Pins are in `research.md` §Ordering pins. "The profile" is 45 ms delay, 60 ms jitter and 5% loss, with client frames at 144 Hz, unless a row says otherwise. Timing bounds are measured on the harness's shared virtual clock. A ramp's span is 1 for reload progress, the overheatAt sample for heat, the capacity sample for cell, and the ramp's first sample for cooldown.

### Automated
**Proof setup**
- [ ] Reproduces first. The harness sends at `SNAPSHOT_TICK_INTERVAL`, advances the host at 60 Hz on the virtual clock (50 ms per three ticks, never a flat 16 ms), stamps each header with the host frame's last tick, runs client frames on their own clock, applies production's newest-snapshot gate and runs time sync over the conditioned link. Samples come from the host's own timers, never synthetic values. On the build this starts from, a connected client's reload-progress target under the profile changes only on frames that apply a sample, and the test is committed failing.

**Ramps**
- [ ] Permit: each ramp (reload, cooldown countdown, cooling, regen) smooths once two full intervals agree (P2, P3, P19). While the two newest intervals agree, the target never retreats, through lost and reordered snapshots and frames with no snapshot (P34, P37), and each frame's change is at most slope × (1 + the slope tolerance) × the timeline's top slew rate × frame time, plus 1e-4 of the ramp's span. The frame whose sample breaks agreement retargets to it, a retreat of at most slope × (one interval + 60 ms); that frame and the first after a hold past the cap are exempt from the per-frame bound (P18, P33, P51).
- [ ] Slopes compare per server tick, so a ramp agrees although its whole-millisecond timer advances 16 or 17 ms a tick and stamps run 1, 2 or 3 ticks apart (P38).
- [ ] Arrival jitter never moves the meter: on a 45 ms delay, 60 ms jitter, lossless link, each frame that applies a snapshot inside an agreeing ramp, up to the sample that ends it, stays within the Permit bound.
- [ ] Time-sync corrections never move the meter: across a reseed and folded echoes mid-ramp, the target never retreats and stays within the Permit bound (P28, P29).
- [ ] An agreeing sample that applies before the timeline reaches its tick leaves the target on the projection, below the sample, within the Permit bound. A sample at the slot's bound sets the target to the bound on its frame (P46).
- [ ] The slope is the host samples' slope: with the client's installed reload time and cool rate set to twice the host's, the projected slope stays within the slope tolerance of the host samples' slope (P43).

**Jumps and bounds**
- [ ] Refuse: a jump never extrapolates. On a 60 ms-jitter, lossless link, a shot's heat step, rapid fire at one shot per two ticks, a cooldown reset, a reload wrap, a cool delay ending and a jump in the timed direction each show the host's sample, and the target stays within slope × (one interval + 60 ms) of the host's value at that sample's tick (P9–P12, P15, P44).
- [ ] A lost snapshot carrying a slope change keeps the error within slope × cap, and the next sample retargets on its frame (P18).
- [ ] Bounds: reload progress never passes 1, and cooldown and heat never pass 0. Cell never passes the capacity sample naming the same host slot; with none, cell does not project, and it projects once that capacity applies (P14, P50). In an overheat lockout, projected heat reaches 0 no earlier than the link's one-way delay less one tick after the host's heat does, and no later than the frame that applies the first sample with the latch down (P13).
- [ ] Refuse: the target falls on a host cancel or slot change, and the reload meter disappears when its active flag falls (P4).
- [ ] In step: on the frame `reloadActive` rises, the target is the Started sample, not a value ahead of it (P1). At the end the target reaches 1 no earlier than the link's one-way delay less one tick after the host completes, and no later than the frame that applies the Completed sample, or the fall sample when the Completed snapshot is lost (P6). On the flag-fall frame the exit fade captures the host's fall value, or the held progress when that batch leaves the progress record out, as the host HUD does (P7, P25).

**Samples**
- [ ] Sample intake: a snapshot with no record for the slot, a held-id resend and a snapshot with no state records each count as a sample at the held value (P22–P24). A left-out delta, a rejected state batch, and a deferred, duplicate or older snapshot count as no sample (P8, P20, P21, P25–P27).
- [ ] Held values hold: through a cool delay longer than an ack round trip, so held-id resends and omitted records both occur, the heat target stays at the host's value (P39).
- [ ] Frame cadence: a frame that applies two snapshots takes both as samples, in sequence order (P35). Below 30 Hz client frames, each frame's target is the projection at that frame's timeline time, not the newest raw sample (P36).
- [ ] Outage: with no sample for longer than the cap, the target holds at min(newest + slope × cap, bound) and does not move until the next sample. That sample retargets on its frame: to the projection from it when the gap interval agrees, else to the sample (P33, P49).
- [ ] Before time sync has a sample, the target is the newest raw sample (P30). Its first sample mid-ramp, at join or rejoin, starts the timeline there with nothing carried from before; the target never falls below the raw sample it showed, that frame is exempt from the per-frame bound, and the projection advances within two intervals (P45).
- [ ] Resets: level unload, manifest commit, rejoin and demotion clear history and timeline, and no slope spans them; samples from a host whose ticks run below the old timeline project once two intervals agree (P31, P32, P48). The first sample naming a new host weapon slot resets that slot's history, a switch away and back included (P16, P17). A heat or cell `[slot]` absence clears the target on its frame, as it clears the store, and the first number after it shows raw (P47).

**Raw readers and isolation**
- [ ] Raw readers stay raw. A host cancel near full, with the projection already at 1, presents a cancel and no complete sound (P5). A fire press while the projection shows full and the newest sample is short of it presents as reloading. Cell dry-fire reads the raw charge (P40). The client's cooldown reconcile reads the store's `player.weaponCooldownMs`. Reload sounds and the overheat cue play on the same frames as with the projection off.
- [ ] State crossings on an enumerated slot fire once per crossing in the applied host samples, never on the projection's advance (P41).
- [ ] UI reads: a binding, a `visibleWhen` predicate and a Bar max on an enumerated slot all read the projection on the same frame. A tween bound to one eases toward the moving projection; with reduce motion on, it shows the projection that frame (P42).
- [ ] Host and single-player are unchanged: each frame, the UI snapshot's value for every enumerated slot equals the store slot.
- [ ] No client heat or cell state is written: each frame the client's enumerated store slots equal the last applied host sample, and the client weapon components' heat and cell fields are unchanged from install. Grep gate: the projection code writes no store slot and no weapon component.
- [ ] The wire is unchanged: `SNAPSHOT_VERSION`, `WIRE_VERSION`, the application protocol, the state-schema fingerprint and the wire-shape tags keep the values they hold on the build this starts from, and no slot record gains a field.

### Manual
- [ ] Loopback recipe (`networking.md` §Manual loopback recipe) on `coop-presentation-test`, client conditioned at the profile: the reload meter ramps smoothly; with `coop_heat_test`, the heat bar steps per shot while firing, then cools smoothly; with `reference_plasma_bolt` from the default loadout, the cell bar steps per shot and refills smoothly.

## Path
- Seams: `hud.ts` `reloadMeter` (untweened bind of `player.reloadProgress`) and `cellBar`, the model for the heat bar. `PlayerHudStatePublisher::tick_for_role_and_report_sampled_weapon`, whose connected-client branch skips reload. `WeaponComponent::reload_feedback_sample`. `ReplicatedWireShape::WieldableSlotNumber`, shared by reload progress and weapon cooldown. `ClientStateApply::apply_snapshot_state` in `postretro-netcode`, where intake belongs. Host timers: `advance_weapon_resource` (cooling, regen) and the weapon stage's reload. The UI snapshot's `slot_values` (`crates/ui/src/output.rs`) is what bindings, predicates and Bar max read. A per-frame neighbour that already runs before the UI snapshot: `update_client_overlay_anchors`.
- Naming: `state_slots/resource_projection.rs` already uses "projection" for something else; pick a distinct module name.
- Sample timeline: the time-sync estimate (`ClockEstimator::estimated_server_tick`) minus half the smoothed RTT, behind a slew and a never-rewind clamp; or each sample's arrival lag smoothed against its recent minimum, as the snapshot-lag law in `networking.md` §Time sync does. Report the top slew rate in the plan of record. `research.md` has why `presentation_host_time` fails.
- Harness: extend `state_slot_loss_harness_test.rs` to the Proof setup row first; the first slice is that harness and the failing reproduction row.
- Rivals considered:
  - A replicated rate or schedule. Only a schedule (value plus the time of the next change, as E17 and `anim_start` carry) beats a derived rate, and only at a change; a sample or two of raw display there is invisible on a meter. It would cost a new owner-private slot or a widened correlated sample, a schema fingerprint change, and host work on every timed slot.
  - Projecting to the host's current value. It leads the reload flag and its sounds by half the RTT, so the meter jumps at a reload start and sits full before vanishing.
  - Interpolating samples behind the timeline, at any delay. Below interval plus jitter (about 90 ms at the profile) it starves and holds under jitter; at or above it, the meter trails the flag and the sounds and vanishes short of full.
  - A separate client-local projected slot, as the damage bearing is decided to use (`networking.md` §Presentation events vs. replicated state). It would make every HUD rebind to get a smooth meter.
  - A default tween on the HUD binding lags in every role and leaves an engine symptom to mod policy.
  - Advancing at the client's local tuning rate breaks `networking.md`'s no-local-fallback rule.

## Open questions
- The hold cap's value. — **delegated**: more than two of the longest stamp gaps plus ordinary link jitter, by at least one tick; report the value and the profile it was checked against in the plan of record.
- The slope-agreement tolerance. — **delegated**: at least the timer's whole-millisecond carry (about 3% at 60 Hz); report it in the plan of record.

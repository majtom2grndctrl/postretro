# E17--enemy-mover-interaction

Brief · resumable · Epic 17 · reads: `context/lib/movement.md` §6 · `context/lib/entity_model.md` §7, §7c · `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Navigation bake, §PRL section IDs · `context/lib/networking.md` §Current contract · read at 7dec6ed0

## Problem

Owner-requested capability, Epic 17: enemies treat movers as solid and as ground, as players already do. Today agent motion sweeps static geometry only and keeps a bare grounded flag, and the nav mesh is baked without movers, so enemies walk through closed doors, stand inside or fall through lifts, are never carried or pushed, and a crusher damages an enemy it merely overlaps in open air. When done, closed doors stop enemies and their routes; lifts, platforms and a room rising into place carry them; a `displace` mover pushes them clear; a mover pinning one holds or backs off exactly as for a player; `crush` damages them only when pinned or engulfed; and enemies route across a docked lift. Derivation: `research.md`.

## Decisions

- **Shared mover-contact primitives, separate controllers.** Carry, displace, release velocity and the push-blocked test become body-agnostic `postretro-physics` primitives that the player substrate and the agent controller both call, in the same order: carry → displace → combined sweep → ground ref → release. Players and enemies share geometry primitives, not controllers (`done/movement--static-overlap-invariant`); the agent keeps its own controller. Agent body motion moves from `sim` into physics, which owns movement and mover collision (`development_guide.md` §Workspace); this is the movement churn `done/gameplay-stack--ai-and-physics-crates` named as the leaf-crate risk, accepted (owner, 2026-11-06). Body-side carry matches `movement.md` §6. The player path stays bit-identical.
- **Agent ground is a `GroundRef`.** `AgentComponent.is_grounded: bool` becomes a ground reference with an `is_grounded()` projection. It is the single source for carry, rider exemption, stuck suppression and link resolution.
- **Enemies follow the player block rules (owner, 2026-10-30).** `displace` pushes an unpinned enemy clear. A pinned living enemy makes `displace` and `stop` hold and `reverse` back off, exactly as a pinned player does; no non-crush mover damages or embeds a living enemy. `crush` damages enemies only when pinned against static geometry or another mover, or engulfed, and keeps damaging past death. A rider never stops or reverses its own mover. This supersedes `movement.md` §6 Moving bases (agents grounded against static geometry alone) and Pinned bodies ("agents are never carried or displaced"), and reverses `done/E17--rider-not-a-blocking-contact` AC8 for riders.
- **A pinned enemy steps clear (owner, 2026-11-06).** A living agent pinned by a non-crush mover drops `blocked` and is moved out of that mover's course through the protected knockback layer, which acts whatever its authored motion verb (`Hold` and `Freeze` included); authored motion resumes once clear. A hold always ends and the mover docks.
- **Corpses ride, never block (owner, 2026-11-06).** A corpse is any quiescent agent: zero HP, or committed to removal by a queued despawn. It keeps gravity, carry and displacement but no steering. It never stops, reverses or holds a mover, and a pin against it is no yield. `crush` still damages it. Diverges from the player rule "dead pawns count": a dead player's assist revive is engine behavior, while a corpse's fate (despawn, gib, revive) is mod policy.
- **Crush effects run in a fixed order.** Multi-victim damage, `Crushed` events and impact callbacks are deterministic across runs. Owed here because agent pins rewrite the crush contact set whose order varies today.
- **Crush outcomes stay mod policy (owner, 2026-11-06).** A corpse under a crusher keeps raising impact facts at the cadence, so a mod gibs it from an impact policy on the crush damage type. An enemy whose health resists the crush type at 0 is crush-immune: the crusher passes through it unharmed, as it does a player at `crush_damage` 0. An agent left inside a mover (an immune enemy under a crusher, a corpse revived inside a closed door) is pushed out by its own displacement on the first tick the push is not blocked; while a non-crush mover still pins it, that mover holds as for any living agent. No new script surface: `health.resist`, `crush_damage_type` and impact policies already express both.
- **Docked means at rest at a waypoint (owner, 2026-11-06).** A mover is docked at a waypoint while it rests there: a timed wait, a finished run, a `goToPathNode` hold, never started, or held there by a pin after a command to leave. Touching a waypoint within one tick's motion (`wait_ms` 0, a pass-through) is no dock. Camera occlusion keeps its stricter `mover_is_docked_closed`.
- **Doors and lifts are baked into the nav mesh (owner, 2026-11-06).** prl-build poses each mover at every waypoint of its path, splits regions so the posed solid geometry falls across whole portals, and emits two record kinds: a *mover gate* (this portal is closed while mover m is docked at waypoint k) and a *mover link* (an edge from m's walkable top docked at k to a landing region). The runtime only switches baked records from dock state. This is "baked over computed" (`index.md` §2), the `done/E17--doors-as-occluders` pattern, and the direction `build_pipeline.md` §Navigation bake names. The NavMesh section bumps to version 3 and every map rebakes; a stale section degrades loudly as today. Undo cost: another version bump.
- **Closed doors are impassable to planning.** A gated portal refuses crossings; regions are never gated, so an agent beside a closed door plans out on its own side and a goal beside one is reached from its own side (owner, 2026-11-06). A gate covers only portals an agent capsule cannot pass against the posed solid geometry, so a hollow mover's doorway stays open. Paths, reachability and replans respect the gate, so the brain fact `targetReachable` means reachable now, false while a docked door cuts the only route (owner, 2026-11-06); `scripting.md` §11 and the scripting reference say so. Enemies detour or latch `blocked` and hold. The gate changes only on dock and undock, never on live posed bounds, so sweepers, crushers and mid-travel doors stay collision's job.
- **Records carry identity, not behavior.** Each gate and link names its mover and waypoint, and a gate's portal names the regions on each side. That is the substrate roadmap **Enemy door operation** reads for door-aware behavior and its brain-facing surface; this brief adds no script surface.
- **Gate changes replan first (owner, 2026-11-06).** Agents whose route a gate change affects, and every agent latched `blocked` when a gate clears, are admitted ahead of target drift within the existing replan budget.
- **Lifts ride baked links.** A link is open only while its mover is docked at its waypoint. Enemies board only an already docked mover, ride with steering and stuck detection suspended, and exit where it next docks at a waypoint with a link toward their goal. This covers lifts, multi-stop lifts and the rising room, kept in this brief (owner, 2026-11-06).
- **Host-only.** Agents are host-simulated (`networking.md`); no wire, prediction or digest change. Only the PRL NavMesh bytes change; gate and link state is host-only, read by nothing a client runs.
- **Non-goals.**
  - Remote presentation of riding enemies: roadmap **Co-op presentation of NPCs on moving geometry**. Until it lands a remote client draws a riding enemy off its mover by RTT/2 plus interpolation delay times mover speed (about 30–50 cm at 150 ms on a 3 m/s lift); host and listen-server views are exact.
  - Enemies opening doors or summoning lifts, and a costed wait-at-door link: roadmap **Enemy door operation**.
  - Movers blocking shots and sight: `drafts/E17--movers-block-shots-and-sight/`. The two land in either order (owner, 2026-11-06); landed first, this brief leaves an enemy held at a closed door able to see and fire through it until that one lands, which is accepted.
  - Nav gates and links for movers that spin while docked: no single pose to bake. They get none; collision and the blocking pass handle them. Roadmap **Enemy nav on spinning movers and jump hints** (owner, 2026-11-06).
  - Carrying agent yaw on rotating movers, `carry_yaw` included: a moving or engaged agent's facing re-derives from the AI facing slew; an idle rider or a corpse keeps its world yaw.
  - Agents using teleporters: only player pawns resolve teleporters (`entity_model.md` §7).

## Acceptance

### Automated
- [ ] A closed mover stops an agent at its skin; once open, the agent passes. A `displace` mover pushes an unpinned agent clear (O40).
- [ ] An agent grounded on a moving mover, with or without a destination, reports mover ground every tick, and its displacement equals the mover's carry within 1e-4 m across start, reverse and stop. A descender beyond ground-stick reach drops it under gravity; stepping off adds release velocity that decays with knockback drag (O2–O5, O33).
- [ ] Riders, including within the airborne grace, never stop or reverse their own mover; a non-rider agent in the sweep still triggers `stop` and `reverse` (O6).
- [ ] A non-crush mover pinning a living agent holds (`displace`, `stop`), or backs off when its reversed course pins nothing and holds when it pins a body (`reverse`), with no damage and no embedding; an agent and a player pinned on one tick get one decision (O9a–O9c).
- [ ] A `crush` mover carrying an agent through open air deals no damage; pinned against static geometry, another mover, or engulfed in one tick, the agent takes damage at the cadence past death (O7–O9).
- [ ] Multi-victim crush yields identical damage, event and callback order across runs, with at least four victims over at least eight runs (O10).
- [ ] A corpse in a `stop` or `reverse` doorway never holds or reverses the door; a corpse on a starting lift rides it; `crush` still damages a pinned corpse. A living agent with a queued despawn behaves as a corpse. An agent killed while another mover pins it holds that mover that tick; the hold releases in the next tick's blocking pass and the mover moves on the tick after (O11, O11a, O39, O47).
- [ ] With the only route closed, a chasing agent latches `blocked` and holds: position spread ≤ 0.05 m over 5 s, stuck recovery fires at most once. The door reaching its open waypoint, it passes within 2 s. Two gate flips before admission cost one replan. A failed replan whose old path crosses no gate keeps that path, as today (O12–O14, O28).
- [ ] Sixteen agents chasing through one door that docks closed each detour or latch `blocked` within ⌈16 / replan budget⌉ ticks of the flip, and none fires stuck recovery more than once after it (O29).
- [ ] On `stress-env-volumes` with every agent chasing a moving player, each agent whose route a door's dock cuts detours or latches `blocked`, and each agent latched `blocked` by it replans after it leaves, within ⌈N / replan budget⌉ ticks of the flip (O29, O43).
- [ ] A `ping_pong` lift returning onto an agent that waits `blocked` at its boarding spot docks within 2 s, and the agent then boards; the same holds for an agent whose behavior is in `Freeze` (O16, O42).
- [ ] No path crosses a docked door: start and goal on opposite sides get no path; an agent beside a closed door plans out on its own side and a goal beside one is reached from its own side; a kept path crossing it is dropped. The rising room docked raised gates no portal its walls do not block, and an agent inside routes out through its doorway (O28, O30, O41, O44).
- [ ] A brain whose target a door cuts off reads `targetReachable` false by its next acquisition-due tick, and true by the next one after the door leaves; Ai, combat scoring and steering read one gate generation per tick (O31, O32).
- [ ] An agent routes across a docked lift: boards, rides with steering and stuck detection paused, exits at the far terminus and replans. A lift leaving mid-board carries a grounded agent and otherwise closes the link (O15–O18).
- [ ] A rider whose lift leaves mid-exit or stops mid-shaft never walks off into the shaft and fires no stuck recovery while riding; a rider knocked off resumes steering and replans; an agent raised by a riser rides as a boarded one; no replan is admitted mid-ride (O34–O38).
- [ ] A rider whose target is reachable from the lift's next dock reads `targetReachable` true on every acquisition-due tick of the ride (O38, O48).
- [ ] An agent whose route crosses a lift docked flush with the floor walks across without stopping, and rides only if the lift leaves while it is on it (O37, O45).
- [ ] After unloading a level whose closed door gates a route and loading one that routes through the same region index, that route succeeds on the first tick (O49).
- [ ] A `ping_pong` door and a `ping_pong` lift gate and link on every tick that begins and ends inside a waypoint wait, and stop on the tick they leave (O24, O46).
- [ ] A door spawned closed and never moved gates routes from the first tick (O25).
- [ ] A closed door commanded open but held at its closed waypoint by a body in its opening course keeps its gate; the gate clears on the tick the door leaves. A `reverse` mover pinned at a middle-node dock backs off and undocks there on that tick (O26, O50).
- [ ] A `ping_pong` sweeper with `wait_ms` 0 and a door held mid-travel run a full cycle with no gate generation change and no replan admitted for them (O27).
- [ ] A mover sent by `goToPathNode` to a mid-path node and held there gates and links at that node as at a terminus: a door held closed there blocks routes, and an agent boards or exits a lift stopped there; a mover passing through the node without stopping does neither (O22, O23).
- [ ] A mover with no waypoints, or with authored spin, produces no gate and no link (O20). A level with zero movers keeps agent motion, steering and paths identical to today: a headless dump of a mover-free map with agents is byte-identical to one captured before the first slice (O21).
- [ ] Player movement and the predict/reconcile suites pass unchanged, and a recorded player run on a mover map (carry, displace, release, ground-stick) produces bit-identical positions and ground refs before and after the move to shared primitives.
- [ ] The source-text gate fails if the agent controller holds its own carry, displacement, release velocity or push-blocked test; both controllers call the shared primitives.
- [ ] The NavMesh section round-trips gates and links at version 3; a version 2 section is rejected with a warning; two bakes of one map are byte-identical; a map with no movers bakes the same regions and portals as before with empty gate and link tables. - [ ] A crush-immune enemy (health resists the crush type at 0) under a crusher takes no damage, and is pushed clear on the first tick the crusher no longer pins it. ## Path

- **First slice:** move `sim/src/agent/mod.rs::collide_and_slide` into physics and generalize `movement/mover_carry.rs` (`apply_mover_carry`, `displace_from_movers`, `apply_mover_release_velocity`, `mover_push_is_blocked_by_static`) plus `ground_ref_from_hit` behind `substrate.rs::integrate_collision`, behavior-preserving. Then thread `CombinedCollisionWorld` into `agent_steering::tick` at SteerAgents (built as player movement builds it). This falsifies the riskiest premise: steering and stuck recovery do not fight the shared substrate (door jitter, lift self-bounce, stuck loop).
- Release velocity enters `knockback_velocity`. The idle (no-destination) branch of `tick_impl` has its own `collide_and_slide` call and takes the same mover contact. Stuck detection pauses while riding. Corpses need a motion-only branch, since `tick_impl` drops quiescent agents today; physics needs its own quiescence test (`is_quiescent` is `sim`-private). `fixed_tick_queued_despawn_quiesces_brain_agent_steering` is rewritten to assert no steering only.
- Blocking pass: agent tuples carry their ground ref; `pin.rs::pawn_bodies` and `run_mover_pin_pretest` take living agents; crush uses `PinWorld::push_is_blocked` plus `point_inside_mover` on the capsule axis for engulfment; `pinned_crush_contacts` becomes ordered or is sorted; `agent_capsules` keeps corpses for crush only. Rival: a separate agent pin module, rejected because it duplicates the predicate.
- Bake: `pipeline.rs` already extracts mover geometry and encodes `kinematic_waypoints` before the navmesh stage, so `navmesh_bake::bake_navmesh` can take them; its stage cache key gains mover geometry and waypoints, and `NAVMESH_STAGE_VERSION` bumps. Split regions at each posed mover's solid footprint in the agent band [floor + step height, floor + agent height] so a lift top at floor level splits nothing; test each candidate portal by sweeping the baked agent capsule across it against the posed mesh. Links come from the mover's upward walkable triangles posed at the waypoint, joined to regions within step height and agent radius. Waypoint index is the mover's resolved path order, as the runtime's `segment_index`. Strongest rival: the load-time gate (`research.md` §Rivals).
- Runtime gate: `sim/src/nav/gate.rs`, a reusable bitset over baked gates and links with a generation counter. A public docked-at-waypoint predicate beside `mover_is_docked_closed`, read from phase every tick rather than from `terminus_events`, which fire only at the two ends (the terminus helpers are private; a `goToPathNode` arrival sets `completed` and clears `target_segment`). The gate updates once per tick after the mover tick and before Ai. A kept path that crosses a gated portal is dropped, since `agent_steering` keeps a path through a failed replan today. `NavGraph` loads the gate and link tables; `find_path` takes the gate (callers: `agent_steering`, `ai/src/compute.rs`, `combat_positioning/scoring.rs`); a generation change marks affected agents drift-priority in `admit_replans`; `endpoints_are_topologically_connected` and `blocked_destination_now_directly_routable` consult it; `region_components` becomes a static superset.
- Links: an open link is an extra edge in A*. An agent on a docked mover resolves its region through the link; `region_at` otherwise picks the floor under it in XZ. Board → Ride → Exit in a new `agent_steering/mover_link.rs`.
- Size: `agent_steering.rs` and `sim/mod.rs` are past ~800 lines and take threading edits only, new logic in siblings; `blocking.rs` splits along the player/agent seam first if it would pass ~1000.
- `agent/overlap_tests.rs::an_agent_ignores_a_mover_top_its_pawn_neighbour_steps_onto` asserts static-only agents and is rewritten. `the_agent_controller_holds_no_step_up_or_snap_to_floor_of_its_own` is the source-text gate for shared primitives: it moves with the controller and keeps guarding, now covering the mover-contact primitives too.
- Allocation: `tick_impl` and `agent_capsules` already allocate per tick, so the probe scopes to the new paths. The counting allocator is installed only in `postretro-sim` test builds; a probe in a physics or ai test reads zero whatever runs.
- At promotion: `scripting.md` §11 (`targetReachable` reads door state); `movement.md` §6 Moving bases and Pinned bodies (agents carried and pinned; corpses ride, never block; dock meaning); `build_pipeline.md` §Navigation bake (mover splits, gates and links; "additive, no format break" corrected, since a record kind bumps the version) and §PRL section IDs (NavMesh version 3).

## Wire format

NavMesh section (id 36), version 3. Mirrors the version 2 layout (`level-format/src/navmesh.rs`): little-endian, unsigned `u32` counts and indices, `f32` coordinates, each table a `u32` count followed by fixed-stride entries, an empty table encoded as count 0. Version 2 body is unchanged; two tables append after the portals.

| Order | Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–n | version 2 body | — | `version` field reads 3 |
| n+1 | `gate_count` | u32 | |
| n+2 | gates | 12 bytes each | `mover_id` u32, `waypoint` u32, `portal` u32 (index into portals). Sorted by (`mover_id`, `waypoint`, `portal`), unique |
| n+3 | `link_count` | u32 | |
| n+4 | links | 36 bytes each | `mover_id` u32, `waypoint` u32, `region` u32, `left` f32×3, `right` f32×3 (the boarding edge, oriented mover → region as portal handedness). Sorted by (`mover_id`, `waypoint`, `region`, `left` by `to_bits`), unique |

`mover_id` is `KinematicMoverRecord.mover_id`. A gate or link naming an absent mover, a waypoint past its path, or an out-of-range portal or region rejects the section, as other index faults do.

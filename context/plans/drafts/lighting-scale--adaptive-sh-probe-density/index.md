# Lighting Scale — Adaptive SH Probe Density

> **Status:** draft. Spike-led thin vertical slice. **Gated on its own Task 1 measurement** — Tasks 2+ proceed only if the spike confirms adaptive density dissolves the direct-SH delta overflow.
> **Track:** Lighting / build pipeline — the O(n³) root-cause lever behind the direct-SH delta storage-buffer wall.
> **Related:** `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Octahedral irradiance volume bake, §PRL section IDs (id 34/35 base atlases, id 27/41 deltas) · `context/lib/rendering_pipeline.md` §4 (Indirect / Animated SH delta volumes / Baked static direct), §7.1 (SH compose) · `context/plans/drafts/lighting-scale--sh-delta-footprint-instrumentation/` (the measurement prerequisite this consumes) · `context/plans/done/sh-array-atlas/` (base-atlas D2→D2Array, already landed) · `context/plans/done/perf-animated-sh-light-culling/` (the sparse-CSR affinity-cell delta form this extends) · `context/lib/experimental_spikes.md`.

## Goal

The SH probe/delta grid is **uniform** — a `grid_x × grid_y × grid_z` grid at a fixed probe spacing (default 1.0 m), so probe count scales O(n³) and most probes sit in empty air far from any surface. That uniform grid is the root cause of the direct-SH delta storage-buffer wall: a stress map at dense settings baked a **~1.22 GiB** direct-SH delta storage buffer, overflowing the engine's self-imposed 512 MiB `max_storage_buffer_binding_size` and failing wgpu bind-group validation at load. Replace uniform density with **adaptive** density — probes dense near surfaces, sparse in open air — to cut baked SH delta storage substantially (industry precedent for adaptive probe volumes is ~an order of magnitude) and dissolve that wall. This spec leads with a measurement spike that decides whether adaptive density is the right lever before committing the broad, cross-subsystem change; if it succeeds, it may **obviate** the gated `sh-delta-texture-array` fallback spec (state below).

## Scope

This is a **large, broad-blast-radius** change touching the SH bake, the delta section format, both compose compute passes, the SH sampler shaders, and the renderer upload path. It is deliberately scoped as a **thin vertical slice, spike-first** — not a monolithic build-out of a full adaptive probe volume. The justification and the deferred-breadth fork are in Rough sketch → *Slice shape*.

### In scope

- **Task 1 spike (gate).** A bake-time, output-preserving measurement pass that classifies the emitted direct-SH delta affinity cells by surface proximity and reports the **projected** delta footprint under a first-cut surface-biased cull/coarsen — does adaptive density shrink the direct-SH delta under 512 MiB (ideally under the 128 MiB WebGPU spec floor) on the stress map at dense settings? Measured-finding output; changes no baked bytes. Gates everything below.
- **One adaptive mechanism, end-to-end through the live direct-SH delta path.** Adaptive subdivision of the affinity/probe grid keyed to geometry/surface proximity: full probe density near surfaces, coarsened (fewer stored probes) in open air, dropped where already-unreachable. The 4×4×4 affinity brick (`AFFINITY_FACTOR = 4`, 64 probes/cell) is the existing primitive — the gap is *subdivision level* + *indirection* + *cap*, not a new primitive.
- **An indirection structure** mapping world position → the correct brick at the correct subdivision level (the Unity APV `ProbeBrickIndex` / Unreal Volumetric Lightmap indirection-texture analog), carried in the direct-SH delta section (version bump) and consumed by the direct compose pass + sampler.
- **A bounded footprint / hard memory cap** on the adaptive delta storage (the Unreal VLM *Maximum Brick Memory* analog). The bake **fails loud** with a named error when adaptive density would exceed the cap, and the loader rejects a section that violates it — the fail-loud property the current uncapped delta path lacks.
- **Runtime integration on the direct path only:** the direct compose compute pass and the direct-SH atlas sampler read through the indirection to fetch the right brick/level for a world position; the renderer uploads the bounded pool + indirection buffers.
- **Logging discipline** (see AC): one-line footprint/probe-count/subdivision summaries at `log::info` in prl-build; detailed per-cell/per-level breakdowns gated behind `-v/--verbose`; runtime footprint logged once per load, never per frame.
- **Task N findings note** (spike deliverable): measured footprint vs. the 512 MiB target and 128 MiB floor, a manual-visual quality read, and a recommendation — does the thin slice suffice, is the full adaptive tree warranted as a follow-on sibling-spec milestone, and is `sh-delta-texture-array` now obviated.

### Out of scope (non-goals)

- **The indirect `DeltaShVolumes` (id 27) path.** Animated-light *indirect* deltas use the same affinity-CSR shape and would benefit from the same mechanism, but the thin slice proves adaptivity on the **direct** delta (id 41) — the path that actually overflowed. Extending to id 27 is deferred breadth, recommended in the findings note, not built here.
- **Adaptive packing of the base SH atlases (id 34 `OctahedralShVolume`, id 35 `DirectShVolume`).** These are already `texture_2d_array` (the `sh-array-atlas` work landed); their overflow was a 2D-dimension limit solved by array layers, not the storage-buffer wall. The thin slice does not re-pack the base atlases adaptively; it consumes the base probe grid as-is. Unifying adaptive density across base atlas + delta is deferred breadth.
- **A full multi-level APV/VLM subdivision tree.** The slice ships **one** adaptivity axis (surface-proximity density level over the existing 4×4×4 brick), not an arbitrary-depth octree of bricks with per-node refinement. Deeper subdivision, if the spike shows it is needed, is the follow-on milestone.
- **Per-cluster streaming / residency of adaptive bricks.** Cell-alignable structure should not be actively fought, but load/evict is a separate epic.
- **Runtime re-subdivision when lights move.** Animated/selected lights are static-positioned in the bake; adaptivity is a bake-time spatial decision.
- **Lowering `REQUIRED_STORAGE_BUFFER_BINDING_SIZE`.** The raised 512 MiB limit stays requested as headroom; this plan makes it non-load-bearing on the direct-SH path, it does not remove it.
- **Probe/SH order changes.** SH order, tile geometry (`tile_dimension = 6`, `tile_border = 1`), and the octahedral encoding are unchanged; only *which probes are stored at what density* changes.
- **Bake-time reduction.** SH bake is a separate perf thread; adaptivity is neutral-to-favorable but bake wall-clock is not a goal here.

## Acceptance criteria

Split per `experimental_spikes.md`: honesty gates (the experiment ran correctly) vs. measured findings (the numbers the experiment exists to learn). Named identifiers and line numbers stay out of AC.

**Task 1 spike — measured finding (gate):**

- [ ] Building the stress map at dense settings (`content/dev/maps/stress-warren-maze-crates.map` with `--sh-probe-spacing 1.33 --lightmap-density 0.2 --soft-shadow-samples 64`) at `RUST_LOG=info` prints, without changing any baked bytes, the **current** direct-SH delta footprint and the **projected** footprint under the first-cut surface-biased cull/coarsen, plus the projected footprint's ratio to 512 MiB and to 128 MiB. A byte-for-byte diff of the `.prl` compiled with and without this task is empty (spike is measurement-only).
- [ ] The projected-footprint report states explicitly whether adaptive density is projected to bring the direct-SH delta under 512 MiB on this map at these settings — the recorded go/no-go finding that gates Tasks 2+ and the `sh-delta-texture-array` decision. This is a recorded value feeding the recommendation, not a pass/fail threshold.

**Tasks 2+ (thin slice) — honesty gates:**

- [ ] The stress map at dense settings compiles to a `.prl` end-to-end with the adaptive direct-SH delta section; the emitted section's declared per-cell subdivision/level metadata is internally consistent with its stored brick pool (bake-time assert re-validated at load: every referenced pool region is in-bounds, every cell's level resolves to a stored brick or an explicit empty).
- [ ] The bake **fails loud** with a clear, named "exceeds adaptive delta memory cap — reduce density or raise cap" error (non-zero exit) when adaptive density would exceed the hard memory cap, instead of emitting an oversized section. Reproduced by setting the cap below the map's need.
- [ ] Loading a `.prl` whose adaptive direct-SH delta section violates the cap or has an out-of-bounds indirection entry logs a clear reject error and disables the direct-SH delta gracefully (matching the existing direct-delta all-or-nothing reject posture), never a wgpu validation crash.
- [ ] The pre-bump direct-SH delta section version is rejected at load with a clear "recompile with current prl-build" error, mirroring the existing version-mismatch reject path.
- [ ] The adapted stress-map `.prl` boots and reaches the first rendered frame with no wgpu validation errors, with the direct-SH delta storage bindings within requested limits.
- [ ] **No per-frame log noise.** The runtime logs the adaptive direct-SH delta footprint (per-binding byte sizes plus total) **once per load**; a grep of a multi-second run's log shows the footprint line at most once per map load. prl-build prints one-line info summaries for the adaptive delta (footprint, probe/cell counts, subdivision-level histogram) and gates detailed per-cell/per-level breakdowns behind `-v/--verbose`; a default (non-verbose) build adds no new per-cell spew.

**Tasks 2+ (thin slice) — measured findings:**

- [ ] The baked direct-SH delta footprint on the stress map at dense settings is measured and reported against the target: **under 512 MiB** (the wall this dissolves), with the margin to the 128 MiB spec floor recorded. Landing over 512 MiB is a recorded finding feeding the recommendation (fork to the full adaptive tree or to `sh-delta-texture-array`), not a silent failure.
- [ ] A manual-visual A/B on the stress map (dev-tools direct-SH / entity-shadow view, DDGI depth-visibility weighting active) shows no objectionable artifact, banding, or popping in baked-static-direct-lit entity regions versus the uniform-grid baseline. An eyeball check — parity to the uniform bake is explicitly **not** the goal (the uniform grid is the baseline being abandoned); the check watches for adaptivity seams where density changes.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Surface-proximity footprint spike (measure-and-report; gate)

Add a bake-time analysis pass to the direct-SH delta bake (`bake_direct_sh_delta_volumes`, `crates/level-compiler/src/direct_sh_bake.rs`) that runs **after** the current sparse CSR is built and **before** the section is returned, changing no emitted bytes. For each emitted affinity cell (one 4×4×4 base-probe brick), classify its distance to the nearest surface using data the compiler already holds — the world geometry / BVH the SH bake already reads — into at least: *near-surface* (keep full 64-probe density), *open-air* (coarsen candidate), *deep-interior/unreachable* (drop candidate, largely already clipped by the portal-reachable region). Compute the **projected** `delta_subblocks` byte total if open-air cells stored a coarser brick (e.g. one level coarser, ~8 probes) and drop-candidates stored nothing, using the per-entry payload identity (`PROBES_PER_CELL × delta_probe_f16_stride(tile_dimension) × 2` = 18,432 B for the default 6-texel tile — derive from the format constants, do not hard-code). Log at `log::info`: current footprint, projected footprint, the projected ratio to 512 MiB and to 128 MiB, and a per-class cell histogram; gate the per-cell detail behind `-v/--verbose`. The deliverable is the recorded go/no-go finding: does surface-biased density project under 512 MiB on the stress map at dense settings. **This gates Tasks 2+ and the `sh-delta-texture-array` decision.** Because the spike is measurement-only it does not touch the oversized bake files' write paths — no split needed for this task.

### Task 2: Adaptive subdivision + indirection format contract (bake + section format)

This task locks the on-disk contract everything downstream compiles against, so it lands before the runtime work. Extend the direct-SH delta section (`DirectShDeltaVolumesSection`, `crates/level-format/src/direct_sh_delta_volumes.rs`, id 41) to carry, per affinity cell, a **subdivision/density level** and an **indirection** from cell → its stored brick's offset in a variable-size brick pool (replacing today's fixed 1-brick-per-CSR-entry assumption where every `(cell, light)` entry is a dense 64-probe sub-block). Semantics of the contract (constraints, not a byte layout — the implementer pins offsets when landing): (a) a per-cell level selected at bake time from surface proximity, with full density near surfaces and a coarser brick in open air; (b) an indirection entry per cell resolving a world-position lookup (world pos → base probe → affinity cell → level + pool offset) to the stored brick or an explicit "empty" sentinel; (c) the brick pool laid out so a cell's whole brick is contiguous, never split. Bump `DIRECT_SH_DELTA_VOLUMES_VERSION`; the loader rejects the prior version with the existing named mismatch error (no compat shim — pre-release). Rework the bake in `direct_sh_bake.rs` so each selected light emits, per reached affinity cell, a brick at that cell's level (surface-proximity classification from Task 1, promoted from projection to actual emission) instead of an unconditional 64-probe sub-block. Preserve the existing x-fastest in-cell probe ordering for full-density bricks so the coincident base-probe point-read stays 1:1; define the coarse brick's probe subset and its in-brick order as part of the contract. Reuse the affinity decomposition and portal-reachable clip already in `crates/level-compiler/src/affinity_grid.rs` (`AFFINITY_FACTOR = 4`, `decompose_affinity_for_lights`, `per_light_cells`). Emit one-line info summaries (footprint, per-level cell histogram) with `-v/--verbose` detail. **Split-before-extend:** `direct_sh_bake.rs` is ~2071 lines; sequence a behavior-preserving split of its delta-bake region along existing seams immediately before this task's extension (see Sequencing) — gated on Task 1's go, so a 2000-line file is not churned for a mechanism the spike may retire.

### Task 3: Bounded memory cap + fail-loud enforcement (bake + load)

Introduce a hard cap on the total adaptive direct-SH delta brick-pool bytes (the Unreal VLM *Maximum Brick Memory* analog), expressed in the bake config and surfaced as a prl-build knob with a sensible default (well under 512 MiB). During Task 2's emission, accumulate the running pool size; if adaptive density at the chosen levels would exceed the cap, the bake **fails loud** with a named, actionable error (which map, which cap, how far over) and a non-zero exit — it never silently emits an oversized section (the failure mode the current uncapped path has). At load, the direct-delta loader re-validates the section against the cap and against indirection bounds, and on violation clears the direct-SH delta all-or-nothing (matching the existing posture where a malformed/partial direct delta disables the whole path so a selected light never runs as both baked direct SH and runtime light) rather than trusting a bad section into a wgpu binding. This task owns the fail-loud AC and the load-reject AC.

### Task 4: Runtime compose + sampler integration through the indirection (direct path)

Wire the adaptive section through the live direct-SH path so the slice runs end-to-end. In the renderer, upload the bounded brick pool and the indirection buffers as storage buffers in the direct compose bind group, alongside the existing direct-delta bindings (`crates/renderer/src/render/direct_sh_compose.rs`, ~431 lines — under the split threshold, extend in place). Change the direct compose compute pass and the shared octahedral direct-SH sampler so that, for a given world position / base probe, they resolve the affinity cell, read the cell's level + pool offset from the indirection, and fetch the brick at that level (a full-density brick point-reads as today; a coarse brick reconstructs the cell's probes from its stored subset — define the reconstruction as the read-side mirror of Task 2's coarse-brick contract). Keep the direct-composed atlas write path (`direct_sh_compose` writing the `D2Array` composed direct atlas) intact — adaptivity changes what feeds the compose, not the composed-atlas output shape. Emit the runtime per-binding footprint **once per load** (mirror the indirect path's `ComposeStorageFootprint` log, labeled unambiguously as the direct path) — never per frame. Touches the renderer SH upload code in `crates/renderer/src/render/sh_volume.rs` (~1986 lines): if this task extends that file's delta-upload region rather than adding a sibling, sequence a behavior-preserving split of that region first (see Sequencing).

### Task 5: Run, measure, decide (findings note)

Compile and run the stress map at dense settings end-to-end. Measure and record: baked direct-SH delta footprint vs. the 512 MiB target and the 128 MiB floor; the per-level cell distribution and effective probes-stored vs. the uniform-grid probe count (the storage-shrink factor, comparable to the industry ~10× claim); compose dispatch time before/after with `POSTRETRO_GPU_TIMING=1`; and the manual-visual A/B read. Confirm the honesty-gate ACs (clean boot, version reject, cap fail-loud, load reject, no per-frame log noise). The deliverable is the findings note: does the thin slice suffice as shipped; should adaptivity be extended as a follow-on sibling-spec milestone to the indirect `DeltaShVolumes` (id 27) and the base atlases (id 34/35); is a deeper multi-level subdivision tree warranted; and — the load-bearing decision — **is the gated `sh-delta-texture-array` spec now obviated** because adaptive density fits the direct-SH delta under the storage-buffer limit without a texture-array migration. Record the recommendation; do not silently contract any number.

## Sequencing

**Phase 1 (sequential, gate):** Task 1 — the measurement spike. Its recorded go/no-go finding gates every task below. If the spike projects the direct-SH delta does **not** fit under 512 MiB under surface-biased density, **stop and fork**: either escalate to the full multi-level adaptive tree as a sibling-spec milestone (spike as spec 1, per the E19 pattern) or fall back to the gated `sh-delta-texture-array` spec. Tasks 2–5 assume the spike said go.

**Phase 1.5 (sequential, conditional split-first):** Before Task 2 extends `direct_sh_bake.rs` (~2071 lines) and before Task 4 extends `sh_volume.rs` (~1986 lines), land behavior-preserving splits of their SH-delta regions along existing seams. Gated on Task 1's go so oversized files are not churned for a retired mechanism. Skip a split only if the extending task adds a sibling module rather than growing the file.

**Phase 2 (sequential):** Task 2 — the format + bake contract everything downstream compiles against. Blocks Tasks 3 and 4.

**Phase 3 (concurrent):** Task 3 (cap + fail-loud, bake + load) and Task 4 (runtime compose + sampler + upload) — independent once the Task 2 contract is locked; Task 3 is a bake/load guard, Task 4 is the GPU read path.

**Phase 4 (sequential):** Task 5 — observes the integrated system; only meaningful once Tasks 3 and 4 land.

## Rough sketch

**Slice shape — thin vertical slice, spike-first (chosen over a sibling-spec milestone, with justification).** This change crosses the SH bake, the delta format, both compose passes, the sampler shaders, and the renderer upload — the same broad integration surface the roadmap says should "land alone and become the integration surface" (Epic 17 A wave guidance) or be split into a small milestone of sibling specs (Epic 19). We choose the **thin vertical slice**: the spike gates, then **one** adaptive mechanism (surface-proximity density level over the existing 4×4×4 brick + indirection + cap) runs end-to-end through **only** the direct-SH delta path — the path that actually overflowed — with the indirection/format contract locked and breadth deferred. Rationale: the uniform→adaptive change is fundamentally a new indirection *contract* that everything downstream depends on; proving it once through the live direct path locks that contract with real data before fanning out. Specifying the whole adaptive system (base atlas + both deltas + multi-level tree + streaming) as one task list is exactly the monolith the guidance warns against. **The spike can fork the plan to shape (b):** if the direct-path slice succeeds but the findings show the base atlas and indirect delta need the same treatment, the follow-on is a sibling-spec milestone (id 27 delta, id 34/35 base atlas, deeper subdivision) with this slice's contract as the substrate — the E19 pattern, decided on data, not up front.

**The primitive already exists; the gap is subdivision + indirection + cap.** `AFFINITY_FACTOR = 4` gives a 4×4×4, 64-probe brick (`PROBES_PER_CELL = 64`), and the direct-SH delta already stores one dense brick per `(affinity cell, selected light)` CSR entry at 18,432 B each (`crates/level-format/src/direct_sh_delta_volumes.rs`, id 41). Unity APV and Unreal VLM are exactly this brick + a subdivision decision (how dense per region) + an indirection (world pos → brick at the right level) + a bounded pool (Maximum Brick Memory). So the build is: keep the brick, add a per-cell surface-proximity **level**, add the **indirection** entry per cell, and cap the **pool**.

**Where the O(n³) win comes from.** The delta already clips each light to its portal-reachable region (`crates/level-compiler/src/affinity_grid.rs`, `decompose_affinity_for_lights` → `per_light_cells`). The remaining waste is that every *reached* cell stores a full 64-probe brick even when it is open air far from any surface, where indirect/bounced light varies slowly and a coarse brick suffices. Coarsening open-air cells is the surface-proximity lever; the ~1.22 GiB ÷ 18,432 B ≈ 71k dense bricks is the population the coarsening thins.

**Contract discipline.** Per the style guide, this spec states constraints (per-cell level; cell→brick indirection resolvable from a world position; whole brick contiguous; hard pool cap; version bump) and leaves byte offsets, the exact level set, and the coarse-brick probe subset to the implementer, who sees the whole picture when landing. The Wire format section pins only endianness/mirror invariants.

**Grounding notes.** `DEFAULT_PROBE_SPACING = 1.0` and the uniform O(n³) grid live in `crates/level-compiler/src/sh_bake.rs`; `REQUIRED_STORAGE_BUFFER_BINDING_SIZE = 512 MiB` in `crates/renderer/src/render/renderer_init_resources.rs`; the direct-SH delta bake is `bake_direct_sh_delta_volumes` in `crates/level-compiler/src/direct_sh_bake.rs`; base atlases id 34/35 are already `texture_2d_array` (the `sh-array-atlas` work; do not re-pack them here). No adaptive/subdivision/indirection/brick-pool concept exists in the SH code today — this is net-new. (The `crates/level-format/src/affinity_grid.rs` path some earlier notes used does not exist; the affinity grid is `crates/level-compiler/src/affinity_grid.rs`, with the on-disk `AFFINITY_FACTOR` mirror in `crates/level-format/src/delta_sh_volumes.rs`.)

## Wire format

New adaptive fields ride the existing direct-SH delta section (`DirectShDeltaVolumesSection`, PRL id 41), which today carries `affinity_factor`, `affinity_dims`, `tile_dimension`, `tile_border`, CSR `affinity_offsets` / `affinity_lights`, and a flat `delta_subblocks` f16 pool. Mirror that section's conventions exactly: little-endian; integer signedness and widths follow the current section; the payload's first byte is `DIRECT_SH_DELTA_VOLUMES_VERSION`, bumped, with the prior version rejected at parse. The adaptive additions — a per-cell subdivision/density level and a per-cell indirection into a variable-stride brick pool — extend rather than replace the CSR: the pool is still flat f16, but a CSR entry's brick is now level-sized (not unconditionally 64 probes), and a per-cell indirection resolves `(affinity cell)` → `(level, pool offset)` or an empty sentinel. Empty-list and "no reached cells" encodings follow the current section (early-return `None`, no payload). Exact field order, offsets, the level enumeration, and the coarse-brick probe count are implementer-pinned at landing (constraints only, per the style guide); the invariant is that a world-position read resolves deterministically to one contiguous brick or an explicit empty, and that the summed pool bytes never exceed the declared cap.

## Open questions / risks

- **Does surface-biased coarsening alone clear 512 MiB?** The whole plan is gated on Task 1 answering this on the stress map. If open-air coarsening is insufficient (e.g. the map is mostly near-surface at dense settings), the slice forks to the full multi-level tree or to `sh-delta-texture-array`. Honest uncertainty — the spike exists to remove it.
- **Coarse-brick sampling seams.** Changing per-cell density introduces a boundary between full and coarse bricks; the compose/sampler must reconstruct a coarse cell's probes without a visible seam against neighboring full cells. Mitigated by the manual-visual A/B AC and by keeping the coarse subset a strict decimation of the full brick's x-fastest order. Residual risk is a mis-resolved indirection reading the wrong level — guarded by the bake/load bounds asserts.
- **Blast radius across three oversized files.** `sh_bake.rs` (2729), `direct_sh_bake.rs` (2071), and `sh_volume.rs` (1986) are all past the split-first threshold. The slice deliberately touches only the direct-delta regions of the latter two and defers the indirect path (which would pull in `sh_bake.rs` and `sh_group.rs`), keeping the split surface bounded. Phase 1.5 sequences the splits, gated on the spike.
- **Obviation of `sh-delta-texture-array`.** If Task 5 confirms the direct-SH delta fits comfortably under the storage-buffer limit adaptively, the gated `sh-delta-texture-array` spec (a texture-array migration of the delta) becomes unnecessary — recorded as an explicit recommendation in the findings note, not silently assumed.
- **Cap default.** The hard memory cap needs a shippable default that is generous enough not to fail typical maps yet tight enough to catch a runaway; Task 5's measurement informs it. Until measured, the cap is a prl-build knob with a conservative default under 512 MiB.

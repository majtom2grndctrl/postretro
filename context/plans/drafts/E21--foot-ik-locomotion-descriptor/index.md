# Foot IK + Locomotion Descriptor

## Goal
Stop feet from sliding and floating. Add a tag-driven foot-IK modifier to the shipped pose-modifier stack, feed it game-logic ground probes, and calibrate playback so stride matches motion. Two independent halves compose:

- **Foot IK** — a two-bone (hip-knee-ankle) analytic solver run once per leg over a model-declared, N-leg-ready leg set, on masked local-TRS joints, consuming per-foot ground probes from `PoseInputs`. Presence of leg tags builds the modifier, exactly as `aimSpine` tags build the aim-pitch bend today; no descriptor gate. A probe miss (airborne, out of reach) falls back to the clip pose — the standard runtime blend-out.
- **Locomotion calibration** — a per-clip `travelSpeed` auto-derived from root motion, an authored per-state override, and a rate rework so speed-scaled playback is exactly `measured / authored` instead of `speed_xz / move_speed`. A `speedScale` archetype toggle turns rate-scaling off for authored-cadence gaits. Enemies inherit the calibration; the shipped E10 speed-scaled walk becomes the degenerate case.

Depends on the shipped `E21--pose-modifier-stack` (`context/plans/done/E21--pose-modifier-stack/`): the stack, `JointMask`, `PoseModifier`/`PoseModifierStack`, `PoseInputs`, the `poseMask` loader path, the `sample_*_modified` samplers, and the tick→collector→renderer plumbing this plan extends. Re-grounded on that landed source.

## Scope

### In scope
- A foot-IK `PoseModifier::FootIk` variant on the stack's closed enum (`crates/model/src/pose_modifier.rs`), holding an ordered leg set and iterating it. Per leg it indexes the matching `FootProbe` in `PoseInputs`, forward-computes hip/ankle world positions by walking the chain's local transforms (the stack's established convention for world-space needs — no collision, no wgpu), solves the knee from the two segment lengths and the hip→target distance (clamped out-of-reach so the leg never hyperextends), writes back hip and knee local rotations, and orients the foot toward the probed normal within an angular limit, blending the solve against the clip pose by a plant weight so a clip-lifted (swing) foot keeps its lift. Each leg is independent; a miss leaves that leg on its clip pose. The leg set, probe array, and solver loop are sized for **N legs**; this plan authors and tests bipeds.
- Game-logic ground probes: a fixed-tick step (beside `update_pose_inputs` in `crates/postretro/src/sim/mod.rs`) that, for each leg-tagged animated entity, samples the unmodified world pose to find each declared foot's world position, casts a short downward ray against the collision world, and writes the per-leg results into the entity's `PoseInputs.feet`.
- A locomotion descriptor authored per archetype (Rust ↔ TS/Luau): an optional `locomotion` block carrying a `speedScale` boolean (default `true`), and a per-state `travelSpeed` override on the animation-state descriptor.
- `travelSpeed` auto-derivation at load from the root-joint translation track (ground units per animated second), the descriptor override taking precedence.
- Reworking speed-scaled playback so the rate is `measured_ground_speed / effective_travel_speed` (clamped), replacing the current `speed_xz / move_speed` reference; the E10 walk is the degenerate case when no root motion, no override, and no `speedScale: false` exist.
- Leg-chain and foot-target joint tagging via glTF `extras`, extending the shipped `poseMask` mechanism: side names for bipeds, indexed names for the N-leg path.
- A behavior-preserving split of `crates/entities/src/components/animation.rs` (~1616 lines) before its playback-rate machinery is extended.
- Headless tests for probe determinism, IK planting on slopes, and `travelSpeed`-driven rate.

### Out of scope
- The pose-modifier stack, masks, aim modifiers, and base plumbing — shipped in the prerequisite.
- A per-archetype IK on/off descriptor field. IK is tag-driven; to disable planting, leave the rig untagged. Dynamic suppression (airborne, dead, out of reach) is the probe-miss fallback, not a descriptor flag.
- A locomotion sync-mode enum. Speed-scaling and IK planting are orthogonal and separately gated (a boolean and tag presence); a three-value `speedScaled`/`ikPlant`/`both` selector would encode a choice neither surface owns.
- Hand IK onto the weapon, full-body IK beyond legs, cloth/hair, facial animation (Epic 21 non-goals).
- Any change to hit-zone authority — leg hit capsules keep reading the unmodified world pose (`sample_*_world`, untouched by the stack).
- Blend trees, directional locomotion, multi-locomotion-state selection — deferred.
- Co-op avatar / weapon presentation and any wire change — the co-op-avatar plan.
- Authoring and tuning monsters with more than two legs, and front legs doubling as melee attackers. The data path here is N-leg-sized; multi-legged content, per-monster leg tuning, and the leg↔melee bridge are the later `Multi-legged & melee-capable legs` spec.
- `travelSpeed` derivation for non-forward clips. Derivation measures root XZ displacement magnitude; a turn-in-place or strafe clip with near-zero net root displacement carries a descriptor `travelSpeed` override instead.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A character whose rig is leg-tagged keeps both feet in contact with flat ground across a walk cycle — no visible sink or float — while a foot lifted by the clip still lifts.
- [ ] On a slope within the walkable limit, each foot plants to the slope surface at the correct height and orients toward the ground normal; the solve rotates only the leg joints and never translates the root, so the plant never drives the pelvis through the surface.
- [ ] When a downward probe finds no ground within reach, that leg falls back to the clip pose with no snap; the other legs are unaffected.
- [ ] IK is driven by tags alone: the same model with its leg tags removed builds no `FootIk` entry and plants no feet (unit test on `build_pose_modifier_stack`); no descriptor field enables or disables planting (review/grep gate — a negative-existence check, not a runnable assertion).
- [ ] A synthetic model tagged with more than two leg chains drives one independent two-bone solve per leg with no code change — the probe array, leg set, and solver loop are not biped-hardcoded.
- [ ] A clip authored with root motion reports an authored travel speed measured from its root translation; a per-state `travelSpeed` override replaces the measured value for that state.
- [ ] A character moving faster than its clip's authored travel speed plays the clip proportionally faster (and slower when slower), so the planted foot does not slide; the rate stays within the clamp bounds.
- [ ] An archetype with `speedScale: false` does not rate-scale — its authored cadence plays unchanged — while the default (absent block, or `true`) rate-scales.
- [ ] The shipped E10 enemy walk still speed-scales identically when its state declares no derived travel speed, no override, and the default `speedScale` — no behavioral regression.
- [ ] Ground probes produce identical results across repeated headless runs of the same tick sequence.
- [ ] After the `animation.rs` split, every pre-existing `postretro-entities` test passes unchanged and no resulting non-test source file exceeds ~800 lines (test modules are exempt — move them with their targets or into sibling `*_tests` files).

### Task 2: `travelSpeed` auto-derivation at load
Derive each clip's authored travel speed at model load in `crates/model`. The root-joint translation track already loads through the generic per-channel path in `gltf_loader::load_clip` — the root joint (topo 0) writes `joints[0].translation` like any other joint; in-place clips have an empty track. For each clip, measure the horizontal (XZ) displacement of the root joint from the first to the last translation keyframe and divide by clip duration to get ground units per animated second; a clip with no root translation track — or one whose net XZ displacement is below a small epsilon (a near-in-place turn or strafe) — yields `None`, so the Task 4 rate rework falls back to the degenerate reference instead of dividing by ≈0. Store the derived per-clip value on `AnimationClip` beside `name`/`duration`/`joints` so the runtime reads it by clip index. Adding a field to `AnimationClip` touches its struct-literal construction sites across `crates/model` (loader, in-crate tests), `crates/postretro` (`hit_zones.rs`), and `crates/renderer` (`mesh_pass.rs`); `AnimationClip` derives `Default`, so the fixups are compiler-guided (build, patch each literal). Derivation only — override and consumption live in Tasks 3 and 4. Track fields are private; read via `times()`/`values()`. Add a `crates/model` unit test on a synthetic clip with a known root translation track.

### Task 3: Locomotion descriptor (Rust ↔ TS/Luau)
Add the authored locomotion surface to the mesh descriptor. Extend `MeshDescriptor` (`crates/entities/src/data_descriptors/types/entity.rs`) with an optional `locomotion` block carrying a `speedScale` boolean, defaulting to `true` when the field or block is absent (preserving current behavior). Add an optional per-state `travelSpeed` override (finite, > 0; ground units per animated second) to the animation-state descriptor; when present it replaces the Task 2 derived value for that state's clip. Extend the two hand-written front-ends — `mesh_descriptor_from_js`/`raw_animation_state_from_js` (`crates/scripting-core/src/data_descriptors/js/entity.rs`) and their Luau twins — to parse the new fields into `RawAnimationState`/the raw mesh shape, then funnel through the **existing shared validator** `MeshDescriptor::build`, which already enforces both front-ends' rules; add `travelSpeed` positivity/finiteness there so QuickJS and Luau reject the same inputs. `speedScale` is a plain bool. Mirror the new fields into the **generated** SDK typedef twins `sdk/types/postretro.d.ts` / `.d.luau` and the reference behaviors under `sdk/behaviors/reference/` — the twins regenerate from the hand-written Rust typedef registry `crates/postretro/src/scripting/primitives/mod.rs` (extend the `MeshDescriptor` and `AnimationStateDescriptor` registrations, beside the existing `crossfadeMs?`) via `gen-script-types`, guarded by the `committed_sdk_types_match_current_registry` drift test; extend the registry and regenerate, never hand-edit the twins. Casing per the Boundary inventory. Carry `speed_scale` onto runtime `MeshAnimation` and `travel_speed: Option<f32>` onto `AnimationState` so the tick and palette build read them — the two reach the runtime by different routes. `MeshDescriptor::build` copies each validated `travelSpeed` into `AnimationState.travel_speed`, and that validated `AnimationState` map is exactly what `mesh_desc.animations` carries into `MeshAnimation::new`, so the per-state override arrives with no extra threading. `speed_scale`, on the sibling `locomotion` block, must instead be threaded explicitly from `mesh_desc.locomotion` through the archetype spawn path at that same `MeshAnimation::new` call (`scripting/builtins/data_archetype.rs`). `MeshAnimation` and `AnimationState` derive serde with an exact-shape round-trip test; give the new fields `serde(default)` (and skip-if-absent on serialize) and extend that test so the round-trip stays clean.

### Task 4: Playback-rate rework to `measured / travelSpeed`
Rework speed-scaled playback (extends `animation/playback.rs` from Task 1) to compute the rate as `measured_ground_speed / effective_travel_speed`, clamped by `RATE_MIN`/`RATE_MAX` and gated by `RATE_CHANGE_EPSILON`, where `effective_travel_speed` is the active state's override (Task 3) if present, else the clip's derived value (Task 2). Both are reachable from the tick: the override rides `AnimationState` in the registry, and the derived value rides the model's `AnimationClip` list in the game-side `HitZoneStore` (already passed into `simulate_tick`, holding `Arc<Vec<AnimationClip>>` per model handle), looked up by the mesh's model handle and the active clip index. The producer `update_brain_animation_playback_rates` (`crates/postretro/src/sim/mod.rs`) already computes `speed_xz` from `agent_steering::path_state().velocity`; give it the store, and change its denominator from `agent.move_speed` to the effective travel speed, applied only when the archetype's `speedScale` is on. Degenerate case: a state with no derived travel speed and no override falls back to the current `speed_xz / move_speed`, so the shipped E10 walk is byte-for-byte unchanged; `speedScale: false` skips rate-scaling entirely (rate stays 1.0). The E10 walk qualifies because it is authored in-place — its cadence reads correctly at `move_speed`, the premise of the current heuristic — so Task 2 derives `None` for it; confirm that in the updated walk-rate test rather than assuming it (a shipped walk clip found to carry root motion would instead calibrate to its measured stride — a deliberate recalibration, which the review should catch, not a silent regression). Update the existing headless walk-rate test and add one asserting the `travelSpeed`-driven rate.

### Task 5: Foot-IK two-bone solver modifier (N-leg data path)
Add a `PoseModifier::FootIk` variant to the closed enum in `crates/model/src/pose_modifier.rs` (a direct add — the enum is deliberately concrete). It holds an ordered leg set (a `LegChain { chain_mask: JointMask, foot_joint }` per leg, not a fixed pair) and iterates it, indexing the matching `FootProbe` in `PoseInputs` by leg index — a biped runs two solves, a hexapod six, from one loop. Its apply arm joins `apply_pose_modifier_stack`'s match beside the aim/split arms; that arm receives the **full** per-joint local buffer (`&mut [LocalTrs]` spanning every joint, not only masked ones), so a leg's ancestors compose to model space — the `UpperLowerSplit` arm's parent walk is the precedent. Per leg (hip → knee → ankle/foot): forward-compute current hip and ankle model-space positions from the chain's local transforms. The solve target is the ankle's current model-space XZ with its height set to the probe's `contact_height` (`FootProbe` carries model-space quantities by contract — Task 9; the probe step, Task 6, produces them); solve the knee angle from the two segment lengths and the hip→target distance (clamped when out of reach so the leg never hyperextends), write back hip and knee local rotations, and orient the foot toward the probed ground normal within an angular limit. Blend the solved pose against the clip pose by a plant weight: full plant when the clip foot sits at or below the probed surface (stance, including following ground that drops away downslope), ramping out as the clip lifts the foot above the surface past a blend band, so a swing/lifted foot keeps its clip lift (AC 1). Each leg is independent — a probe miss (`FootProbe.hit == false`) or out-of-reach target ramps that leg fully to its clip pose without touching the others. Reads only `PoseInputs` and the local buffer — no collision, no wgpu. Add `crates/model` unit tests: flat-ground plant, sloped-ground plant with normal alignment, out-of-reach clamp, miss fallback, a clip-lifted (swing) foot that keeps its lift with ground present beneath it, and a synthetic model with more than two leg chains producing one independent solve per leg.

### Task 6: Game-logic ground probes feeding `PoseInputs`
The foot-probe types — `FootProbe`, `PoseInputs.feet`/`foot_count`, `MAX_FEET` — land in Task 9 (foundation, Phase 1); this task adds the game-side step that fills them. Probe index `i` aligns with leg-set index `i`; `FootProbe` carries model-space contact height, ground normal, and a hit flag.

Add a fixed-tick probe step in `crates/postretro/src/sim/mod.rs`, ordered **after** `update_pose_inputs`. It reaches per-model skeleton, clips, and the Task 7 leg set through the retained game-side CPU model store the hit-zone system already populates and `simulate_tick` already receives (`HitZoneStore`, keyed by model handle, holding `Arc<Skeleton>` + `Arc<Vec<AnimationClip>>`; it already runs `sample_*_world` for zone bounds); Task 7 installs the leg set into that store in the same load sweep that moves skeleton and clips in. For each leg-tagged animated entity, resolve its current sample params from the anim state the render-free way `mesh_anim::animate_entity` does (hit_zones.rs's `pose_world_joints` is the same-crate template), and, for each declared leg, sample the animated foot position from the **unmodified** world pose (`postretro_model::anim::sample_blended_world` / `sample_clip_looped_world` — collision-free; model space). Transform that foot point model→world via the entity transform and cast a downward ray with `collision::cast_ray` (or `cast_ray_combined` when movers are present), bounded to a short **planting reach** below the foot — ground beyond that reach reads as a miss (`hit == false`) — capturing time-of-impact and surface normal, applying the walkable-normal convention (`COS_WALKABLE`, today a private const in `collision/mod.rs` — expose it `pub(crate)` or re-derive the threshold) already used by movement ground-stick. Convert the hit back to **model space** (inverse entity transform) before writing, so `FootProbe` feeds the model-local solver (Task 5) directly.

Set `foot_count` to the entity's leg-set length and write `feet[0..foot_count]` (leg index aligned with the leg set) — a read-modify-write that leaves the aim fields `update_pose_inputs` set intact; conversely, `update_pose_inputs` must carry the existing `feet`/`foot_count` forward rather than reconstructing a fresh `PoseInputs` literal that would clobber them (the carry-forward protects the values on ticks and entities the probe step skips; without the probe step setting it, `foot_count` stays at its `0` default and the solver sees no feet). The write stays in the tick because the render collector borrows the registry immutably; this world-pose-only sample is distinct from the renderer's modified palette sample — the inherent game-side cost of probe inputs, acceptable for the few IK entities on screen. Add a determinism-harness test (`sim/determinism_tests.rs`) asserting repeatable probe outputs.

### Task 7: Leg-chain and foot-target tagging at load (side names + N-leg)
Extend the loader's `read_pose_masks` (`crates/model/src/gltf_loader.rs`) with leg/foot mask names. Recognized spellings: side names `legL`/`legR` (a leg's hip→knee→ankle chain) and `footL`/`footR` (that leg's foot/ankle target joint) for the biped case, mapping to leg-set indices L=0, R=1; and indexed names `leg{i}`/`foot{i}` (i = 0, 1, 2, …) as the general N-leg form. A model uses one spelling family; mixing side and indexed names for the same leg set warns. Reindex memberships through the same topo remap as the other masks. Collect the results into an ordered leg set (capacity `MAX_FEET`; extra legs warn and are dropped) and surface it on `LoadedModel` as a variable-length `Vec<LegChain>` field — kept outside the fixed `Copy` `PoseMaskSet`. In `build_pose_modifier_stack`, push one `FootIk` entry carrying the whole leg set whenever the set is non-empty — mirroring how a non-empty `aimSpine` chain yields an `AimPitchBend` entry. To let the fixed-tick probe step (Task 6) read the leg set game-side, install it into the retained CPU model store: add a `legs` field to `ModelHitZones` (`crates/postretro/src/scripting/systems/hit_zones.rs`) and move it in where `insert_from_load` destructures `LoadedModel { skeleton, clips, joint_zones, pose_stack, .. }` — the same level-load sweep that already retains skeleton and clips. Unknown/malformed tags warn, never fail. A biped tags `legL`/`legR` + `footL`/`footR`; nothing in the loader, solver, or probe path assumes exactly two. Add a `crates/model` unit test on `build_pose_modifier_stack`: a leg-tagged model yields exactly one `FootIk` entry carrying the expected leg count, and the same model with its leg tags removed yields none (AC 4).

## Boundary inventory
The locomotion descriptor crosses Rust ↔ TS/Luau (no wire or FGD surface — mesh presets are authored, not map KVPs). The leg/foot tags cross the glTF-authoring boundary only. Casing: camelCase in TS/Luau and the decoded JSON/table, snake_case in Rust, matching the shipped `crossfadeMs`/`crossfade_ms` and `moveSpeed`/`move_speed` conventions.

| Name | Rust | Authored key (TS / Luau) | Values |
|---|---|---|---|
| locomotion block | `MeshDescriptor.locomotion: Option<LocomotionDescriptor>` | `locomotion?` | object below |
| speed-scale toggle | `LocomotionDescriptor.speed_scale: bool` | `speedScale?` | boolean, default `true` |
| per-state travel-speed override | `AnimationState.travel_speed: Option<f32>` | `travelSpeed?` (on an animation state) | finite, > 0; ground units / animated second |
| leg chain (glTF `extras`) | leg chain mask via `read_pose_masks` | node `extras` `poseMask: "legL"`/`"legR"`, or `"leg0"`, `"leg1"`, … | topo joint set, one per leg |
| foot target (glTF `extras`) | leg foot joint | node `extras` `poseMask: "footL"`/`"footR"`, or `"foot0"`, `"foot1"`, … | single joint, one per leg |

Leg/foot masks extend the shipped `poseMask` `extras` mechanism; they are glTF-authoring only. Side names cover bipeds (L=0, R=1 in the ordered leg set); indexed names are the general N-leg form and align each leg with its `PoseInputs.feet` slot.

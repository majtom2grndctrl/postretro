## Delegated answers

- **On-path tolerance.** A declared point is on-path when its perpendicular distance to the host's frozen path segment (fire origin along the frozen direction, from 0 to the frozen reach) is at most `radius + d + 0.25 m`, where `d` is the launched muzzle's perpendicular offset from the eye ray (`muzzle_world_origin` with the resolved placement; 0 without a muzzle), computed once and frozen on the shot at FIRE (`splash_on_path_tolerance`). Whether a host contact sits "at" a declared world point (P18) is a fixed 0.1 m slack along the path (`AT_POINT_SLACK`): a host contact decides for a world record when it is nearer than the declared point or no more than 0.1 m beyond it, and when the cursor passes a declared world point with no contact, the host first casts ahead up to 0.1 m past it (capped at reach) so a wall just beyond the point still takes the blast. (Review round 1 narrowed this from the on-path tolerance, which let a point declared short of an unreached wall resolve in open air.) Reason: on a non-late FIRE both paths start from muzzles on the same eye ray and converge on the same eye line, so they part by at most the muzzle's offset from the eye (P25), plus the eye's prediction error, which 0.25 m covers. A late start parts by the pawn's movement since the start, and the P17 and P26 tests place that beyond the bound.
- **Where march state lives, and who owns the step.** In `postretro-netcode`'s `splash_march` module. Each march is a `SplashMarch` in a sibling map inside `OpenAuthorizedShots`, so every path that carries, cleans or resets open shots carries its marches too (client leave, pawn removal, expiry, level reset). Reason: netcode already owns the open shot, its declaration and the observer flight, and the Projectiles-stage ingest closure it supplies already runs where the march must. A march opens when a client splash FIRE is recorded. Binding a declaration still retires the open shot, which keeps the one-declaration-per-shot spine, and judges its first valid record once. The march steps once per host tick inside that closure, after intake and before `projectile_stage::advance`, with the tick's world passed in; nothing steps or lands on the FIRE tick.
- **`projectile_static_contact_distance`.** Deleted, with the splash branch of `projectile_hold_ticks`; `AuthorizedShot::projectile_static_contact_distance` became `splash_on_path_tolerance`. Reason: the march finds the host's own contact each tick, so the FIRE-frozen distance has no reader. Splash declarations drain at the minimum hold and bind to their march.

# E17--mover-relative-rider-presentation

Brief · resumable · Epic 17 · reads: `context/lib/networking.md` §Current contract, §Host-side remote-pawn presentation, §Snapshot apply ordering · `context/lib/movement.md` §6 (Moving bases) · `context/lib/entity_model.md` §5 · `context/lib/rendering_pipeline.md` (View vs. sim split, kinematic movers) · `plans/done/E17--rotating-movers/` · research: `research.md` · read at 8489a1fe

## Problem
E17 rotating-mover play-tests found co-op riders that do not stay on their platforms. It is an observed defect, seen by players on host and client screens. Cause: a rider and the mover it stands on are drawn at different times or from different poses. Remote riders are drawn at the interpolation delay while their mover is drawn at predicted current time, on a client's screen and on the host's. A snapshot correction also rewrites a mover's current pose without its previous one, so the drawn mover steps under everyone standing on it, the client's own view included. When this is done, every player rider stays planted on the platform it stands on from every seat, through spin, reversal and correction. The local eye and pawn are placed through the drawn mover on every seat, so a correction leaves them on the deck. Landing, leaving and changing movers look as they do today.

## Decisions
- **The registry's presented pose is the one render-time mover pose.** Every reader of a mover's drawn pose keeps the registry's render-time accessor (`entity_model.md` §5), so draws, attached lights, ghosts, sound anchors and future readers agree by construction (`research.md` §Where the pose lives). Smoothing is a render-only per-entity correction that accessor applies after its blend; simulation reads of `Transform` never see it. Readers that take the simulation pose for a drawing decision, such as visibility cull and a sound's fire-time anchor, also cover the drawn pose. Placement: the entities crate owns the correction as mechanism, client netcode seeds it, and the app advances it once per frame before any presentation consumer.
- **Hot paths: the registry accessor, rider placement and rider foot probes, every frame.** None allocates per frame in steady state, and their cost is measured in the per-frame Presentation CPU stage (`development_guide.md` §1.4).
- **Every rider's feet probe the presented mover pose each frame, on every seat.** That covers remote riders, the local pawn and NPCs standing on a mover; feet not on a mover keep today's probe. This diverges from `networking.md` §Host-side remote-pawn presentation, where host-seat foot probes stay on the live end-of-tick mover, and from the per-tick probe single-player and the host run for their own legged meshes. Once riders are placed on the presented pose, a probe against another pose plants feet off the drawn surface.
- **Remote riders rebase on both seats:** a client's view of every remote player and the host's view of each client's pawn. When both samples that bracket the delayed render time stand on the same mover, each sample is placed in that mover's frame at its own tick. The blend happens in that frame, and the result is placed through the mover's presented pose as the rider's presentation transform. Each sample's mover pose is authoritative for its tick: on a client, the newest authoritative mover sample at or before it, since a mover the host did not resend has not changed; on the host, the same tick. Scale and every other orientation term are kept, and neither seat's delay, buffer or teleport-cut rule changes.
- **The rebase keys on a sampled ground reference, not on entity kind.** The player ground reference already on the wire supplies it, so no wire field, `WIRE_VERSION`, PRL field, FGD key or script surface changes. Roadmap Epic 17 "Co-op presentation of NPCs on moving geometry" then only has to replicate agent ground; it reuses this rebase unchanged.
- **Transitions fall back to world space, eased.** An interval that lands, leaves or changes movers blends in world space. The first interval with the same mover on both ends attaches; the first whose ends differ detaches. Each change of placement eases the drawn rider from the pose it was drawn at the frame before onto its new pose over presented time, so a rider never steps; a teleport cut still cuts clean. A missing ground sample, a mover pose missing for a sample's tick, an unloaded mover, or a non-finite or non-invertible frame falls back to the sampled world pose, without panic or a stale attachment.
- **`carry_yaw` turns a remote rider with its mover.** With the locally loaded mover's `carry_yaw` on, the rider's yaw is carried relative to the mover's world-up yaw, so it advances with the presented mover exactly once. With it off, mover rotation never enters orientation. Pitch and roll never do (`movement.md` §6; `carry_yaw` stays PRL data and never crosses the wire, per `plans/done/E17--rotating-movers/`).
- **Remote legs turn with the body.** Animation and lower-body heading keep reading replicated intrinsic velocity (`plans/done/E17--rotating-movers/`, Remote rider presentation). When the rebase turns a rider, the legs keep the heading relative to the body that the sample had.
- **Mover corrections are smoothed, render-only, on connected clients.** A snapshot correction leaves the drawn mover where it was drawn the frame before, then converges on the corrected pose over presented time, at a rate independent of frame rate. A correction that moves any point of the mover's bounds by at least the pawn reconcile's teleport-class floor snaps instead (`networking.md` §Current contract, reconcile). Smoothing clears on mover despawn, level unload, mover rebind and non-finite input.
- **The local eye rides the drawn deck on every seat.** When both fixed-tick endpoints stand on the same mover, the pawn's capsule centre is blended in that mover's frame and placed through its presented pose. Eye height is then added along world-up, so mover pitch and roll never tilt the eye. The camera eye and the local pawn mesh (shadow and mirror) take the result, world offset included; any other case keeps today's world-space blend, and a connected client's mesh also takes the world offset. The render-yaw residual keeps owning yaw, and with `carry_yaw` on the view yaw also takes the presented correction's world-up yaw (`research.md` §Local eye).
- **A mover correction reaches the client's eye once, and the eye steps only on a cut.** A mover correction is also a correction to the pawn standing on it, and pawn corrections already seed the world-space presentation offset. The mover's share reaches the eye through the presented pose only. The rest, from mispredicted input or a hold, keeps the world-space offset, which the mover never rotates. Leaving or landing on a gliding mover, and its despawn or rebind under the pawn, hand the residual at the eye's point between the deck and the world offset. With `carry_yaw` on, the view yaw takes the same hand-offs through a render-only yaw offset, while movement commands keep the settled yaw. A snapping deck cuts the eye standing on it, and a teleport cuts the eye clean with no hand-off.
- **Presentation never writes authority.** Pawn movement, the client's movement commands, ground reference, mover phase, collision poses, mover history and outgoing snapshots are never written by any of this. Non-riders, static bases, remote entities that replicate no ground and maps without movers present as today.
- **Non-goals:**
  - Co-op rebase of NPC riders: the roadmap item above owns it, and they need a wire bump this brief rules out.
  - Delaying predicted movers to the remote-actor time: it would bring back input lag on the client's own platform.
  - Arc interpolation for NPCs riding movers on single-player and the host: they keep the straight blend between ticks, about 0.1 mm off the arc on the carousel (`research.md` §Local eye).
  - Camera pitch/roll carry, angular momentum, nested movers, scale carry, and general temporal smoothing for other entities.

## Acceptance
### Automated
**Remote riders stay planted:**
- [ ] On the rotating-platform fixture under the mandated conditioned-link profile, a stationary remote rider on a mover that rotates and translates stays at its mover-local point within `1e-4` m of the drawn mover for at least ten revolutions, with no accumulating drift. This holds on a connected client viewing the host and on the host viewing the client (pin P11). The fixture adds a mover that both spins and travels, and a second player riding it. The client sees that player as a remote, and the host presents the client's pawn from its delay buffer.
- [ ] Spin start, stop and reversal mid-interval keep the same bound.
- [ ] With `carry_yaw` on, a remote rider's yaw equals its blended yaw relative to the mover plus the presented mover's world-up yaw. Sampling the same frame twice gives the same yaw. With `carry_yaw` off, mover rotation adds zero yaw. A mover pitch or roll leaves the rider's orientation untouched in both cases.
- [ ] An interval whose bracketing samples name the same mover attaches, and one whose samples differ (landing, leaving, mover to mover) converges on today's world-space pose, eased from the pose drawn the frame before so the rider never steps. Both sides are tested.
- [ ] Ground history survives sparse deltas: Transform-only and movement-only samples each attach when the latest ground at or before their tick names the mover.
- [ ] A ground sample that arrives after a newer sample still decides every later sample it precedes. A rider never attaches to a mover it had already left. (pin P9)
- [ ] Missing ground, missing or evicted mover history, history that no longer reaches back to the rider's tick (pin P8), an unloaded mover, a non-finite mover frame and a zero-scale mover frame each draw the sampled world pose, without panic or a stale attachment on the next interval.
- [ ] A remote rider's teleport generation change cuts as today; the first interval after the cut attaches only once its two samples name one mover.
- [ ] A planted foot on a rider keeps its hit and its model-space contact height within `1e-3` across snapshot arrivals and render alphas: a rebased remote on both seats, and the local pawn and an NPC riding on single-player and the host, on a mover that moves vertically, so a probe against any other pose misses by more than the bound.

**Corrections are smooth and counted once:**
- [ ] On the frame a mover correction is applied, at any alpha and on a frame that runs zero fixed ticks, the drawn mover moves at most `1e-4` m and `1e-4` rad from the previous frame's drawn pose beyond its own motion. A synthetic correction converges to the corrected pose without accumulating drift, in the same presented time at 30, 60 and 240 Hz rendering.
- [ ] A correction applied on a frame that runs no fixed tick shows no step on that frame, on later zero-tick frames, or on the first frame that ticks after it. (pin P1)
- [ ] On the frame a correction is applied, the view yaw, the remote rider rebase, the foot probe, the sound anchor and the render pass all read one mover pose, and that pose equals the previous frame's drawn pose plus the mover's own motion. (pin P2)
- [ ] Before and after the change, the harness records the worst same-frame visual step for the conditioned rotating-platform run and the synthetic zero-tick correction. The before figure shows the step this brief removes.
- [ ] Two corrections to one mover on one frame compose. A correction that moves any point of the mover's bounds by the teleport-class floor snaps, including one that only rotates; a smaller one glides. A snap lands whole on the frame it is applied at any alpha, including a frame that runs no fixed tick (pin P18). Despawn, level unload, rebind and non-finite input clear the smoothing.
- [ ] A connected client's pawn standing on a corrected mover keeps its eye within `1e-4` m of its point on the drawn deck from the correction frame to the end of the glide, at 30, 60 and 240 Hz rendering, including frames that run no fixed tick (pin P3). With `carry_yaw` on, its view yaw follows the drawn mover's yaw with no step. A correction from mispredicted input on the same frame still glides through the world-space offset, along world axes, unturned by the mover's spin or its gliding correction (pin P17).
- [ ] With `carry_yaw` on, a correction applied on a frame that runs no fixed tick keeps a standing pawn's view yaw on the drawn deck's yaw at any alpha. This holds on that frame, on later zero-tick frames and on the first frame that ticks. (pin P13)
- [ ] On the frame a mover correction is applied, at any alpha and on a frame that runs no fixed tick, the eye of a pawn standing on it moves by no more than its own motion. (pin P14)
- [ ] A pawn that jumps off, or steps onto another mover, while a correction is mid-glide moves its eye by no more than its own motion on that frame, at any alpha, and on every frame until the next tick (pin P15). It lands on its world position by the end of the glide.
- [ ] A snapshot that corrects a mover and also moves the pawn's ground onto or off it moves the eye by no more than its own motion on that frame, at any alpha, and on every frame until the next tick. (pins P5, P15)
- [ ] A pawn that lands on a mover whose correction is mid-glide moves its eye by no more than its own motion on that frame, and stays on the drawn deck to the end of the glide, at any alpha, and on every frame until the next tick. (pins P4, P15)
- [ ] A pawn that teleports off a mover mid-glide cuts to its arrival with no carried glide. (pin P6)
- [ ] A rotation-only correction that snaps the deck cuts the eye of a pawn standing on it on the same frame. A smaller one glides both. (pin P7)
- [ ] A mover that despawns or rebinds under a standing pawn mid-glide moves the pawn's eye by no more than its own motion, at any alpha and on a frame that runs no fixed tick. (pins P12, P16)
- [ ] Single-player, the host's own pawn and a client pawn on static ground or airborne draw an eye bit-equal to before. Their pawn mesh is bit-equal to before, except that a connected client's mesh now carries the same world-space presentation offset as its eye while one glides.

**Local eye rides the deck:**
- [ ] On single-player, the host's own pawn and a connected client, a pawn standing on a mover that turns a quarter turn in one tick is drawn at the arc midpoint at alpha 0.5, not the chord midpoint. The eye's distance from the rotation axis stays constant within `1e-4` m across alphas.
- [ ] With the mover pitched or rolled, the eye sits exactly eye height above the placed capsule centre along world-up.
- [ ] Landing, leaving, mover change, fly-camera, menu camera, teleport hold, the level-install hold, a reconcile eye cut and a missing pawn each draw exactly today's world-space blend. The frame after any of them carries no attachment from before. (pin P19)
- [ ] A teleport-class correction that leaves the pawn on a gliding mover moves its eye once. The interval after it does not step. (pin P19)
- [ ] A connected client's own pawn mesh, shadow and mirror included, moves by no more than its own motion on every frame where its eye hands off.
- [ ] With `carry_yaw` on, leaving, landing on, or losing a gliding mover turns the view yaw by no more than the pawn's own turn on that frame, and the movement commands' yaw is unchanged by the hand-off. (pin P20)
- [ ] On a frame where smoothing is active, the mover's beauty, shadow, occluder, attached light, ghost and sound-anchor poses, the remote rider rebase, the foot probe, and the client's eye and pawn mesh all agree with the mover's presented pose. A test feeds a correction and checks each against it.
- [ ] A mover whose presented pose is in view draws, even while its corrected pose is out of view. Its SH sample regions and a sound fired from it on that frame follow the drawn pose.

**Authority and scope:**
- [ ] With rebasing and smoothing active, pawn positions, the client's movement commands (pin P10), ground references, mover phases, collision poses, mover history and serialized snapshots, layout included, are bit-equal to a run with both disabled, and `WIRE_VERSION` is unchanged.
- [ ] With `carry_yaw` on, a rebased remote rider's locomotion animation and lower-body heading relative to its aim equal those of the same run without rebasing. With it off, they are equal outright.
- [ ] Non-riders, riders on static ground, remote entities that replicate no ground and a mover-less map draw bit-equal to before.
- [ ] A fixture with every remote pawn riding, across several movers at once, keeps every bound above. Rebasing, foot probes and smoothing allocate nothing per frame in steady state, and the Presentation CPU stage reports their cost before and after on this fixture.

### Manual
- [ ] Host and client ride the kinematic-platform carousel together under the dev lag proxy at the harness profile, facing each other. Through spin, reversal, jumping off and landing back on, each sees the other planted with feet on the deck and a steady view, and no correction pop. A recipe for this lands in the map's README.

## Path
- Seams: `EntityRegistry::interpolated_transform` and `set_presentation_transform` (`crates/entities/src/registry.rs`); `ClientReplication::sample_into_registry` and `RemoteInterpolationBuffer::presented_pose` (client remotes); `host_presentation::present_client_pawns`, `probe_presented_client_pawn_feet` (host seat); `MoverHistoryBuffer` and `client/replay_poses.rs` (client mover pose at a sample's tick); `host_presentation::record_client_pawn_poses` (host sample with its mover pose); `ClientReplication::apply_components_to`, `prepare_mover_apply`, `MoverCorrection`, `apply_authoritative_mover_corrections` (corrections); `reconcile.rs` ordinary-correction arm and `seed_presentation_offset_with_life_change` (own-pawn share); `FrameTiming`, `InterpolableState`, every `push_state`/`hold_state` caller, `follow_camera_to_local_pawn`, `effective_render_yaw`, `mover_yaw_carry_ground`, `MeshRenderCollector::collect_inner` (local eye and pawn); `KinematicMoverRenderCollector::collect_with_mirror` (its cull reads the current bounds; SH regions already cover both), `light_bridge.rs`, `ghost_presented_transform`, `sound_events/anchors.rs` (consumers that follow by construction); `MoverPoseSource` (foot-probe adapter).
- Chosen shape: a sparse render-only correction keyed by entity, applied inside `interpolated_transform`, plus a `MoverPoseSource` adapter that answers mover poses from it at the frame alpha. Strongest rival: an App-owned per-frame mover pose table handed to listed consumers. It caches each pose once, but every unlisted reader draws a different mover while smoothing runs.
- Remote rebase: store each sample's pose in its mover's frame, and with `carry_yaw` its yaw relative to the mover's world-up yaw. On the host this happens at record time, since ground is authoritative there. On a client it happens when sampling, so a late ground sample corrects later sparse samples (pin P9). History that no longer reaches a sample's tick returns the oldest pose today; treat that as missing (pin P8).
- Foot probes: a `MoverPoseSource` adapter answers mover poses from the presented pose at the frame alpha. Single-player and the host move rider probes from `simulate_started_tick_with_presentation_aim` (`update_foot_ground_probes`) to a per-frame pass over riders only.
- Correction storage: sparse, keyed by entity, so an entity without a correction pays one empty lookup.
- Legs: turn the sampled intrinsic velocity by the rebase's world-up yaw before the pose pass.
- View yaw: a render-only yaw offset beside the position offset, read by the drawn view only.
- Own-pawn share: the mover-explained share is the mover correction's displacement of the pawn's pre-correction point, when ground names the same mover before and after. Hand-offs move the residual at the eye's point into the world offset with the sign that keeps the eye still.
- First slice: the client-view remote rebase on the rotating fixture, measured against the ten-revolution row. It tests per-sample mover poses at snapshot spacing with lost packets, the riskiest assumption.
- Split first, behaviour-preserving, own commits: `crates/netcode/src/client.rs` (remote presentation out), `crates/postretro/src/main.rs` (camera follow and yaw carry helpers out), `crates/sim/src/sim/mod.rs` (foot probing out). `interpolation.rs`, `mesh_render.rs` and `runtime_movers.rs` are past 800 lines but mostly tests; add at their boundaries.

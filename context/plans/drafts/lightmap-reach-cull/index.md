# lightmap-reach-cull

Brief · compact · reads: `context/lib/build_pipeline.md` §Compiler pipeline steps 3, 5, 6, 10 and 12, §Build Cache · `context/lib/development_guide.md` §6.4 · read at 343ff98b6

## Problem

The roadmap's "lightmap dark-texel cost", sized by two developer-run spikes (`flood-cull-findings.md`, `leak-diagnosis-findings.md`, beside this brief). Lightmap Bake is about 80% of a cold full-hallway build: 860 s of 1079 s on the owner's Mac. About 74% of its covered (light, texel) pairs end as dark early-outs, all nine of the texel's probe rays to the emitter blocked, mostly by light reaching through walls. The cause: the walk bounds a light only by range, cone and plane. Nothing bounds it by where light can travel through open space. The one flood the compiler has, `affinity_grid::light_portal_reach`, fills the whole connected component of runtime cells, so on a single-component map it rules nothing out. When this is done, Lightmap Bake skips a light's charts that open space cannot carry it to, and moves no output byte; the byte moves it depends on belong to its two prerequisites, landed first. A compile-time proof that every face edge is matched, within the tracer's τ, by an edge running the other way checks the cull's precondition at any map size, and the holes it finds are the hole warnings authors see.

## Decisions

- **Prerequisites land first, each its own plan; this brief does not re-specify them.** The cull's byte gates compare against `main` after both.
  - *`face-extract-split-epsilon`* (branch `fix/face-extract-split-epsilon`) closes every hole that let a ray cross solid from one open leaf to another.
  - *`watertight-tracer`* replaces Möller–Trumbore with the Woop/Benthin/Wald watertight test, grown by an in-plane tolerance τ that closes crease cracks and T-junctions narrower than it; makes the box test conservative (pbrt-v4 `1+2γ3`, a NaN slab constrains nothing); pads BVH boxes to cover the grown triangle and that test's rounding; tests the remembered occluder only when its leaf box passes the same `BoundedRay` predicate, so the cache cannot change the answer on the slab or the prune path; keeps the distance prune; folds one `TRACER_EPOCH` into the key of every cached stage that reaches the tracer; and re-derives the shaft-cull margins. Skipped texels inside a culled pair write no remembered occluder (research §Occluder caches); this prerequisite makes that move no byte, so it is no gap of this brief.
- **Exact by construction, verified like the shaft cull.** The standard is `plans/done/lightmap-exact-skips/`'s: byte-identical to the unculled walk, checked by fixture parity, a verify mode and whole-PRL `cmp` gates, not proven for every float input. A gate that moves a byte stops the build and goes to the owner as a measured amendment. No version bump is pre-authorized, and the cull moves no cache key.
  - *Precondition:* every boundary between open space and solid inside a culling light's reach carries a face the hit test cannot miss.
  - *Named gaps:* a ray lying in a face's plane, which the hit test rejects as parallel while the BSP rounds it into solid; a crack wider than τ, which the coverage proof cannot prove, switching the cull off.
- **The flood is never more closed than the walk it culls for.** It may count a leaf as solid only when the walk traces against geometry that seals it in the same bake. Anything a later lighting state can open or remove counts as open. This keeps the cull exact as the roadmap adds states.
  - *Movers:* doors, sliding walls, and a room that rises out of the ground sit outside the static BSP, BVH and lightmaps (research §Exactness argument), so the flood passes through every pose and the cull never decides their light.
  - *Destruction:* pieces that break away, and latent portals that open on fracture (roadmap §Moving and destructible geometry), are static before the break and would seal the area behind them. When either lands, its geometry must count as open to the flood, or its after-state break faces and revealed rooms lose light. Nothing like this exists yet; the rule is stated so the first destructible feature inherits it rather than silently breaking the cull.
- **A lightmap reach index in its own compiler module,** outside `lightmap_layer` because it reads partition state, not charts. Per eligible light it holds the open full-tree leaves the flood reaches; its query takes full-tree receiver leaves. Its R bounds the segments the lightmap sampler traces, and no other bake's.
  - *Where it stops:* light → cell reach over runtime cells, filtered by the portals a closed mover blocks, belongs to the door and mover work (`ready/mover-light-flow--foundation/`, its research step 3). The name stays lightmap-scoped so it doesn't squat that concept. This index takes no blocked portals and answers no runtime cell.
- **The flood runs on the full compiler BSP, not runtime cells** (research §Graph choice). The per-texel rule needs exact sub-leaf solidity, which `RuntimeCells` leaves to readers of the full tree. Nothing the flood builds reaches the PRL, the cache or a later stage.
- **The flood.** Seeds: the open leaves the emitter sphere (`light_size` + 1 mm) overlaps. A portal is crossed only when its polygon comes within R of the light centre. The flood never enters a solid or exterior leaf. R bounds every segment the sampler traces for any texel the light lights: `falloff_range` + `light_size` + the footprint radius + a margin above rounding. Distances are exact, in f64.
- **Eligible lights: points and spots.** Suns, lights the stage's embedded-emitter test flags, and lights whose origin sits in a solid or exterior leaf are never culled. The cull follows the stage's flag at either penumbra-floor setting and runs no embedding test of its own.
- **Chart test, relaxed.** A (light, chart) pair is culled when every open leaf the chart's padded, lifted texel slab touches lies outside the light's reach. Solid and exterior leaves the slab touches are ignored. The slab contains every ray origin and footprint point the sampler uses for that chart, including texel-centre drift off the face plane (`drafts/lightmap-chart-plane-drift/`). Where containment cannot be shown, the pair is kept.
- **Per-texel rule inside a culled pair.** A texel stores 0 without tracing only when its own padded footprint slab touches open leaves only. Every other texel traces as a kept pair's texel does, exact skips included. This covers buried texels, which crease cracks reach, and texels whose first millimetre of ray can enter a wall. Never key on the texel centre's leaf: that test skipped 21 lit hallway texels.
- **An exact coverage proof, by oriented edge balance, switches the cull off.** Along every edge line of the faces the walk traces, on every sub-span, the edges running one way must be matched one-for-one by edges running the other way, each edge of a pair within τ of the other's face in the tracer's crack-width metric (`watertight-tracer` research §Tolerance), reading the tracer's τ constant. An edge left unmatched is an unproven span. Every face is wound outward from solid, so a closed surface balances, and a dropped face leaves one direction unmatched, at a hinge too, where each remaining edge still lies within τ of some face (research §Coverage proof).
  - *Exact:* distance to a convex face is convex along a straight segment, so a sub-span whose ends both lie within τ of a face lies within τ of it throughout. Sub-spans break at edge ends along the line and wherever an edge's nearest face changes, and f64 rounding counts toward a hole. Nothing is sampled and nothing rests on parity cancellation, so the proof holds at any map size and coordinate.
- **A crack is proven within τ; anything else is a hole.** An edge span the proof cannot prove is a hole. The boundary is τ, not the roughly 2τ that τ closes: a false hole costs bake speed, a false crack breaks exactness.
- **A map with any hole culls nothing.** Holes come from face extraction, not from authored intent, so a loud, slower, still-correct bake beats a second proximity test per light. One hole costs the whole map its cull, about 1.75× Lightmap Bake on the hallway. When the map has an eligible light, the stage also warns that the reach cull is off for the whole map, and why.
- **The coverage proof is the only author-facing hole warning.** It runs on every build, warm or cold, with or without an eligible light, and never fails the build (`partition/manifold.rs` header). It warns with each hole's location and brush provenance, up to the parity check's sample cap, plus the total; at info it logs the edges it proved and its time. Log levels and text change; no output byte does.
  - *`check_watertight`* keeps its place after face extraction and its report, but logs its spans at info instead of warning of "possible holes you can see through": parity also flags spans that are not holes, and every span the hallway keeps after the split-epsilon fix is a parity artefact (leak findings §Verdict). The cull reads none of it: its parity weld hides narrow cracks, and its spans carry no width.
- **Verify mode is an invariant check compiled into every build, off by default in every profile.** `POSTRETRO_LIGHTMAP_REACH_VERIFY=1` turns it on and `0` off; a malformed value warns once and keeps the default, as `development_guide.md` §6.4 describes. It is always on in tests and in every gate run that does not measure time or the cache (Acceptance). It re-walks every culled pair with every other production skip and empty occluder caches, and requires every record to match bit for bit. While it is on, the lightmap layer and section caches are bypassed, so a warm build re-walks every culled pair too. A mismatch fails the bake, naming the light, chart, texel and both values.
  - Off by default, departing from §6.4's default-on precedent: dev-profile compiles are the checkout default, and default-on would re-bake the lightmap stage cold and let a named gap halt a level designer (`partition/manifold.rs` header).
  - This departs from the shaft cull's test-only verify. That cull's correctness lives in its own geometry test; this one's precondition lives in authored maps, which fixtures cannot cover.
- **The stage logs one info line:** pairs walked, pairs culled, texels skipped, texels traced inside culled pairs, whether the cull was switched off, and, with verify on, pairs re-walked. The counts cover the partitions walked this build; the line states how many came from cache, and a stage served wholly from cache says so instead of printing zeros. These are stage totals, not the per-texel sizing counters the shaft-cull brief kept out.
- **Production always culls.** Culls-off is a test seam only, as for the exact skips. No prl-build flag.
- **Non-goals:**
  - *Other consumers of the index:* SH-family bakes, ChunkLightList and runtime reach each need a different bound and keep `light_portal_reach` (research §Other consumers). The weight-map walk is the natural second consumer, in its own change.
  - *Emitter-pairing cost,* the roadmap line's other lever: a separate mechanism, unmeasured against a culled bake. It stays on the roadmap.
  - *Dropping culled pairs whole* instead of writing 0 records. It saves more, but first needs proof that a missing record and a 0 record compose to the same bytes downstream (shadowmask coverage). Roadmap entry at landing.
  - *Closing crease cracks* by compiler edge snapping. Only if verify mode ever fires (leak findings §Recommendation 5).
  - *`drafts/geometry-integrity-validation/`* stays a draft with its own scope: this brief's coverage proof takes over its hole detection and the author-facing hole warning. The draft keeps its degenerate-triangle warning, exterior-leak point file and `--strict-geometry` gate, and owns gappy-geometry repair (`done/movement--static-overlap-invariant` names it). Its hole clustering and gate, when built, read the proof's holes, not parity spans.

## Acceptance

### Automated

Test suite. Each fixture asserts the pair is culled or kept, and that lightmap and shadowmask bytes with the cull on equal the cull off, at penumbra floor on and off. Verify mode is on in every fixture.
- [ ] Two rooms split by a solid wall, a light in one, a chart on the far wall within range: the pair is culled. Texels whose footprint touches the wall's solid are traced, the rest are skipped.
- [ ] The same rooms joined by a doorway whose portal lies within R: kept. The portal moved just past R: culled.
- [ ] A chart whose texel centres sit in an unreached leaf while its padded slab reaches into a reached one: kept.
- [ ] In the far room, a chart on a brush top whose slab overhangs into that brush's solid: culled under the relaxed rule.
- [ ] A long oblique chart whose texel centres drift off its plane by more than the slab's smallest lift, with a lit texel whose drifted centre lies in a reached leaf: bytes equal, culled or kept. The test fails when the slab is built from the face plane instead of the walk's texel positions.
- [ ] Inside a culled pair, a traced texel walked right after a run of skipped texels: the culled and unculled walks hold different remembered occluders at that texel, and its record equals the unculled walk's and one computed by a brute-force test of every triangle, no BVH (research P4).
- [ ] An emitter sphere straddling a portal seeds both leaves.
- [ ] A `kinematic_mover` brush between a light and a chart, at any waypoint pose: the pair is kept.
- [ ] A sun and a light whose origin sits in solid: nothing culled. An embedded emitter: not culled with the floor on, where the stage flags it; culled where unreached with the floor off, where the stage flags none.

Coverage proof. Test seams that move or remove faces act before both the proof and the BVH (research P7).
- [ ] On the traced faces of a closed brush fixture with a T-junction the oversize-face cut leaves and an overlap: zero unproven spans. With a 2 cm strip removed, unproven spans lie only along that strip, at its location.
- [ ] A map that culls, with two crease edges moved apart by just under the tracer's τ, read from its constant: the proof proves them and the map still culls. Moved just over τ: an unproven span there, the map culls nothing, and the stage warns that the cull is off for the whole map (research P6).
- [ ] The same map with a crease crack whose width is under τ at its quarter points, where the spike's classifier probed, and over τ between them: unproven spans between them, and the map culls nothing.
- [ ] The same map with a crease crack just over τ, far enough from the origin that the parity weld merges its two edge lines (past about 190 m at τ = 0.5 mm): `check_watertight` reports no open span there, the proof finds an unproven span, and the map culls nothing.
- [ ] A hinge, two closed brushes meeting along one edge so four traced faces share that line: zero unproven spans, with the four faces offered to the proof in every order. With one of the four removed, or two whose edges run the same way along it, an unproven span lies along the hinge itself, though every remaining edge there lies within τ of another face, and the map culls nothing (research P8).
- [ ] A face set where `check_watertight` reports odd-covered spans and the proof proves every edge: the map culls, those spans log at info, and no warning names a hole.
- [ ] A map with extracted faces removed at more locations than the warning's sample cap: it culls nothing, and its warning names locations with their brushes up to the cap, plus the total, and says the cull is off for the whole map. A warm rebuild warns the same. The same map without the removal culls. With its only eligible light swapped for a sun, the hole warning stays and no cull-off line appears.
- [ ] A planted fault, a pair the flood keeps forced culled as `force_clear` forces a tile clear, makes verify mode fail and name a texel the walk lights, with both values.
- [ ] Verify mode re-walks a culled pair as production walks a kept pair: shaft cull and clip pre-pass on, both occluder caches empty at the pair's first texel. On the two-rooms fixture, the re-walk's tallied shaft tests equal a walk's with only the reach cull off (research P1).
- [ ] Verify mode on, one governor permit: the two-rooms fixture bakes to completion. Inside a culled pair's walk, its re-walk and the per-chart mask build, the thread holds exactly one permit, and stage progress advances once per pair (research P2).
- [ ] Existing parity holds: production matches the reference oracle at each floor setting, warm equals cold, output is identical across worker counts, and overlap texels match across every face cut. The two-rooms fixture, with pairs culled and border texels traced, holds the same parity at one worker and many (research P3).

Gate runs. Machine-checked, launched by hand; the owner may run the long ones. Baseline: `main` after both prerequisites, same session, idle machine, one cold `--release` bake at a time. Verify is on in every run below except the timing, border-cost and warm rows, which need production's walk and caches.
- [ ] Whole-PRL `cmp`, identical: hallway, hallway-mini, movement-feel, kinematic-platform, `gate-heavily-lit` (sun), `combat-demo` (embedded emitters), `soft_shadow_test`; with `--no-penumbra-floor`, hallway-mini and `soft_shadow_test`.
- [ ] Verify switch, in the release binary: `POSTRETRO_LIGHTMAP_REACH_VERIFY=1` turns verify on, and the stage line's pairs re-walked equal its pairs culled, cold and warm (research P5); `0` turns it off; unset, it is off and the line reports no re-walks; a malformed value warns once and keeps it off. With it on, hallway, hallway-mini and movement-feel read zero mismatches and pairs re-walked above zero.
- [ ] The coverage proof reads zero unproven spans on hallway, hallway-mini and movement-feel, and the cull runs on all three. On each, `check_watertight`'s spans log at info and the build logs no hole warning.
- [ ] Hallway Lightmap Bake ≤ 0.65× baseline, SH Bake reported as a control (prototype: 0.57× against the pre-fix baseline), and the coverage proof's time reported from its info line. Permit side: the cold run's stage line reads pairs culled at least 40% of pairs walked.
- [ ] Border cost: on the hallway, texels traced inside culled pairs are at most 10% of texels skipped (estimate: about 4%). Above that, stop and report before landing.
- [ ] movement-feel and kinematic-platform: Lightmap Bake within measurement noise of baseline.
- [ ] Warm: the new binary over a cache the baseline binary filled logs `[cache] lightmap_section hit`; with the section memo removed and `--verbose`, it logs a hit for every `lightmap_layer`. Both write a PRL identical to the cold bake, and the stage line says the stage was cached instead of printing zeros.
- [ ] Grep gate on the diff: no new prl-build flag, no spike instrumentation or `POSTRETRO_LM_` variable, and no stage version constant changed. The coverage proof holds no τ literal; it reads the tracer's constant. The cull reads no `check_watertight` output. `affinity_grid::light_portal_reach` keeps every caller it has on `main`, and the reach index takes no runtime cell, runtime portal or blocked-portal input.

### Manual

None required. The cull changes no pixel: every output byte is gated by `cmp` above.

## Path

- **Prototypes on `spike/lightmap-flood-cull-sizing`.** `lm_flood_sizing::ball_flood`, `ball_radius`, `sphere_leaves`, `point_polygon_distance`, `chart_leaves` and `chart_deep` (its inverse is the per-texel rule). Starting points only; the Decisions supersede their tolerances. No spike instrumentation lands. Proposed names: module `lightmap_reach`, type `LightmapReachIndex`.
- **Full-tree portals.** `RuntimeCells::of_partition` generates and drops them. Return them, or regenerate (46 ms on the hallway). They need to live only until every light's reach is built: drop them before the walk, or keep them if the flood runs lazily per light.
- **Coverage proof.** It runs after the oversize-face cut over the faces the walk's BVH holds, before the first pair is walked (research P7). Atlas preparation, cut and BVH rebuild included, runs on every build, so the proof can run there whether the lightmap stage then bakes or hits the cache. Candidates per edge are the opposite-running edges whose faces come within τ of it, from the bake BVH or a spatial query, so cost grows with edge count times local face density. On each sub-span the directed edges present are few: a maximum bipartite matching between the two directions settles it in any order (research P8), and an edge it leaves unmatched is an unproven span. A face's fan diagonals run both ways inside it and cancel, so the proof can read triangles or face loops rebuilt as `lightmap_bake::face_cut::fan_polygon` rebuilds them. `check_watertight`'s sorted breakpoint sweep is the precedent for sub-spans; its quantized line keys are not. An unproven span's midpoint and brush feed the warning through the existing `OpenEdge` and `pipeline::watertight_open_edge_provenance`. The spike's `lm_leak_trace::classify_open_spans` samples, so it is no starting point.
- **Cull placement.** A culled pair is still walked, so the flag belongs at the walk (`lightmap_layer::chart_walk::bake_light_layer_chart_clipped`), not in `plan_charts`. The per-texel mask depends only on the chart, so compute it once per chart and share it across lights (research P3).
- **Sequencing with `mover-light-flow--foundation`.** Both touch the lightmap walk's per-light pass: it adds a partition consumer, this a per-pair flag. Whichever lands second rebases; the cull changes no lit texel, so the foundation's gating counts don't move.
- **Seams.** `lightmap_layer::ExactSkips` and the test tallies in `lightmap_layer/skip_stats.rs`. Its `verify` field and `force_clear` knob are `#[cfg(test)]`; this verify mode needs its switch outside `cfg(test)`, and `force_clear` is the planted-fault precedent. The re-walk is not `ExactSkips::OFF`, which drops the shaft cull too, and it must not re-enter the governor (`governor.rs` header: enter once at the outermost boundary).
- **Rivals.** *The baked cell-visibility relation (id 46),* which the roadmap line suggested: its components are the unbounded flood, which skips nothing on single-component maps. *A dark-shaft test,* proving per chart that triangles block every segment: a wall is split into many faces, so the proof must cover their union, and rays grazing the shared edges leave the same rounding gaps the flood names. *The strict chart rule* keeps 4% of the win.
- **First slice.** The coverage proof and the index, with the two-rooms fixture under verify mode. Then one counted hallway run to confirm the permit side before tuning anything.
- **File size.** `pipeline.rs` is past 2,000 lines: wire through `pipeline/lightmap_stage`, not inline. `lightmap_layer/tests.rs` is past 2,700: new fixtures get their own test module.
- **Docs at landing.** `build_pipeline.md` step 12 §Exact skips gains the reach cull, its contract, the coverage proof with its bound, the tracer's τ, and the never-more-closed invariant. Step 3 says the coverage proof owns hole warnings and the watertightness check logs at info. The roadmap's "lightmap dark-texel cost" keeps its emitter-pairing sizing and drops the reach cull; add the dropped-pairs follow-up.

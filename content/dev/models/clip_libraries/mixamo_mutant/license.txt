Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* The Mixamo Creature Pack (Mutant) on the Mutant rig, plus Falling Idle and a
  hit reaction, for the shadow hunter. It is kept apart from mixamo_brute (the
  same rig) so the two enemies' clips can be tuned separately.
* Clip names and their Mixamo sources:
    idle (breathing idle), idle_b (idle), idle_c (idle 2), walk, run, punch,
    swipe, swipe_b (swiping, creature pack), smash (jump attack),
    smash_b (the pack's second jump attack), roar, roar_b (roaring, pack),
    flex (flexing muscles), jump_a (jumping), jump_b (jumping 2),
    death, death_b (dying, pack), hit_reaction, falling (Falling Idle),
    turn_left_45, turn_left_45_b, turn_right_45, turn_right_45_b, turn_right_90.
  Where the pack and the earlier hand-picked download hold the same clip
  frame for frame, one copy is kept.
* Built with:
    mixamo_to_gltf.py --library --base Mutant.fbx --yaw 180 \
      --in-place smash smash_b hit_reaction
* --yaw 180 turns the rig to face +Z, the front AI facing aims, so root motion
  runs the way bound bodies face.
* smash, smash_b and hit_reaction are pinned in place: the jump attacks land
  1.6 m forward and the hit reaction staggers 1 m back, so the body would snap
  back when the clip ended. Deaths keep their motion: they hold the last frame.
* Turn clips end turned; a state that plays one snaps back to the entity's
  facing when it ends.

## Delegated answers
- **Emitter placement and tick-sound attenuation** — the emitter and the tick sound anchor at the afflicted entity's body centre (the centre of its collision capsule or bounds, falling back to its transform origin when it has none), and the sound uses the default positional one-shot attenuation that impact sounds use. Embers read as coming off the body rather than the feet, and reusing the impact curve keeps status sounds mixed like the hits that caused them.
- **Which entities carry status presence** — only entities with health, because only they can hold an instance (`applyStatus` skips subjects without health), and the payload is omitted while no status is live. An entity with no live status therefore costs nothing on the wire.

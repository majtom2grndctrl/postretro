# reflection-probes

Brief · resumable · reads: `context/lib/index.md` §1.1, §3 · `rendering_pipeline.md` §4, §7.3, §7.3a, §10, §13 · `build_pipeline.md` §Custom FGD, §Compiler pipeline, §Ray traversal, §PRL section IDs, §Build Cache · `resource_management.md` §5, §9 · `scripting.md` §2 · read at afb4acaa4 · builds after `fluid-depth-fog-waves`

## Problem
Owner-requested capability. Water reflects nothing: the engine has no environment-reflection term, and the planned screen-space layer (`plans/drafts/fluid-ssr-reflections`) goes dark on every ray miss — screen edge, behind the camera, the far wall of a vista — because nothing sits beneath it. Players see the lit room mirrored in the water: parallax-correct as they strafe past a pool at eye height, near-mirror at grazing angles, strongest looking across a large pool from high ground, dim looking straight down. Brightness follows the live lighting, flicker included, at a fixed per-pixel cost. Every map's water reflects with no authoring, existing maps included, and a mapper who wants a better fit draws a box. This is the static base layer; the dynamic layer (masked SSR, emitter glints) composites over it later. The probe record is reflector-agnostic so a glass floor or polished stone can read it later; water is its only reader here.

## Decisions
- **Technique: distance-corrected box-projected local probes, scaled per light** (`research.md` §Prior art, §Distance, §Light share). The runtime slab test bounds a short runtime search along the reflected ray against the probe's stored per-texel distance; the tile is sampled toward the hit it finds, or toward the box hit when geometry lies beyond the box. No precomputed intersection table, no per-pixel technique choice by lookup direction, no temporal reprojection, no SSR-exit fallback. The documented `env_cubemap` point entity and its deferred bake tool are superseded; `rendering_pipeline.md` §13 is amended at promotion to permit image-based specular on fluid only.
- **Capture at compile time**, in its own cached prl-build stage, against the static world: movers never appear, `switch` brushes do. Load-time GPU capture is rejected: it is runtime render-to-texture for mirrors, which `resource_management.md` §9 rules out (`research.md` §Load-time capture).
- **Capture shading = albedo × (direct + indirect) + emissive, at the reference state.** Direct is the static lightmap, every animated light's weight map, and the baked `_shadow_type sdf` lights the runtime's per-fragment selection picks at each capture hit (at most four, as `sdf_light_select.wgsl` chooses), with visibility (the lightmap bakes exclude sdf lights). Indirect, base SH plus every animated light's delta, is scaled by the runtime's production indirect weight; a runtime-tuned dev weight then mismatches the bake, accepted (`research.md` §Capture shading). Emissive uses the runtime's per-prefix multiplier; no specular, no dynamic-tier lights.
- **Reference state.** An animated light with a curve at compile time enters at the cycle mean of its runtime clamped curve, a script-targeted light that keeps a Quake `style` curve included; a curveless light enters at its authored colour, a script-targeted light given the flat placeholder included. Either holds whether or not the light starts active.
- **Live lighting by light share.** Per probe the compiler stores the top-K animated lights by contribution, each with its share of the probe's captured light, as scaled above, in directional lobes blended smoothly so a flickering lamp never draws a seam. The fragment scales the capture by ratio(dir) = 1 + Σ_l share_l(dir) × (current_l / ref_l − 1), per RGB channel, clamped at 0; current_l is the light's live value from the per-light compose descriptors the fluid pass already binds, evaluated as SH compose evaluates it, and ref_l its RGB reference, stored in its share entry. Light identity is the AnimatedBakedLights roster index, the same in the compiler and at runtime; lights beyond K stay at reference, and the compiler logs each probe's covered share. The ratio reads no SH, so switches reach it at any distance and promotion never moves it (`research.md` §Light share). Rival rejected: SH-ratio normalization.
- **Storage: one full-sphere octahedral tile per probe**, in the SH atlas's direction mapping and border convention, at a fixed engine tile size (128) carried in the section 54 header, so a later authored size is additive. Full sphere keeps the record usable by a mid-room probe (`research.md` §Capture point). Beside it, a distance tile at the same size, mapping and border holds each texel's mean over its supersamples of 1/|hit − capture point|₁, 0 where the ray hit nothing (R16F). 0 is reserved for a miss and no finite hit stores it, so a later sky term can fill exactly the directions the capture saw nothing.
- **Capture point is stored, 2 mm above a water plane at or below every plane its probe binds**, so lookups stay in the upper hemisphere (`research.md` §Capture point). A face is inside an authored box when its area centroid is, the test binding uses. A derived probe's point is its group's horizontal area centroid; an authored box's is the horizontal area centroid of the up-facing fluid faces on the lowest plane inside it, fixed before binding, so it sits over real water even when a smaller box claims that basin. A centroid not over its own faces' water moves to the nearest point on those faces. An optional horizontal-only `_capture_offset` moves an authored point, for a pillar standing in the water there; an offset that is vertical, malformed or leaves the box is a build error naming the entity. A box binding planes at more than one height keeps the probe and warns, naming the heights.
- **Probes are compiler-derived by default; a brush entity overrides.** Every up-facing fluid face no authored box claims gets a derived probe. Each face fits a box from its own area centroid, clamped to the hull, enclosing what the water sees: a pool sunk below its room's floor fits the room, not the pit. Groups are the connected components of the box-match relation, within the fit tolerance, among one fluid entity's faces at one plane height, so face order never changes them; a group stores the union of its members' boxes. An authored `reflection_volume` brush (brush volumes are named `<thing>_volume`) has an AABB that is its projection box and influence region: an up-facing face binds the smallest authored box containing its area centroid, ties going to the earlier box in map entity order; a sloped or side face binds nothing; no overlap priority KVP in v1. Rival rejected: authored-only probes, which leave every existing map's water dark until a mapper boxes it.
- **Drops and cap.** A probe whose capture point lies in solid, inside any fluid, or outside the hull is dropped with a warning naming the entity, authored or derived; so is an authored box binding no face. Order: authored capture-point drops (a box with no up-facing fluid face inside has no point and drops here, with the binding-no-face warning), then binding among the kept boxes, then authored boxes binding no face (they claim nothing, so nothing rebinds), then derived probes for unclaimed faces and their drops, then the cap. A dropped box's faces bind as if it were absent; a dropped derived probe's faces bind nothing. A per-level cap fits the probe array's portable layer limit: authored probes past it refuse the build, naming the cap; derived probes past it are dropped, largest bound surface area kept first, with one warning naming the count.
- **Runtime placement.** The fluid pass gains the probe array, the distance array and the share table and reads the per-light compose descriptors it already binds; storage use is unchanged and the forward pass is untouched. Evaluation is per fragment: direction varies too fast for vertex evaluation (§7.3a's faceting rule). Fluid faces bound to no probe render exactly as today. The capture bakes every lighting term together, so the probe term draws only while every lighting bit of the per-term lighting mask (`rendering_pipeline.md` §4) is on; any isolating mask omits it.
- **Weight: Schlick Fresnel with F0 = 0.02 × `surfaceReflectivity`**, range 0..=50, topping at F0 = 1: 0 is none, 1 is water, about 2 reads as glass, about 10 as wet polished stone, 50 a mirror at every angle. F0 is the quantity a later glass or stone reader takes from its material, so one meaning serves every reflector (`research.md` §Fresnel). It is a fluid presentation field (`scripting.md` §2): client-local in co-op, out of the digest, never feeds drift.
- **Viewed from below, the probe term is off.** Fluid faces draw double-sided; when the view ray reaches the face from its down side, the reflection weight is 0 and the fragment renders as today. Snell's window and total internal reflection are non-goals.
- **Capture shadow** (owner, amendment 2026-10-15). A fragment the stored distance places behind a surface seen from the capture point (the far side of an object in or beside the water) draws no probe term, faded over a pinned margin; the capture holds nothing it could reflect there. The compiler warns, naming the probe, when the capture point sees less than half of a probe's bound water area.
- **Composite: `F·R + (1−F)·(surface over fog over scene)`, coverage `F + (1−F)·a`.** Opacity never dims the reflection: clear water mirrors fully at grazing, and a zero-opacity fluid still draws its reflection. This settles the same question for the SSR layer.
- **Look is the author's style switch**, `render.reflections.style`, `"pixel"` (default) or `"smooth"`, the vocabulary `fluid-ssr-reflections` adopts. Smooth reflects the flat face normal per fragment. Pixel evaluates one direction per water texel cell, tilted by the cell's whole-texel displayed offset with an engine-owned constant (§7.3a), so the reflection breaks into the blocks the albedo moves in. `fluid-surface-motion` made reflection style author-owned because reflections were post effects; this in-shader term extends that rule deliberately, so one switch governs both reflection layers. Frame capture loads no manifest and draws reflections at the defaults: reflectivity 1, pixel style.
- **Non-goals.** Probe reads on any non-fluid receiver (world, mover or model materials): the forward pass has no free sampled-texture binding (`rendering_pipeline.md` §4, §7.3). Characters, movers, projectiles and dynamic lights in the water: the dynamic layer, a follow-up amending `fluid-ssr-reflections`. Map-placed models (`prop_mesh`): runtime entities that never enter the compiler BVH, so no probe holds them. Per-light probe layers composed at runtime, and streaming: `research.md` §Light share, §Budget. Prefiltered roughness mips: water's normal is flat per face. Blending across box boundaries: a seam at a brush boundary is accepted when both boxes match their rooms. Layered (multi-hit) distance or colour, and the hidden faces of objects near the water: a single capture point never sees them. Splitting a probe where its capture point cannot see or resolve its water (owner, 2026-10-15): the screen-space layer covers most of that water at the grazing views where it shows, and the rest — the underside of a dock over open water — waits for that layer, probe-to-probe continuation, or a planar reflection.

## Wire format
Section id 54, optional: absent means no probes. Little-endian, `u32` counts and indices, `f32` scalars, authored probes in map entity order then derived probes in fluid entity order, mirroring EnvironmentVolumes (id 52) framing. Header: internal version 2, tile dimension, tile border, atlas format tag (the lightmap's irradiance format tags), distance format tag (f16 inverse L1 distance, the only value), lights per probe K, lobe count, probe count, binding count. Per probe: source entity index (the authored brush, or the fluid entity of a derived probe), box min and max in engine space, capture point, capture layer index, then K share entries, each the light's AnimatedBakedLights roster index (a reserved value marks an unused entry), its RGB reference, and its share per lobe. Bindings: (volume index, face index, probe index) triples, so id 52 is unchanged. Then the atlas payload, layer-major, each tile's bytes in the stated format. Then the distance payload, layer-major, one tile per probe at the same dimension and border, little-endian f16; 0 means no surface. A header of a later version is refused with a message naming the section.

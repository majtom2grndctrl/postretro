Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* scene.gltf is the Sketchfab export, unchanged (used by the target dummy and
  the animation demo).
* model.gltf / model.bin are the shadow hunter's body: the same mesh with its
  Mixamo-named skeleton, converted with mixamo_to_gltf.py (scale 100,
  --yaw 180) and bound to clip_libraries/mixamo_mutant. Then:
    - lifted 0.936 m so the soles sit at y = 0 (the export's origin was at
      mid-body);
    - hit zones stripped: the converter zones only head and legs, and a ray
      missing every capsule misses the model, so the torso would be
      unhittable; hits land on the descriptor's authored hitbox instead;
    - re-rested from its A-pose onto the library's T-pose with
      tools/rest_to_library.py, so the Mutant clips play as authored instead
      of swinging the arms across the body.
* The base-color material was wired by hand after conversion (a converter
  rerun drops it); the engine reads base color only.

## Owner ruling (2026-10-11): push rule 3 → protected drift layer
Rule 3's mechanism is amended. The owner chose the knockback-layer pattern over a stateless cap exemption, a relaxed cap, or a per-tick restatement of M15:
1. Horizontal push accrues in a protected drift layer on the movement component. Intent speed caps and steering act on locomotion only; the layer rejoins before collision, which projects it like knockback, so walls absorb it.
2. Vertical push adds directly to vertical velocity, which no intent caps; gravity, lift-off and the terminal cap along the push govern it.
3. The medium decays the layer: ground friction while grounded (steady drift ≈ push / `GROUND_STOP_FRICTION`), the fluid's viscosity while swimming (current ≈ push / viscosity), and nothing airborne (momentum carries out of a wind volume until landing friction).
4. The layer is predicted and reconciled like `knockback_velocity`. Its wire field takes the branch's one `WIRE_VERSION`/`SNAPSHOT_VERSION` bump, which `Swim` (Task 7) then shares.
Amend `movement.md` §6 (Knockback / caps) and the brief's rule 3 wording at land-the-plane. M15 stands as written.

### Evidence that raised it
**False premise:** "Push adds to velocity after the state intent and is never clipped by the intent's speed caps." Adding after the intent protects the push only on the tick it is added. Velocity carries into the next tick. There, with movement input held and `air.bunny_hop` off, the airborne cap in `intents/normal.rs` and `intents/crouching.rs` clamps total horizontal speed to the walk/run/crouch tier (`movement.md` §6: airborne Normal re-clamps carried momentum). With input held, airborne wind stalls at that cap plus `push·dt`; with no input it grows until the terminal cap. M15's airborne half ("not clipped by the air speed cap") therefore fails under the Decision's mechanism. Evidence: `crates/physics/src/movement/push_tests.rs` `airborne_push_survives_the_air_cap_on_the_tick_it_is_added` pins only the per-tick half. The rest of Task 4 (lift-off, terminal speed, ground drift, slide, agents, upward level gravity, harness) is built and passing, and is unaffected by options (a)–(c).

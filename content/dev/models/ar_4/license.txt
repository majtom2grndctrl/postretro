Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* AR_4 extracted from Quaternius "Modular Sci Fi Guns" (Nov 2021) and converted to
  the engine's external glTF layout (model.gltf + model.bin + textures) via
  prop_to_gltf.py: gun + magazine joined into one mesh node, scaled x0.5 (~0.9 m
  long), origin relocated to the grip for hand_r socket attachment.
* The source materials are flat baseColorFactor colors with no textures or UVs.
  Each was baked to a 4x4 solid-color PNG (linear -> sRGB) and wired as that
  material's base-color texture, so the engine's texture-based model loader
  reproduces the intended colors (a mesh without UVs samples every vertex at
  texture coordinate (0,0), which is uniform for a solid color).

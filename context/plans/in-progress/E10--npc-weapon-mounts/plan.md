- Path, near-path gate: the muzzle-to-target leg runs "beside the moment check in the fire proposal" → it runs at the launch in apply (`crates/ai/src/npc_launch.rs`), after the interim aiming classification, which only apply can complete (an earlier outcome's reaction lands after compute, NM-6, NM-15). Compute's proposal is unchanged. One decision per due shot serves the first shot and every burst shot, so the tests and the launch share one pose (NM-1). A first shot that cannot launch (held, or not aiming) leaves its activation unstarted: the weapon transaction restores, nothing spends, no cooldown restarts and a fresh turn grant is released (NM-13); a clip moment held there whiffs and counts once, an attack with no clip moment counts once per hold and proposes again next tick (N5). - Path, interim classifier: the record is `ClipClock.interruption` (`None`, `Foreign`, `FailedOwnSwitch`), serialized and in `PartialEq`. `begin_tick` sets `Foreign` on a foreign switch, apply sets it on a switch since compute, and any own switch that draws (switched or already in state) clears it; a failed own switch sets `FailedOwnSwitch` unless `Foreign` holds (NM-32). The launch reads the own draw from `plan_own_draw` (apply's switch predicate with its flinch-route suppression), hoisted before `advance_actor` and reused for the switch itself.

## Delegated answers
- Fixture attack clip — not `punch`: measured with `socket_dump`, `cannon_tip`'s barrel at `punch`'s strike (t = 0.267) points 41° off the model's front and 40° down, and sideways later. `cannon_brute` aims in a `smash_windup` stance and fires on the release into `smash_strike` with no clip moment: the held windup pose and `smash_strike` at t = 0 put the barrel 26° off front and level, the only brute pose a stance sequences inside the 35° cone. Shots aim from the muzzle at the target's aim point, so the barrel's 26° is presentation only. Pinned by `cannon_brute_attack_clip_points_the_cannon_forward_at_the_shot`.
- `muzzleSocket` on a player pawn's entry — validates, mounts nothing, and is ignored for fire origin. Pawns launch from placement plus `muzzleOffset`, which client prediction reproduces.
- An entry mounting several models with more than one `muzzle` socket — warn once per level load naming the archetype and weapon, and launch from the first in socket-name order. Deterministic and loud, with no new key.
- Near-path test and the eye verdict's loss grace — no grace: a raw test on the fire tick. A grace would launch a shot into cover.

## Owner ruling needed
- Near-path leg toward a nearer aim point (Decision "Only cover near the gun holds a shot", Acceptance N3). The Decision reads "With the target's aim point nearer, the whole path is tested." Review round 1 found that a surface within the projectile's radius of a point-blank aim point (a lintel just over a player's eye) held every shot while sight saw the target. The build stops that leg one projectile radius short of the aim point, as sight forgives a hit at its aim point (`npc_launch::near_path_blocked`). Round 2 traced it: every obstacle on the line between muzzle and aim point is still caught; only the off-axis band within the last radius, inside the target's own hitbox, is skipped. Proposed wording: "With the target's aim point nearer, the path is tested to one shot radius short of it." Alternative: revert to the full path and accept point-blank holds under low surfaces.

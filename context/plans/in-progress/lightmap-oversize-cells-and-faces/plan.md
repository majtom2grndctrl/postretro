- Animated chunk subdivision split UV midpoints; inside a cut face's sub-chart the drift could pass `chunk_atlas_rect`'s snap and overlap sibling rects or drop a texel row. Chunks now split on whole texels of the chart's interior grid, so every chunk edge is a texel edge. An unsplit chunk keeps its chart's exact UV bounds and is byte-identical to before; a split chunk moves to the nearest texel edge. The weight-map key hashes the chunk section, so no stage bump.
- Brief *Decisions* "the fewest sub-faces of near-equal extent" → two pieces when two fit, else the fewest whose longest piece fits with overlap on both sides; that can be one more than an exact fit of the end pieces needs. `build_pipeline.md` says so.
- Three commits landed since the brief's `19fb3fc40` read. Only `16c330a68` touches source: it rekeys the SDF atlas and CellResidencySet on density-independent inputs, matching the SDF and id-51 Decisions. Every cited symbol was re-read at `e5eb0807c` and matches the brief.

- **Sub-chart overlap width.** 2 parent-grid texels past each cut line (4-texel overlap). Overlap texels bake bit-identical before encoding; the encoded check measured the worst decoded step at a cut (0.085) inside BC6H's error on ordinary texels of the same bake (0.113), so aligned placement was not pursued. The owner's seam check (README check 7) is the visual gate.
- **kinematic-platform gable and lights** (owner ruling 2026-10-01: two spot lights, one static and one animated, no point lights). East gable, ridge along x at y 8, apex z 3460 (87.9 m); west hip at 3:1 so the west wall stays a 63.8 m rectangle; all four walls raised to z 3476 so no horizontal plane splits the room below the ridge and the shell stays closed. Low brushes split the east wall at 4.06 m, so the cut face is the wall's south half from 4.06 m to the apex (83.8 m, 2100×274 texels), cut once about 46 m up. Spots at x 2900, z 1610, y −300 (static) and −380 (animated, `style 2`), `_light_size 32`, cones 20°/35°, aimed so the cut sits in their cone fade; a pillar at x 3190–3206, y −198…−150 throws a soft stripe from each across the cut and splits the roof slopes under the chart limit.

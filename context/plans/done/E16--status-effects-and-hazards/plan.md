## Delegated answers
- **Emitter placement and tick-sound attenuation** — the emitter and the tick sound anchor at the afflicted entity's body centre (the centre of its collision capsule or bounds, falling back to its transform origin when it has none), and the sound uses the default positional one-shot attenuation that impact sounds use. Embers read as coming off the body rather than the feet, and reusing the impact curve keeps status sounds mixed like the hits that caused them.
- **Which entities carry status presence** — only entities with health, because only they can hold an instance (`applyStatus` skips subjects without health), and the payload is omitted while no status is live. An entity with no live status therefore costs nothing on the wire.

### Status stage measurement (AC 49)

`cargo test --release -p postretro-sim --lib -- --ignored --nocapture status_stage_damaging_stress`, 600 measured ticks after 120 warm, this container:

| Run | Stage time per tick | Allocations per tick |
|---|---|---|
| Markers (`damage: 0`): the stage's own bookkeeping | 9.0 µs | 0 |
| Damaging, no policy: + the damage chokepoint per tick | 119.3 µs | 2135 |
| Damaging, one impact policy: + policy evaluation per tick | 235.3 µs | 4698 |

The chokepoint adds about 110 µs and 2135 allocations per fixed tick (its contributor record, source-id string and impact dispatch per damaging tick); one policy adds about 116 µs and 2563 allocations. The stage's own bookkeeping allocates nothing. Per the brief, chokepoint and policy cost are reported, not gated.

Hot paths and constraints carried by the tasks: the status stage (task 4) allocates nothing per tick for its own bookkeeping, reuses a scratch held by the sim, walks only entities holding a `StatusComponent`, and reports as its own CPU stage; presence serialization (task 7) sends a `u64` only for entities with a live status and rides the existing component-removal path; presentation (task 8) updates on presence change, not per frame.

## Review

Preflight before review was green apart from two build breaks it caught or missed. It caught the non-test build missing `sound_events::entity_key`. It missed the `observability`/`observe-live` builds missing `ComponentKind::StatusPresence`; that build also hit an existing test helper that predates `swim` and `vault`. All three are fixed.

`/review-panel` (9 reviewers over 4 slices plus a seam pass) found 2 must-fix, 9 should-fix and about 15 nits or drift. All were fixed in one `/fix-review-findings` round, then the gate re-ran green: fmt, clippy `-D warnings`, the full `cargo test`, and `--features observability,observe-live`. Fixed:
- An off-tick `applyStatus` on a target at 0 health latches now; health is judged at the next tick start, after the recovery policy evaluates (P32). Applications at that point return `StatusApplyOutcome::Latched`.
- A late-authorized remote hit on an instance's expiry tick revives the instance ended that tick, keeping its serial, phase and stacks (P33, AC 19).
- The client clears the host status-name table wherever it drops its tuning, and `statusNames` is validated on decode. An empty presence payload has its own error variant.
- The status emitter and tick sound sit at the same body centre on host and client, and the tick-sound cadence follows whole fixed ticks. Emitters skip entities despawned before render.
- A hot reload that reorders fluids remaps `ImmersionComponent.fluid` by name.
- Status ranges are checked in f64.
- Docs: the retired producer-gate text is gone from the SDK docs and the scripting reference; the reaction verb table, the binding-sugar list and the fluid table cover statuses; the `burn_death` recipe note is added.
- Comment drift is fixed throughout.

Left as is, with reasons:
- A refresh keeps the instance's first source id. "The applying hit's source id" is satisfied by creation.
- An *unguarded* self-reapplying policy forks a second instance once its applier despawns. That is the documented P1 footgun, and the recipe guards it.
- A target with a pending deferred despawn keeps ticking until it is removed.
- The immediate-application flag has no panic guard. There is no early return in its span.
- TS and Luau fail in differently shaped errors on non-table `defineStatus` options; both fail load.
- A late remote rocket kill lands after its tick's due burn tick, which is inherent to post-tick authorization.
- A client that disconnects without unloading keeps the old name table until its next level unload.
- Naming nits: `settle_frame_end_impacts`, and the `AppDrain` stamp on trigger-bound damage.

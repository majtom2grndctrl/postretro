# Lighting Scale — Variable Base-Probe Density

> **Track:** Lighting / build pipeline + renderer — storage, VRAM, and per-fragment sampler bandwidth of the SH irradiance volume.
> **Builds on:** `context/plans/done/lighting-scale--sh-base-atlas-at-rest-slimming/` (id-34 v9 compact base, reserved `density_level`, load-derived indirection word) · `context/plans/done/lighting-scale--sh-adaptive-coarsening-v2/` and `context/plans/done/lighting-scale--delta-sh-probe-coarsening/` (the composed-receiver-error classifier `classify_levels`, three-level bricks, per-brick compose workgroups).
> **Evidence:** `context/plans/drafts/lighting-scale--sh-probe-density-coarsenability-spike/findings.md`. **Foreclosure record:** `context/research/base-density-forward-predictor.md`.
> **Retained material (by reference, not re-derived):** `research.md` in this folder — three-level rescope arithmetic, industry survey, hardware target and Pascal f16 contract, cap posture.

## Goal

Cut the SH irradiance volume's runtime footprint — composed-atlas VRAM and per-fragment sampler traffic — by storing each 4×4×4 probe brick at one of three densities (64 / 8 / 1 tiles) chosen by the shipped composed-receiver-error classifier, and by compacting every SH atlas the samplers read to exactly those stored tiles. The loss is by design, steered into open, smooth, low-contribution volume; fidelity near surfaces and sharp lit regions is preserved by the classifier and by mapper protection. The target that binds the numbers is the owner's: very performant on GTX 10-series, smooth on laptops with shared-memory iGPUs.

## Measured basis

Recorded values (owner sweep and spike runs; Task 8 re-derives the "before" side on the measurement host). `campaign-test.map` at the 1.0 m shipping default (`DEFAULT_PROBE_SPACING`):

| Quantity | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Grid probes / valid probes | 194,028 / 57,128 (29.4%) | at-rest-slimming Measured basis |
| Affinity bricks (`ceil(dims/4)`, grid 74×23×114) | 19×6×29 = 3,306 | spike findings, Measured finding 4 |
| Composed **total** atlas VRAM today | 194,028 tiles × 288 B ≈ 55.9 MB `Rgba16Float`, dense per probe | `create_total_atlas_texture` sizes to `atlas_dimensions × layer_count` |
| Composed **direct** atlas VRAM today (present whenever id 35 is) | same 55.9 MB; a third full-size intermediate atlas when id 45 is present | `resolve_direct_atlas_usage` |
| Base id 34 at rest (v9, BC6H, valid-only) | ~2.1 MB tiles + 1.55 MB dense metadata (projection) | at-rest-slimming |
| Per-fragment sampler cost today | 8 corner taps per atlas (world/fog: 1 atlas; entities: 2) + 8 moments loads when probe occlusion is on | `sh_sample.wgsl` corner walk |
| Coarsenable bricks (composed error gate) | campaign 43.9% @0.02 / 75.1% @0.10; kinematic-platform 83.8% / 88.8%; stress-warren-mini 18.5% / 41.2% | spike Measured finding 1 |

Two independent factors drive the composed-atlas reduction, and the findings note attributes them separately: **valid-only membership** (uniform L0 already drops 194,028 → 57,128 stored tiles on campaign-test, 3.4×) and **level coarsening** on top (an L1 brick stores 8 tiles, an L2 brick 1). Per-fragment traffic falls with locality rather than tile count: an L2 brick's eight corner walks hit one tile.

## Scope

### In scope

- **id 34 v10 / id 35 v3 format contracts:** `density_level` gains meaning (0/1/2, brick-uniform); both sections' at-rest payloads carry the brick's **stored set** in brick-major order under one shared atlas geometry; version bumps with the existing named reject.
- **Compiler:** stored-set emission for id 34 and id 35 (L2 mean synthesized at bake); a base-density classification stage reusing `classify_levels` over composed-tile error, the mapper protection union, a delta pin that keeps storage no coarser than any present delta entry, seam smoothing; a per-map fidelity knob (worldspawn KVP + CLI) and a measurement-only forced-level flag.
- **Runtime:** a load-derived per-probe indirection word (level + validity + slot) fed to the compose passes through the existing binding and to every sampler through the depth-moments texture's unused B/A channels; composed total and direct atlases sized to stored tiles; loader cross-checks.
- **Compose passes (ids 34+27, 35+41, 35+45):** slot-addressed base reads and stored-slot-only writes.
- **Samplers:** one shared WGSL resolve helper replacing `probe_tile_origin` arithmetic in `sh_sample.wgsl`, consumed unchanged in signature by the five reader shaders; the SDF shadow pass's moments decode.
- **Dev-tools continuity and docs:** probe-marker readback over the stored atlas, a density-level marker mode, mapper docs, FGD.
- **Findings note:** before → after VRAM, section bytes, per-pass GPU time on the available test adapter (named; Turing-class), level histogram and pin attribution, manual-visual read.

### Out of scope (non-goals)

- **The bake-time win** (skipping dense probe bakes in coarse bricks). Closed in source by `context/research/base-density-forward-predictor.md`: the error that forces density is manufactured in composition, downstream of the base bake a forward predictor must decide about, so no cheap forward predictor clears the bar. Coarse bricks bake all 64 probes and store few.
- **Any new density predictor.** The shipped classifier (`classify_levels`, composed-receiver error) steers the loss. Surface distance and distance-to-light are dead predictors (spike Measured finding 2); the bake's light-contribution helpers (`incident_radiance_at_point`, `light_contribution_lambert`, `falloff`, `light_reaches_point` in `sh_bake.rs`) are not consumed here — the composed field already embodies delivered contribution.
- **Wire changes to the delta sections (ids 27/41/45).** Their payloads are keyed on dense metadata validity (`valid_probe_mask_for_affinity_cell` reads `base.probes[i].validity`) and their own `cell_levels`, and their affinity grid derives from `grid_dimensions`, none of which this spec changes. No bump; the Invariants table pins the one new cross-section constraint the loader checks.
- **Adaptive coarsening of ids 27 and 45.** They stay uniform L0 (the v2 ruling); this spec's delta pin honors that by keeping storage L0 in every cell they reach. Relaxing it is Open question 1.
- **Lossiness or parity gates.** No AC compares against the dense baseline; acceptance is a manual-visual hunt plus measured before → after numbers (`experimental_spikes.md`).
- **Runtime density levels, streaming, camera cascades, probe relocation, dilation, per-region fidelity overrides** (`research.md` survey dispositions). Per-region control is the existing `sh_protect_volume` (force L0).
- **Billboard direct scatter (ids 47/48).** Dense per-probe `texture_3d` sampled by probe index, never through the atlas resolve (`sample_billboard_direct_scatter` in `billboard.wgsl`); 8 B/probe.
- **Depth-moment texture compaction.** It stays dense over the grid; its format changes (Float → Uint) to carry the word, its size does not.
- **A cap or budget for the stored atlases.** Stored tiles ≤ dense tiles by construction, so the existing `atlas_fits` gate on stored geometry is the only bound; delta caps (256 MiB aggregate, 128 MiB loader floor) are inherited unchanged.

## Direction

**Problem.** The composed SH atlases are dense over the world-AABB grid — one 288 B tile for every probe, invalid or smooth or not — so their VRAM and every fragment's eight taps scale with probe density regardless of where density buys image quality. The at-rest slimming compacted the base at rest but left the composed atlases dense so samplers never changed; the dense composed atlas is exactly the VRAM and bandwidth this spec exists to cut.

**Prior commitments.**
- The shipped delta coarsening reconstructs dropped probes in the compose (one workgroup per brick, kept lattice in shared memory) and keeps the composed atlas dense. This spec inverts that boundary deliberately: reconstruction moves into the per-fragment sample path, so the composed atlas can shrink. The delta compose's brick structure is reused; its reconstruction math (`sh_reconstruct::reconstruct_l1_tile` / `reconstruct_l2_tile`) is the one definition the sampler must match.
- `AFFINITY_FACTOR = 4` is locked (compose workgroup-coupled); bricks are the shipped 4×4×4 cells.
- The reserved `density_level` byte in the 8 B probe record (`OCTAHEDRAL_PROBE_STRIDE`) is the field this spec populates; v9 rejects nonzero, so the bump changes validation semantics with no record-layout churn.
- The composed-atlas invariant I1 of the v2 spec (dense `Rgba16Float` D2Array, untouched samplers) is **retired here by design** — it was written so the later base-density work would meet an unchanged seam, which this spec is.
- The composed-receiver-error classifier and its operating point (`CoarsenParams::default()`) are inherited; the fidelity knob scales the base classification's gates only, never id 41's.
- Hardware target (owner): the binding low floor is **laptop shared-memory iGPUs** — runnable and smooth — which sit below `rendering_pipeline.md` §10's GTX 16-series (Turing) desktop floor; the smoothness goal is the divergence to capture in `context/lib/` at promotion. Desktop support is a **non-binding ballpark** (roughly Pascal-and-up), not a committed floor. Real testing is on the available Turing hardware: measured findings name that adapter and no task claims a Pascal-class number. Invariant I4 (f16 storage / f32 arithmetic) is kept as the **existing** shipped contract — safe and cheap on every target, the Turing test card included; native WGSL `enable f16` stays foreclosed as a conservative default (a Pascal-class desktop runs native fp16 at 1/64 rate), and this spec builds the f32 reconstruction path regardless, so the choice costs nothing on the test hardware.
- Pre-release no-compat-shim posture (`development_guide.md` §1.6): version bumps reject stale sections.
- Per-stage binding budgets (`rendering_pipeline.md` §7.4, §10): forward FRAGMENT sits at 8/8 storage buffers and 16/16 sampled textures; the indirect compose BGL at 8/8 storage buffers. The sampler-side indirection therefore cannot take a new binding; it rides the depth-moments texture that every sampler pipeline already binds (Invariant I5).

**Alternatives rejected.**
- *Split into two specs — valid-only uniform-L0 composed compaction first, per-brick levels as a fast-follow* (the `sh-base-atlas-at-rest-slimming` precedent, whose "Why this is its own spec" split one certain win from one risky win): rejected here because both halves pay the *same* irreversible sampler door. Compacting the **composed** atlas retires the dense `atlas_dimensions` sampler contract (`crates/level-format/src/sh_volume.rs`, the "dense `atlas_dimensions` sampler contract" note) regardless of level — unlike the predecessor's at-rest compaction, where compose re-expanded to that dense contract so no sampler changed. A split would pay the one-way-door cost twice for no reversibility gain. Instead Task 2 emits uniform L0 first and AC12 records dense → uniform-L0 → classified as three points, staging the same certain-then-risky milestone inside one spec.
- *Compose-side reconstruction with a dense composed atlas* (the shipped delta shape): forfeits the whole win — the dense composed atlas is the cost.
- *Two levels or a deeper tree:* rejected on the retained rescope arithmetic (`research.md`).
- *One level map per composed atlas* (indirect vs direct): would let world geometry coarsen where only the direct term is sharp, but needs two words per probe — no room in one 32-bit channel pair — and two resolves. Rejected now; recorded as the fallback if Task 8 measures the single-level pin cost as large (Open question 2).
- *Brick-snapped sampling* (interpolate only the containing brick's lattice): a sample cell straddling a brick face belongs to neither brick, and adjacent bricks reconstruct the shared face differently — a seam at every face. Rejected; the base-lattice corner walk is retained and each corner resolves through its own brick.
- *L1 stores valid corners only (kept-rank, as the deltas do):* the sampler would need the brick's validity mask to rank a corner. Rejected: L1 stores all eight corner slots (invalid corners as zero tiles, alpha 0) so a corner's slot is arithmetic from the brick's base slot.
- *A separate per-probe word texture or storage buffer for samplers:* no free sampled-texture or storage slot in forward's fragment stage (§10 budgets). Rejected on budget, not preference.
- *Restructuring the delta CSR sections with the base grid:* not needed (Out of scope, warrant there).

**Placement.** Level assignment is a compiler stage (bake-time, post-hoc over composed tiles); the format carries one level per brick; the renderer derives the word at load and both compose and sample paths consume it. The lever is bake-time, so its home is prl-build (CLI flag, worldspawn KVP, and the TUI config that already exposes `--sh-probe-spacing`), never a runtime setting; the engine's dev-tools get a read-only level overlay.

**Foreclosure / reversibility.** The sampler resolve is the one-way door: after it lands every SH reader depends on the word and the stored-slot geometry. Undoing it means re-widening the composed atlases and restoring tile arithmetic in five shaders and three compose passes — a rewrite, not a flag. The format side is cheap to undo pre-release (re-bake). Nothing here forecloses per-region fidelity, streaming, or a later two-level-map split: the word's bit budget is the only pressure, stated in Wire format.

### Task 5: Sampler resolve — one helper, five readers, SDF decode

This task owns the moments-texture format atom, which must land in one change or bind-group and pipeline creation fail validation: `upload_depth_moment_texture` (`sh_volume.rs`) creates `Rgba16Uint` (same extent and bytes-per-row); the BGL entry for `BIND_SH_DEPTH_MOMENTS` in `sh_bind_group_layout_entries` (inherited by the mesh superset) and the group-1 binding-2 entry in `sdf_shadow.rs` become `Uint`; every declaration becomes `texture_3d<u32>` — the five readers `forward.wgsl`, `fog_volume.wgsl`, `billboard.wgsl` (group 3), `skinned_mesh.wgsl`, `kinematic_brush.wgsl` (group 4), and `sdf_shadow.wgsl` (whose `sample_open_distance` decodes `.r` via `unpack2x16float`); Chebyshev decodes moments as `unpack2x16float(r | (g << 16))`, bit-identical values. Then, in `crates/renderer/src/shaders/sh_sample.wgsl`, replace `probe_tile_origin` with a resolve that takes a corner probe index and returns its sampled tile: load the probe's word from `sh_depth_moments` (the same `textureLoad(idx)` coordinate the Chebyshev read uses), decode level / validity / slot, and per level: L0 → one `textureSampleLevel` at the slot; L2 → one at the brick's slot; L1 → for each of the brick's corners whose `trilinear_weight` for this probe's local coordinate is nonzero, one tap at `brick_slot + corner_index` (canonical `corner_locals` order), accumulate `weight × alpha × rgb`, renormalize by `Σ weight × alpha` (exactly `reconstruct_l1_tile` with alpha as the absent-corner signal). This per-corner definition is the **value** contract. The **cost** contract (`research.md` §Sampler tap cost): a sample cell whose eight corners lie in one L1 or L2 brick must issue at most eight tile taps (L1: the brick's eight corner slots with the cell's brick-relative trilinear weights; L2: one tap) — the whole-cell path is mandatory, not an optimization, because the per-corner arithmetic alone issues up to 64 taps for a body-interior L1 cell; straddling cells may issue up to 32 taps but never touch more than eight distinct tiles. The helper stays atlas-parameterized (`sample_probe_atlas_tex(atlas, idx, dir)` keeps its signature) so `sample_sh_indirect_corners_pair` and `sample_sh_indirect_direct_corners` are unchanged apart from validity: `is_valid` comes from the word, not from `sample.a` (the complete inventory of alpha-validity reads is the `>= 0.5` test in `sample_sh_indirect_corners_pair` and the one in `sample_sh_indirect_direct_corners`). Fog and any path with depth visibility off now performs the word load it previously skipped (one `textureLoad` per corner) — a recorded cost, not a defect. No `enable f16`; all arithmetic f32 (Invariant I4). Tests: the resolve-arithmetic CPU pin (AC3) in `render-cpu`; the existing shader-binding inventory test extended to assert the moments declaration type in every reader; AC6 budget tests.

## Wire format

Constraint-level; field order and exact encodings are implementer-pinned at landing. Little-endian, section-internal version first, named reject on mismatch, as today.

- **id 34 (v10).** Grid geometry fields unchanged in meaning. Probe records keep the 8 B stride; `density_level` ∈ {0,1,2}, identical across a brick, 0 on partial edge bricks, L1 only with ≥1 valid corner. One atlas geometry block (per-layer dimensions, tiles per row, tiles per layer, layer count) describing the stored-tile atlas over N_s; format tag (`IRRADIANCE_FORMAT_BC6H` / `_RGBA16F`) and byte-length identity over that geometry. Payload membership and order: bricks x-fastest over the affinity grid; within a brick the stored set of Task 1 (L0 valid probes x-fastest; L1 eight corners in `corner_locals` order, zero tile for an invalid corner; L2 one mean tile). No per-brick slot table on disk — slots derive from metadata by prefix sum (Invariant I3).
- **id 35 (v3).** Same atlas geometry block, tag, and payload order as id 34's stored set for the same map; no metadata (levels and validity come from id 34; the loader cross-checks geometry and length).
- **ids 27 / 41 / 45.** Unchanged. The one new constraint is cross-section and load-time: for every cell with ≥1 CSR entry, `density_level[cell] ≤ cell_levels[cell]` (storage never coarser than the delta).
- **Indirection word (runtime only, never serialized).** One 32-bit word per grid probe carrying level (2 bits), validity (1 bit), and a slot (≥ 28 bits: the probe's own stored slot at L0, the brick's base slot at L1/L2). The all-zero word must decode as invalid — invalid probes leave their B/A halves zero, and the dummy moments texel is zero. The same array feeds the compose storage buffers and the moments texture's B/A halves; the moments texture is `Rgba16Uint` with RG = the f16 moment bits unchanged.

## Open questions

1. **Delta pin vs ids 27/45 (owner call, the biggest open question).** Ids 27 and 45 ship uniform L0 (v2 ruling: script/animation amplitudes have no bake-known bound), so Invariant I2 pins storage to L0 in every cell an animated light reaches — forfeiting this spec's win there. The alternative lets the brick level govern and coarsens 27/45 entries to it at bake, which is the first adaptive coarsening of those sections and reopens the v2 safety ruling. Recorded default: preserve the ruling; Task 8 reports the pinned fraction (AC14) so the owner decides on measurement.
2. **Single level map.** If AC14 shows the shared level (gated by the composed field including direct) forfeits much of the indirect atlas's coarsenability, the fallback is one level per composed atlas at the cost of a second word — a follow-on spec, not an agent improvisation.
3. **Fog's added word loads** (P14): expected small; AC15 records the fog pass time.
4. **`--sh-density-force-level` disposition** after Task 8: keep as a measurement flag beside `--sh-protect-aabb`, or retire.
5. **Fidelity semantics:** a multiplier on the two relative gates is the minimal knob; discrete named presets (near-lossless / perceptual / loose, the spike's 0.02 / 0.10 / 0.25 rows) may read better to mappers. Decide at Task 6 with the multiplier as the default.

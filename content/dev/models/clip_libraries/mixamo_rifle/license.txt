Packaging notes (PostRetro):
* Clips from the Mixamo "Pro Rifle Pack", plus firing_rifle, hit_reaction and
  reloading from the "Basic Shooter Pack", on the exo_red rig. Stage one
  folder with every FBX from the Pro Rifle Pack's binary (fbx-bin) download,
  exo_red.fbx included, and "firing rifle.fbx", "hit reaction.fbx" and
  "reloading.fbx" from the Basic Shooter Pack. Built with:
    mixamo_to_gltf.py --input-dir <staged folder> --base exo_red.fbx       --library --strip-face
  See docs/character-animation.md.
* A rebuild with today's script differs from the committed bake only on
  mixamorig:Throat, which no clip keys and no vertex follows (checked
  2026-11-14).
* Measured moment for clip events (Clip-timed attacks, not yet in the
  library): firing_rifle kick at 0 ms, where the gun hand drops and the
  spine starts to pitch back with the recoil.
